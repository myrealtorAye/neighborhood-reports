What I do

As a listing agent. Pricing from comparable sales and not from an index, the holding-cost math that decides whether a reduction beats waiting, and the disclosure and inspection issues that surface in escrow if nobody raises them first.

As a buyer's agent. What a loan actually reaches at a given price, where the county loan ceiling cuts off the minimum-down programs, what the transfer tax costs in that specific city, and what the era of the housing stock means for an inspection.

In probate, trust and divorce sales. I am not the attorney and not the CPA, and I do not advise in their lanes. I provide the valuations and the net-proceeds analysis they need, flag the title, condition and vacancy issues early, and run the sale so the family or the parties are not coordinating it themselves.
