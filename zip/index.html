ZIP code housing market reports, East Bay and Central Valley

A report for every ZIP code in Alameda, Contra Costa and San Joaquin counties that has a published home value series. 115 of them. Each one is measured at ZIP level rather than borrowed from its city, and each is rebuilt every month when the new data publishes.

A ZIP code is a postal delivery area. It is not a city boundary, not a school district and not a neighborhood. Some of these ZIPs cross a city line, and where that happens the city transfer tax can differ by tens of thousands of dollars on the same sale price. Those pages say so.
