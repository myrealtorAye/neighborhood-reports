Pleasant Hill BART area, Pleasant Hill: the October 2026 read

The Pleasant Hill BART area is the transit district on the city's southern edge, and it is a different market from the tracts around it.

Pleasant Hill became a city to stop a neighbour. Concord was expanding west, and the voters here approved incorporation on November 7, 1961, with one city history giving November 14 and the sources differing by a week. That 1961 vote blocked Concord's westward expansion, and it is part of why Clayton on the far side kept pushing for its own city after a 1963 Concord annexation move. Diablo Valley College is here. The town had a post office from 1948, thirteen years before it had a city hall, and at least one account mistakes that post office year for the incorporation date.

Pleasant Hill has 13,939 housing units and 9.6 percent of them predate 1950. 60.0 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 62.6 percent, and the ACS median value is 1,077,100 dollars. ZHVI is about 973,000 dollars, up 1.3 percent over the year.

So the buying questions here are newer-construction questions.

Get the homeowners association budget and the reserve study, not the dues figure, and ask exactly what the association maintains and what it does not.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate, and ask which assessment districts the parcel sits in and the year each one expires, in writing. A newly built East County subdivision can carry a community facilities district on top of the base rate, and that line runs for decades and does not come off with the mortgage.

Then ask the inspector about grading and drainage, because a young subdivision on engineered fill shows its settlement in the first fifteen years.

Then the part specific to this address. Walk to the station from the address and time it. Then pull the zoning and ask the city and the county about the station area specific plan, because this district was planned deliberately and the plan is public. Note that the transit village itself sits in unincorporated county territory at Contra Costa Centre, so confirm which jurisdiction the parcel is in.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $973,036 sale in Pleasant Hill BART area carries about $1,070 in transfer tax. Custom is to split it, so about $535 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $11,676.

What waiting costs

On the same trailing rate, two years of waiting adds up to roughly $28,927 in price and payment combined, and five years to $88,993.
