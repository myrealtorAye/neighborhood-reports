Crockett, Crockett: the October 2026 read

61.4 percent of the housing in Crockett predates 1950. Almost nowhere else in the Bay Area reads like that.

Crockett is a sugar town. The C and H refinery on the Carquinez Strait is the reason the place exists and it still dominates the waterfront. American Sugar Refining bought C and H in 2006, and the Crockett plant processed its last shipment of Hawaiian sugar in 2017.

Crockett has 1,713 housing units and 61.4 percent of them were built before 1950, the second oldest housing stock in this county. 68.0 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 56.5 percent, and the ACS median value is 776,100 dollars. ZHVI is about 659,000 dollars, down 1.2 percent over the year. Confirm first that the address is unincorporated county territory, because it changes who issues your permits, who provides your services, who polices it and how your tax bill is built. Take it from the county, not from a listing. Then ask which school district serves the parcel.

So the first questions here are the old housing questions.

Pull the year built. On pre war stock, ask about foundation bolting, knob and tube wiring, galvanized supply lines, and the condition of the original windows and siding. Confirm the legal unit count with the city, not the listing, and get the permit history for the parcel.

Ask whether the building is nonconforming, because that limits what you can rebuild after a loss and it affects financing and insurance.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate.

Then the part specific to this address. In a town this old, three things. Pull the zoning for the parcel and everything touching it. This part of the county runs heavy industry alongside housing and a permitted use next door is not always what operates there now. Then ask for the site history in writing on the parcel itself. Then on the hill ground, ask the inspector about soils and drainage on the parcel and about where water leaves it in a wet month. Ask about the condition and the ownership of any retaining structure. Get a written fire insurance quote on the exact address with the risk rating explained. Crockett is built on a slope down to the strait and the grade is in every parcel.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

What it costs to own here

The all-in monthly number for the typical home here is about $6,509, $78,109 over twelve months. It counts the payment, the tax bill, insurance, utilities and a maintenance allowance, and leaves out HOA dues.

On 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed average was 7.4 percent, where the year-ago figure was 6.27 percent. The 2026 one-unit loan limit in Contra Costa County is $1,249,125, and that figure is both the conforming ceiling and the FHA ceiling.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $659,046 sale in Crockett carries about $725 in transfer tax. Local practice splits it down the middle, roughly $362 each, though the contract decides.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $7,909.

What waiting costs

Hold off two years and the combined price and payment cost is near -$18,812. At five years it reaches -$55,735.

This rests on one observed year carried forward. The two year figure is the defensible one; the five year figure is arithmetic, not a forecast.
