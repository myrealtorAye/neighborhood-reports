Broadmoor, San Leandro: the October 2026 read

Broadmoor is one of San Leandro's established residential neighborhoods on the older side of the city, and its story is the city's.

Rancho San Leandro was granted to Jose Joaquin Estudillo, born in Monterey in 1800. He served a year as alcalde of Yerba Buena, then brought his wife and ten children across the bay and settled on San Leandro Creek just outside the Peraltas' Rancho San Antonio, the first settlers in what became Eden Township. The grant ran from San Leandro Creek south to San Lorenzo Creek. Estudillo died in 1852. Casa Peralta, at 384 West Estudillo Avenue, was built in 1901 by Ludovino Peralta and has been on the National Register since January 4, 1982.

Orchards followed the rancho, cherries above all. That is where the Cherry City name comes from. Daniel Best Agricultural Works brought the first large sustained payroll and industry followed. After the Second World War the orchards became housing.

San Leandro has 31,954 housing units and 26.2 percent of them predate 1950. 57.3 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 58.1 percent, the ACS median value is 844,200 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,186. Broadmoor sits toward the older end of that range, with a mix of pre war and early postwar single family housing on a regular grid.

So pull the year built first, because the inspection list is different at either end of that range and the gap on one street can be thirty years.

Pre war: foundation bolting, knob and tube wiring, galvanized supply lines, and the permit history on every addition. Postwar: the roof, the supply lines, the electrical panel capacity, the sewer lateral and the furnace.

Then the fault. The Hayward Fault runs along the hills just east of this city, so ask whether the foundation has been bolted and ask the title company about Alquist-Priolo zone status.

Then check the lot and the setbacks if an accessory dwelling unit matters to you, and walk to the nearest BART access at the hour you would use it.

What it costs to own here

Figure about $8,160 a month to own the typical home in Broadmoor, $97,923 a year. Loan, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance are in that figure. HOA dues are not, and they vary too much to average.

The survey average for a 30-year fixed is 7.4 percent at 2026-10-15, compared with 6.27 percent a year back. The conforming and FHA ceiling for a one-unit property in Alameda County is $1,249,125 in 2026.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

Broadmoor charges 12.10 per thousand, so the typical $841,932 home owes roughly $10,187 at transfer. Convention puts about $5,094 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $12,629.

What waiting costs

Waiting two years at this place's own trailing rate costs about -$1,914, counting the price increase and the payment increase together. Over five years it is -$5,768.

That rate is one observed year extrapolated forward, not a forecast, and compounding a single strong year out to five produces a large number by construction. Weight the two year figure more heavily.
