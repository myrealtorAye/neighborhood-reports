Piedmont: the October 2026 read

Piedmont's boundaries were traced off a sewer map, in a hurry, to stop Oakland annexing it.

Piedmont exists because Oakland was about to swallow it. When it became clear that Oakland, wanting the tax base, was preparing to annex this hill, a charter was drawn up in a hurry and Piedmont incorporated on January 30, 1907. The boundaries were taken off a sewer line map, and that is why the city's edges weave in and out of Oakland the way they do. It became a charter city on December 18, 1922, and voters adopted the charter on February 27, 1923.

Piedmont has 4,073 housing units and 71.7 percent of them predate 1950, the highest pre war share of any city in this series. 94.7 percent are single family detached and owner occupancy runs 90.9 percent, both also the highest. The ACS median value tops out at the Census ceiling of 2,000,001 dollars and the city ZHVI is 2,549,073.

Piedmont is also completely surrounded. Every road out of it runs into Oakland, and the neighborhoods on the other side of that line are Montclair, Grand Lake, Piedmont Avenue and Oakmore, each with its own market and its own rules. Mountain View Cemetery, laid out by Frederick Law Olmsted in 1863, sits just beyond the northern boundary.

Nothing else in these three counties looks like that. Emeryville, a few minutes away, runs 5.8 percent detached and 24.2 percent owner occupied. Piedmont is almost entirely century old houses owned by the people living in them.

So this is a single product market and the work is concentrated in two places.

The inspection, because the stock is old and large. Get a specialist inspection on anything pre 1930. Ask about foundation bolting first, then knob and tube wiring, galvanized supply lines, the sewer lateral and the permit history on every addition. Ask whether any local historic designation applies before planning an exterior change. On the sloped parcels add soils and drainage history and the condition and ownership of any retaining structures.

And the boundary, because of how it was drawn. Piedmont's city line weaves in and out of Oakland block by block, and which side a parcel sits on decides the school district, the transfer tax and the services. Oakland charges 16.10 dollars per thousand in transfer tax at its typical value, and its brackets apply to the whole sale price instead of only the part above the line, so the difference between the two sides of that boundary is not a rounding error. Piedmont has 4,073 housing units in total, against Oakland's 191,713, and its city ZHVI of 2,549,073 dollars sits against Oakland's 710,647. Take the city from the assessor's parcel record, never from the mailing address, and run the transfer tax both ways before you model a net.

What the market is doing right now

Typical home value in Piedmont is $2,549,073 as of 2026-09-30, up 1.42 percent from the prior month and up 14.6 percent over the trailing year.

Realtor.com does not publish monthly listing metrics for Piedmont, so there is no inventory, days on market or asking price figure to report here. The value series above is measured separately and is unaffected.

Inside it

This report covers 3 areas of Piedmont. What each one actually is:

  • Central Piedmont. Central Piedmont is the civic middle of a city that exists because of a sewer map.
  • Lower Piedmont. Lower Piedmont is the edge where the city meets Oakland, and in places the boundary runs down the middle of a block.
  • Upper Piedmont. Upper Piedmont is the top of the hill, and it has the largest houses and the longest views in the city.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $2,549,073 here, transfer tax runs about $35,942 at 14.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $17,971 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Emeryville at 26.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $63,727.
