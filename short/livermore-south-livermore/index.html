South Livermore, Livermore: the October 2026 read

South Livermore is the wine side of the city, and the wine goes back to the 1880s.

Wente, Concannon and Wetmore were working out of this valley in that decade and between them helped shape the California wine industry. The Livermore Valley has been a wine region ever since, and the southern part of the city is where the vineyards and the tasting rooms are. The ground sits partly on Rancho Las Positas, secured by Robert Livermore and Jose Noriega in 1839. William Mendenhall platted the town on November 4, 1869 and named it after Livermore, and the city incorporated on April 1, 1876.

Livermore has 31,936 housing units and 4.5 percent of them predate 1950. 69.2 percent are single family detached and owner occupancy runs 72.0 percent, and on both measures Livermore is second only to Piedmont among Alameda County cities, the ACS median value is 1,105,600 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,677. South Livermore runs to larger parcels and newer housing than the downtown grid.

So the questions here are land questions first.

Ask what the parcel's zoning permits and whether an agricultural preserve contract applies, because that carries both tax treatment and use restrictions and it transfers with the property. Get the answer from the county.

Ask whether the property is on well water or district water. If it is a well, ask for the yield and the water quality testing. Ask about any septic system, when it was last inspected and when it was last pumped.

Then the house, matched to its year built: the roof, the supply lines, the electrical panel capacity, the sewer lateral where there is one, the furnace and the water heater.

Then the cooling. Livermore is inland and summer here is hot, so ask the age and condition of the air conditioning and what a season costs to run.

Then drive the commute west at your real hour and come back at the hour you would come back. The pass is the whole calculation on this side of the valley.

  • Dunsmuir. Dunsmuir is one of Livermore's residential pockets with little published history of its own.

What it costs to own here

Carry on the typical home here is roughly $10,160 monthly, or $121,923 annually. That covers the loan payment, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance at a stated budget. HOA dues are excluded, because they differ building to building.

As of 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed averages 7.4 percent, up or down from 6.27 percent twelve months before. In Alameda County the 2026 one-unit limit is $1,249,125. It serves as the conforming conventional ceiling and the FHA ceiling at once.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $1,071,869 sale in South Livermore carries about $1,179 in transfer tax. Local practice splits it down the middle, roughly $590 each, though the contract decides.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $16,078.

What waiting costs

On the same trailing rate, two years of waiting adds up to roughly -$56,651 in price and payment combined, and five years to -$165,189.

Treat it as a scenario, not a prediction. The appreciation input is one year of observed change carried forward, and five years of compounding on one year of data flatters the result.
