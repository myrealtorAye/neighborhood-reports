Mission-Garin, Hayward: the October 2026 read

Mission Boulevard runs along the foot of the hills here and Garin Regional Park is immediately above, and the neighborhood is named for both.

Garin is the open space side of the Hayward Fault. That fault runs along the base of these hills the length of the East Bay, it created the rise the park sits on, and it is among the most studied faults in California. Living against that parkland is this neighborhood's amenity and its main question at the same time.

The city below grew on fruit. Hayward incorporated on March 11, 1876 as Haywards, named after a hotel, became one of California's leading fruit districts, ran Hunt Brothers' cannery from 1895, and called itself the Apricot City by 1950 with about 14,000 residents. Mission Boulevard was the road all of that moved along.

Hayward has 52,243 housing units and only 9.1 percent of them predate 1950, so this is overwhelmingly a postwar city. 51.2 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 58.0 percent, the ACS median value is 854,400 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,391. On this eastern edge the stock is mixed in era, with sloped lots as the ground rises toward the park.

So the fault comes first. Ask the title company whether the parcel sits inside an Alquist-Priolo earthquake fault zone, request any seismic and soils report on the property, and ask whether the house has ever been bolted to its foundation.

Then the hillside list: drainage history for the parcel, the condition and ownership of any retaining structures, and where water leaves the lot in a wet month.

Then a written fire insurance quote on the exact address before your contingencies expire, with the risk rating explained, because a property backing onto regional parkland is priced differently by carriers.

Then the house, matched to its year built.

Then walk up into Garin from the end of your street. Regional parkland at the top of the road is the reason to be here and it is worth knowing how long that walk really takes.

Inside it

For context, the other parts of Hayward I cover, and what each one is:

  • Burbank. Burbank is one of Hayward's older central neighborhoods, close in to downtown, and the published history belongs to the city, not the pocket, so this is that history plus the development record.
  • Downtown Hayward. The city was incorporated on March 11, 1876 under the name Haywards, with the s, and it was named after a hotel.
  • Eden Shores. Eden Shores is the newest ground in Hayward and it sits out on the bay edge.
  • Fairview Hills. Fairview Hills is on the unincorporated slope above Hayward, and that word unincorporated is the practical headline.
  • Fairway Park. Fairway Park is postwar Hayward out toward the bay side, laid out around a golf course, and the name is literal.
  • Glen Eden. Eden is the old name for this whole area.
  • Harder Tennyson. Harder and Tennyson are the two roads this neighborhood is named for, in the southern part of Hayward, and the history here is the city's.
  • Hayward Highland. Hayward Highland sits on the slope above the city with the Hayward Fault running along the foot of it.

What it costs to own here

Carry on the typical home here is roughly $7,332 monthly, or $87,979 annually. That covers the loan payment, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance at a stated budget. HOA dues are excluded, because they differ building to building.

As of 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed averages 7.4 percent, up or down from 6.27 percent twelve months before. For 2026, one-unit financing in Alameda County tops out at $1,249,125 on both the conforming and the FHA side.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

Figure $7,168 in transfer tax on a $746,664 sale here, at 9.60 dollars per thousand. Convention puts about $3,584 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $11,200.

What waiting costs

Hold off two years and the combined price and payment cost is near -$88,953. At five years it reaches -$248,166.

One year of data, extended. That is worth saying plainly, because compounding it out to five years makes the total look more certain than it is.
