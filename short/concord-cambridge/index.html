Cambridge, Concord: the October 2026 read

Cambridge is a central Concord neighborhood, and Cambridge Park is the open space the city names inside it.

Concord started as a Mexican land grant. Rancho Monte del Diablo went to Salvio Pacheco in 1834. In 1868 the Pacheco family and Francisco Galindo laid out a town on it called Todos Santos, and the Contra Costa Gazette announced the new name Concord on April 17, 1869.

The city filled in after that on a tight schedule. Park and Shop and then Sun Valley Mall opened as regional shopping destinations through the 1950s and 1960s, and BART reached Concord in 1973. Joseph Eichler built three tracts here, Rancho Del Diablo, Rancho Los Santos and Parkwood Estates, roughly 175 homes in 1963 and 1964.

Concord has 47,703 housing units and 6.7 percent of them predate 1950. 58.7 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 61.6 percent, and the ACS median value is 797,600 dollars. The city's own figure is that 73 percent of its existing homes went up between 1950 and 1979.

So the buying questions in Concord are mostly postwar questions.

Pull the year built and the permit history for the parcel. On a 1950s or 1960s tract house, ask about the electrical panel and its capacity, the supply lines, the sewer lateral, the roof, and whether the foundation has ever been bolted.

Original aluminium windows and an original kitchen are either the discount or the asset here. The inspection report tells you which.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate, and ask which assessment districts the parcel sits in.

Then the part specific to this address. Cambridge Park is one of four open spaces the city lists in the Monument corridor planning area, alongside Meadow Homes Park, Ellis Lake Park and Todos Santos Plaza. Walk to it from the address. Then pull the zoning for the parcel and its neighbours, because this part of the city puts commercial and residential use close together.

One thing hangs over every address in this city. The former Concord Naval Weapons Station covers roughly 2,225 acres and the reuse plan calls for more than 12,000 homes, 6 million square feet of commercial space and more than 800 acres of parks, with Brookfield Properties as master developer. The city announced on September 24, 2026 that the Navy had approved the term sheet, and the governor signed SB 328, exempting the project from the Surplus Land Act and streamlining its environmental review. The site is a federal Superfund site with lead and arsenic in it and the Navy has to finish the cleanup before anything gets built. Nobody can tell you the delivery date.

And ask your agent to put the base reuse timeline in writing as a range, not a date. A buyer three miles from 12,000 future homes is making a bet on traffic, schools and supply, and nobody honest will give you a single year for it.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

What it costs to own here

The all-in monthly number for the typical home here is about $7,041, $84,498 over twelve months. It counts the payment, the tax bill, insurance, utilities and a maintenance allowance, and leaves out HOA dues.

On 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed average was 7.4 percent, where the year-ago figure was 6.27 percent. For 2026, one-unit financing in Contra Costa County tops out at $1,249,125 on both the conforming and the FHA side.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $720,907 sale in Cambridge carries about $793 in transfer tax. Convention puts about $396 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $8,651.

What waiting costs

Hold off two years and the combined price and payment cost is near $3,028. At five years it reaches $9,163.

Treat it as a scenario, not a prediction. The appreciation input is one year of observed change carried forward, and five years of compounding on one year of data flatters the result.
