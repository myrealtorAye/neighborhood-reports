Bay Point, Bay Point: the October 2026 read

Bay Point is unincorporated, it sits on Suisun Bay, and 55.6 percent of its housing is owner occupied.

Bay Point is unincorporated Contra Costa County territory on the Suisun Bay shoreline between Concord and Pittsburg. The national memorial at Port Chicago is on this stretch of water. On July 17, 1944 at 10:08 at night an explosion there killed 320 men, the worst stateside disaster of World War II. The trial that followed was the largest naval mutiny case in United States history and it led to the desegregation of the Navy. The Port Chicago Naval Magazine National Memorial was established in 1992 and became a unit of the national park system in 2009. It stands on an active military base and can only be visited by reservation.

Bay Point has 6,914 housing units and 7.9 percent of them predate 1950. 68.6 percent are single family detached and owner occupancy runs 55.6 percent. The ACS median value is 525,000 dollars, among the lowest of any place covered in this county. A scope note on price. Neither Zillow nor Realtor.com publishes a separate value series for this community, so there is no citywide figure to quote for it. Work from closed comparable sales on the specific streets and treat any citywide number a listing shows you as belonging to somewhere else.

So the buying questions are mostly postwar questions.

Pull the year built and the permit history for the parcel. On a tract house of this era, ask about the electrical panel and its capacity, the supply lines, the sewer lateral, the roof, and whether the foundation has ever been bolted.

An original kitchen and original aluminium windows are either the discount or the asset. The inspection report tells you which.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate.

Then the part specific to this address. Confirm first that the address is unincorporated county territory, because it changes who issues your permits, who provides your services, who polices it and how your tax bill is built. Take it from the county, not from a listing. Then ask which school district serves the parcel. Pull the zoning for the parcel and everything touching it. This part of the county runs heavy industry alongside housing and a permitted use next door is not always what operates there now. Then ask for the site history in writing on the parcel itself. And ask your insurance broker how the industrial and military proximity is rated for this exact address, and ask the county what notification and emergency systems cover it.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $559,194 here, transfer tax runs about $615 at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $308 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $6,710.
