Sunol: the October 2026 read

Sunol has 330 housing units. That is not a typo and it is the most important number on this page.

The place sits in the hills between Fremont and Pleasanton, unincorporated, in the valley the regional water system runs through. 97.3 percent of its housing is single family detached, among the highest shares anywhere in this series. 43.6 percent predates 1950. Owner occupancy is 82.1 percent and the ACS median value is 1,333,300 dollars.

One thing does run through Sunol, and it is a railway. The Niles Canyon Railway operates on the original alignment of the First Transcontinental Railroad, the eleven mile section between Sunol and the Niles district of Fremont. The National Register listed that stretch on October 13, 2010 as the Niles Canyon Transcontinental Railroad Historic District. The Pacific Locomotive Association leased the right of way and began rebuilding track in 1987, and the first passenger train ran out of Sunol on May 21, 1988. Trains still run it.

So the valley that holds 330 homes also holds the western end of the line that first connected California to the rest of the country, and the depot is a short walk from most of the housing.

A place this small does not behave like a market. In a normal month there may be no sales at all. That means comparable sales are frequently unavailable, and any index or automated valuation built on this little transaction volume should be treated with real caution instead of quoted as fact. Pricing here reasons from the land and the condition, not from a recent sale down the street, because there usually is not one.

So the work is land work.

Establish jurisdiction: Sunol is unincorporated, so Alameda County handles planning and permits, and you should ask exactly which services come with the address.

Ask about water and waste. Whether the parcel is on a well or district water, and if a well, the yield and the water quality testing. Whether there is a septic system, when it was last inspected and when it was last pumped.

Ask about access. Whether the road to the property is public or private, and if private, who maintains it and under what recorded agreement, because that obligation transfers with the land.

Then get a written fire insurance quote on the exact address early, with the risk rating explained. In a small valley ringed by open hills, that number can decide the purchase.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $1,417,745 here, transfer tax runs about $1,560 at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $780 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $21,266.
