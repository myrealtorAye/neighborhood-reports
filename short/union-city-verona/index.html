Verona, Union City: the October 2026 read

Verona is a Union City neighborhood built in the city's postwar expansion.

The Horner brothers made the first non-native settlement here in 1850. Henry C. Smith founded New Haven in 1851, and in 1853 New Haven combined with the nearby town of Alvarado and became the county seat of the newly created Alameda County. Alvarado carries the name of Governor Juan Alvarado, the same governor who granted Guillermo Castro the rancho that became Castro Valley. In 1870 the California Beet Sugar Company opened at Alvarado and ran until 1873, the first successful sugar beet factory in the United States. Ezra Decoto sold land to the railroad, in 1867 by one account and 1870 by another, and the settlement that grew there took his name.

Union City itself is recent. Alvarado and Decoto voted to incorporate together in January 1959, sources giving the vote as January 13 and the incorporation as January 26, and the petition was filed to preclude annexation by Hayward. Tom Kitayama became the first mayor.

Union City has 21,901 housing units and 4.0 percent of them predate 1950. 57.8 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 64.7 percent, the ACS median value is 1,104,900 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,713.

So this is predominantly a postwar single family market and the consistency is what to use. Where housing went up inside a narrow window, comparable sales genuinely compare and the entire price spread is condition and updates.

Pull the year built and the square footage, compare only within the same cohort, and put your effort into the inspection report.

The list for this era: the roof and its age, the supply lines, the electrical panel capacity if you intend to add load, the sewer lateral, the furnace and the water heater.

If the property sits inside a homeowners association, get the budget and the reserve study, not the dues figure.

Then check the lot dimensions and the setbacks early if an accessory dwelling unit is part of your plan, because single storey houses on regular lots are the best candidates and the setback usually decides it.

Then walk to the Union City BART station and time it honestly, and drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

Transfer tax on the typical Verona home, $1,195,668, comes to roughly $1,315 at a rate of 1.10 per thousand. Convention puts about $658 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $17,935.

What waiting costs

Waiting two years at this place's own trailing rate costs about -$52,718, counting the price increase and the payment increase together. Over five years it is -$154,610.

This rests on one observed year carried forward. The two year figure is the defensible one; the five year figure is arithmetic, not a forecast.
