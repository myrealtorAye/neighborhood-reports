Lodi: the October 2026 read

Lodi started as a railroad stop called Mokelumne Station, when the Central Pacific put a station here in 1869. The renaming is a genuine muddle. A city page says the Legislature changed the name in 1874, to stop the confusion with Mokelumne City and Mokelumne Hill. Britannica gives 1873 and suggests a racehorse of the day. A city records finding aid says the residents did it themselves in 1878. Another account credits Assembly Bill 639 and points at Lodi, Italy. Residents voted on incorporation in 1906 and the county Board of Supervisors declared Lodi incorporated on December 6 of that year.

The arch is the landmark. E. B. Brown built it in 1907 for the first Lodi Tokay Carnival, an event that exists today as the Lodi Grape Festival, and a city page calls it the oldest standing welcome arch in the United States. It was restored in 1956 after it had deteriorated into a hazard. The carnival was built around the Flame Tokay, a grape developed here, and the whole point of it in 1907 was to advertise that grape.

Lodi has 24,806 housing units and 15.9 percent of them predate 1950, the highest pre war share of any city in San Joaquin County. 64.9 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 56.8 percent, and the ACS median value is 492,500 dollars. ZHVI is about 509,000 dollars, down 1.4 percent over the year.

That 15.9 percent pre war share is the highest in the county, so in Lodi the inspection list is a pre war list more often than anywhere else in San Joaquin.

So pull the year built first. Foundation bolting, knob and tube wiring, galvanized supply lines, the original windows and siding, the permit history. Downtown, add the zoning for the parcel and its neighbours and ask the city about any specific plan or historic area covering the address.

One research warning specific to this town. The visitor bureau, the chamber and the local paper will all confirm an address, and their published hours routinely disagree with each other. Use them for days and confirm hours with the business.

And get the tax bill for the exact address off the county roll, not the listing estimate.
