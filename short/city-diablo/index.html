Diablo: the October 2026 read

Diablo has 476 housing units and 100.0 percent of them are owner occupied.

Diablo is unincorporated Contra Costa County territory at the foot of Mount Diablo, built around a country club. The towns around it became towns and it did not. Danville incorporated after its voters approved it on June 8, 1982, and San Ramon followed in 1983, after valley wide attempts failed at the ballot in 1964, 1967, 1973 and 1976.

Of those 476 units, 8.4 percent predate 1950 and 92.0 percent are single family detached. ZHVI is about 3,103,000 dollars, up 3.8 percent over the year, the highest level of any place in Contra Costa County. One number to read carefully. The ACS reports a median owner occupied value of 2,000,001 dollars here, and that is the survey's ceiling, not a measurement. The actual median sits above the highest figure the survey will print.

In a place this small the comparable sales problem is real. 476 units will not produce a useful monthly sample, so ask for closed sales going back far enough to mean something and expect to reason from a handful.

So the questions are jurisdiction, rural service and the club. Confirm first that the address is unincorporated county territory, because it changes who issues your permits, who provides your services, who polices it and how your tax bill is built. Take it from the county, not from a listing. Then ask which school district serves the parcel, since district lines out here do not follow the boundaries a buyer assumes. Ask whether the parcel is on sewer or on septic, and if septic, get the most recent inspection and the pumping record. Ask whether water comes from a district or a well, and if a well, get the yield and the water quality tests in writing. And ask in writing whether country club membership transfers with the property, what the initiation and dues are, and whether there is a waiting list.

Then ask the inspector about soils, drainage and retaining structures, and get a written fire insurance quote on the exact address.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $3,102,551 here, transfer tax runs about $3,413 at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $1,706 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against Richmond at 26.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $77,564.
