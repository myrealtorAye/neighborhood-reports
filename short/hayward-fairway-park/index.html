Fairway Park, Hayward: the October 2026 read

Fairway Park is postwar Hayward out toward the bay side, laid out around a golf course, and the name is literal.

The city it belongs to was built on fruit. Hayward incorporated on March 11, 1876 as Haywards, taken from a hotel name, and spent seventy years as one of California's leading fruit growing districts. Hunt Brothers opened a cannery in 1895 and it anchored the seasonal economy. By 1950 the city had about 14,000 residents and the nickname Apricot City. Then the war ended, the orchards came out, and tracts like this one went in.

Hayward has 52,243 housing units and only 9.1 percent of them predate 1950, so this is overwhelmingly a postwar city. 51.2 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 58.0 percent, the ACS median value is 854,400 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,391. Fairway Park is squarely in that postwar cohort: single family houses on regular lots, consistent in era and type.

That consistency is what to use. Where the housing went up inside a narrow window, comparable sales actually compare and the whole price spread is condition and updates.

So pull the year built and compare only within it, then spend your effort on the inspection report, because in a uniform tract that document is your negotiating position.

The postwar list: the roof and its age, the supply lines, the electrical panel capacity if you plan to add load, the sewer lateral, the furnace and the water heater.

Then the fault. It applies across this city: ask whether the house has ever been bolted to its foundation and ask the title company about Alquist-Priolo zone status.

Then the water. This is the flatter bay side of Hayward, so get the flood zone determination with a written insurance quote before your contingencies expire.

Then check the lot and setbacks if an accessory dwelling unit is in your plan, and drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it.

Inside it

For context, the other parts of Hayward I cover, and what each one is:

  • Burbank. Burbank is one of Hayward's older central neighborhoods, close in to downtown, and the published history belongs to the city, not the pocket, so this is that history plus the development record.
  • Downtown Hayward. The city was incorporated on March 11, 1876 under the name Haywards, with the s, and it was named after a hotel.
  • Eden Shores. Eden Shores is the newest ground in Hayward and it sits out on the bay edge.
  • Fairview Hills. Fairview Hills is on the unincorporated slope above Hayward, and that word unincorporated is the practical headline.
  • Glen Eden. Eden is the old name for this whole area.
  • Harder Tennyson. Harder and Tennyson are the two roads this neighborhood is named for, in the southern part of Hayward, and the history here is the city's.
  • Hayward Highland. Hayward Highland sits on the slope above the city with the Hayward Fault running along the foot of it.
  • Hayward Hills. The Hayward Fault runs along the base of these hills, and it is the reason there are hills here at all.

What it costs to own here

Owning that typical home runs about $8,816 a month, $105,792 a year, once the loan, the property tax, insurance, utilities and a maintenance budget are all counted. HOA dues are not in it, because they vary by building.

The survey average for a 30-year fixed is 7.4 percent at 2026-10-15, compared with 6.27 percent a year back. The 2026 one-unit loan limit in Alameda County is $1,249,125, and that figure is both the conforming ceiling and the FHA ceiling.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $917,321 here, transfer tax runs about $8,806 at 9.60 dollars per thousand. It is normally split down the middle, about $4,403 on each party. The contract governs, not the custom.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $13,760.

What waiting costs

On the same trailing rate, two years of waiting adds up to roughly -$13,315 in price and payment combined, and five years to -$39,813.

The input is a single trailing year, projected. It is not a forecast, and the five year number is the one to distrust first.
