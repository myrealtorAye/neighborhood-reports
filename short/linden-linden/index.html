Linden, Linden: the October 2026 read

Linden is a small place, and the numbers below are the whole market.

Linden is unincorporated San Joaquin County territory east of Stockton, in the cherry and walnut country. Stockton down the road was founded in 1849 by Captain Charles Maria Weber, who worked out that supplying the gold miners paid better than joining them, and the farm towns east of it grew to feed that trade.

Linden has 989 housing units and 6.5 percent of them predate 1950. 100.0 percent of them are single family detached, and that is the real figure, not a rounding. Owner occupancy runs 56.1 percent and the ACS median value is 600,100 dollars. ZHVI is about 677,000 dollars, up 3.7 percent over the year.

So the buying questions are mostly postwar questions.

Pull the year built and the permit history for the parcel. On a tract house of this era, ask about the electrical panel and its capacity, the supply lines, the sewer lateral, the roof, and whether the foundation has ever been bolted.

An original kitchen and original aluminium windows are either the discount or the asset. The inspection report tells you which.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate.

Then the part specific to this address. Confirm first that the address is unincorporated San Joaquin County territory, because it changes who issues your permits, who provides your services, who polices it and how your tax bill is built. Take it from the county, not from a listing. Then ask which school district serves the parcel. Ask whether the parcel is on sewer or on septic, and if septic, get the most recent inspection and the pumping record. Ask whether water comes from a district or a well, and if a well, get the yield and the water quality tests in writing. On valley agricultural ground, also ask what is farmed on the adjoining parcels and when it is sprayed.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

The 30-year fixed average is 7.4 percent as of 2026-10-15, against 6.27 percent in the same week last year. For 2026, one-unit financing in San Joaquin County tops out at $832,750 on both the conforming and the FHA side.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $676,713 here, transfer tax runs about $744 at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Local practice splits it down the middle, roughly $372 each, though the contract decides.

What waiting costs

Price and payment together, a two year wait here runs about $58,563. Stretch it to five and the figure is $186,764.

The input is a single trailing year, projected. It is not a forecast, and the five year number is the one to distrust first.
