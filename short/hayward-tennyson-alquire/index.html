Tennyson-Alquire, Hayward: the October 2026 read

Tennyson and Alquire are the two roads at the centre of this south Hayward neighborhood, and the published history belongs to the city, not the pocket.

Hayward incorporated on March 11, 1876 under the name Haywards, taken from a hotel. The economy was fruit. From the second half of the nineteenth century this was one of California's leading growing districts, shipping cherries, apricots, peaches, tomatoes and potatoes by rail, and Hunt Brothers ran a cannery here from 1895 that anchored each season. By 1950 Hayward had about 14,000 residents and the Apricot City nickname. After the war the orchards came out for tracts, and south Hayward is that build out.

Hayward has 52,243 housing units and only 9.1 percent of them predate 1950, so this is overwhelmingly a postwar city. 51.2 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 58.0 percent, the ACS median value is 854,400 dollars and the median gross rent is 2,391. Here the stock is postwar, with single family tracts and multi unit buildings along the main roads, and South Hayward BART nearby.

So pull the year built first and confirm the legal unit count with the city, not the listing. If there is more than one unit, get the permit history and ask whether the building is nonconforming.

Then the postwar list: the roof and its age, the supply lines, the electrical panel capacity if you plan to add load, the sewer lateral, the furnace and the water heater.

Then the fault that runs through this city. Ask whether the foundation has ever been bolted and ask the title company whether the parcel sits inside an Alquist-Priolo earthquake fault zone. That is a recorded designation and it carries disclosure consequences.

Then the lot. Check the dimensions and the setbacks if an accessory dwelling unit is any part of your plan, because single storey houses on regular lots are Hayward's best candidates and the setback is usually the binding constraint.

Then drive the commute at your real hour, and walk to the BART platform once.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 9.60 dollars per thousand, a $813,806 sale in Tennyson-Alquire carries about $7,813 in transfer tax. Custom is to split it, so about $3,906 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $12,207.

What waiting costs

Price and payment together, a two year wait here runs about -$51,725. Stretch it to five and the figure is -$149,765.

That rate is one observed year extrapolated forward, not a forecast, and compounding a single strong year out to five produces a large number by construction. Weight the two year figure more heavily.
