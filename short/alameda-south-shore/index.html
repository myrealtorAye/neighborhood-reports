South Shore, Alameda: the October 2026 read

This neighborhood did not exist before November 1955. It was the floor of San Francisco Bay.

Alameda voters approved a landfill ordinance in the 1950s to expand the city's tax base. The plan called for 400 acres of made land along the south shore, with another 55 acres added onto Washington Park. Utah Construction got the job and hired Yuba Construction of Benicia to build the dredge. The dredge, the Franciscan, reached Alameda's south shore on November 7, 1955, and by the end of the following year the land that is now South Shore existed. In one month alone it pumped roughly a million cubic yards of sand through two miles of six foot pipe, a figure the historian Imelda Merlin recorded in her geographical history of the city.

So every house in this neighborhood sits on engineered fill placed in the mid 1950s, and the housing stock dates from then forward. That is the opposite of the rest of Alameda, where the question is how old the Victorian is.

It also puts you on the water, with Crown Memorial State Beach along the shoreline. Formally that beach was once called Alameda Beach and it carried the same Coney Island of the West nickname that belonged to the amusement park a little further west.

Here is what matters when you buy on fill. Ask for the geotechnical history on the parcel and ask the inspector directly about settlement, because differential settling is the characteristic problem of mid century bay fill and it shows up in foundations, in flatwork and in door frames that no longer close square. Look at the floors with a level, not just with your eyes. Get the flood zone determination and a real insurance quote in writing before your contingencies are gone. And if the house has been re-leveled at some point, get the documentation on who did it and what they found. On this ground that paperwork is worth more than any cosmetic upgrade in the listing.

Inside it

For context, the other parts of Alameda I cover, and what each one is:

  • Alameda Point. This is 1,560 acres of the island that belonged to the United States Navy until 1997, and the Secretary of the Interior made the heart of it a National Historic Landmark District in January 2013.
  • Ballena Bay. Ballena Bay is an island off the side of an island, and a good part of it runs on a lease from the city.
  • Bay Farm. A man named Amos Mecartney tried to farm this ground in the 1870s and it did not work out.
  • Bronze Coast. The Bronze Coast sits between the Gold Coast and the rest of the island, and the name tells you exactly what it is: the same era, a step down in scale.
  • Central Alameda. Central Alameda is the part of the island everybody drives through and nobody can draw the boundary of.
  • Fernside. There was a seventy room mansion on Fernside Boulevard and it burned to the ground in 1897 with nobody in it.
  • Gold Coast. The name is not marketing.
  • Harbor Bay Isle. Harbor Bay Isle was supposed to be ten thousand homes.

What it costs to own here

Carry on the typical home here is roughly $9,519 monthly, or $114,232 annually. That covers the loan payment, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance at a stated budget. HOA dues are excluded, because they differ building to building.

On 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed average was 7.4 percent, where the year-ago figure was 6.27 percent. Alameda County's 2026 one-unit loan limit sits at $1,249,125. That is the cap for conforming conventional and for FHA alike.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

Figure $13,076 in transfer tax on a $998,185 sale here, at 13.10 dollars per thousand. Convention puts about $6,538 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Dublin charges 1.10 per thousand against Oakland at 16.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $14,973.

What waiting costs

Hold off two years and the combined price and payment cost is near $80,106. At five years it reaches $254,463.

The input is a single trailing year, projected. It is not a forecast, and the five year number is the one to distrust first.
