Brentwood: the October 2026 read

Brentwood is named after a town in Essex and was farmed by a British investment company.

Brentwood is named after an English town and was farmed by a British company. The name comes from John Marsh's ancestral home, Brentwood in the county of Essex. Balfour, Guthrie and Co., a British investment house, bought the Marsh ranch in 1910 and invested heavily in California agricultural land. Local business owners formed the Brentwood Improvement Association in 1947 to petition for a city, the election was held on January 15, 1948 and the city dates from January 21, 1948. The area is still known for cherries, corn and peaches.

Brentwood has 21,756 housing units and 2.5 percent of them predate 1950. 90.0 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 82.2 percent, and the ACS median value is 836,600 dollars. ZHVI is about 782,000 dollars, down 0.9 percent over the year.

So almost everything here is a detached house built since 1950 and a large share of it since 1990, owner occupied, frequently inside an association and frequently inside an assessment district.

That last point is the one that costs money. Ask which community facilities districts the parcel sits in, what each levies and the year each is scheduled to expire, in writing from the county. In East County that line runs for decades and does not come off with the mortgage.

Then get the association budget and the reserve study, not the dues figure, and ask the inspector about grading and drainage, because a young subdivision on engineered fill shows its settlement early. In the golf course communities and in Summerset, get a written answer on membership and on any age restriction before anything else.

And drive the Highway 4 corridor at your real commute hour.

And one habit worth keeping in this county. Pull the year built before you form any opinion on a listing, because it decides the entire inspection list and it is the single most useful number on a property profile. Then get the tax bill for the exact address off the county roll, not the listing estimate, and read which districts and assessments are on it. Those two documents cost nothing, they take a day, and between them they settle most of what a buyer actually needs to know before writing an offer.

What it costs to own here

Owning that typical home runs about $7,568 a month, $90,814 a year, once the loan, the property tax, insurance, utilities and a maintenance budget are all counted. HOA dues are not in it, because they vary by building.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $782,072 sale in Brentwood carries about $860 in transfer tax. Convention puts about $430 on each side of the table. That is custom, and it is negotiable.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $9,385.

What waiting costs

The two year cost of waiting, price plus payment, comes to about -$16,333 here. The five year figure is -$48,634.

One year of data, extended. That is worth saying plainly, because compounding it out to five years makes the total look more certain than it is.
