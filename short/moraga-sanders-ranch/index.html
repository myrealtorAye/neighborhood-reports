Sanders Ranch, Moraga: the October 2026 read

Sanders Ranch is a subdivision on the eastern edge of Moraga, out where the town meets open ground.

Moraga carries a Californio family name. Joaquin Moraga built the Moraga Adobe in 1841, and it is the oldest of the five surviving adobe houses in Contra Costa County. Saint Mary's College moved here in 1928 and the campus has shaped the town ever since. The town is young as a jurisdiction. An incorporation election carried in November 1974, with November 13 given as the date, and the town was assembled out of three places that had grown separately: Moraga Town, Rheem and Rheem Valley. Rheem carries the name of its developer, Donald Laird Rheem, a co-founder of the Rheem Manufacturing Company. The first inhabitants of this valley were the Saklan people, of the Bay Miwok group, though one account names the Ohlone instead. One correction worth carrying: the Moraga Adobe that Joaquin Moraga built in 1841 stands on Adobe Lane in Orinda, not in this town.

Moraga has 5,719 housing units and only 1.6 percent of them predate 1950. 66.8 percent are single family detached, a lower detached share than its Lamorinda neighbours, and owner occupancy runs 83.1 percent. The ACS median value is 1,663,200 dollars and ZHVI is about 1,607,000, up 3.3 percent over the year.

So the buying questions out here are hill questions and they come before the house.

Ask the inspector about soils and drainage on the parcel and about where water leaves it in a wet month. Ask about the condition and the ownership of every retaining structure, because a shared retaining wall on a slope is the most expensive thing that can go wrong.

Get a written fire insurance quote on the exact address with the risk rating explained, and ask what the insurer wants cleared to write it. In this part of the county that quote decides deals.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate.

Then the part specific to this address. Pull the tract map for the subdivision from the county recorder. The recording date, the original lot dimensions and the approved street layout are all on it, and that record beats anything a listing will tell you about when and how this neighborhood was built. Then confirm what sits between the lot line and the open space, and get the fire insurance quote before your contingencies expire.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

On a home at $1,606,792 here, transfer tax runs about $1,767 at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $884 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against Richmond at 13.50. Same county, same price, and the spread is $19,924.
