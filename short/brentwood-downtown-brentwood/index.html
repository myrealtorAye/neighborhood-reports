Downtown Brentwood, Brentwood: the October 2026 read

Downtown Brentwood is the old farm town centre, and 2.5 percent of the city's housing predates 1950. Most of that is here.

Brentwood is named after an English town and was farmed by a British company. The name comes from John Marsh's ancestral home, Brentwood in the county of Essex. Balfour, Guthrie and Co., a British investment house, bought the Marsh ranch in 1910 and invested heavily in California agricultural land. Local business owners formed the Brentwood Improvement Association in 1947 to petition for a city, the election was held on January 15, 1948 and the city dates from January 21, 1948. The area is still known for cherries, corn and peaches.

Brentwood has 21,756 housing units and 2.5 percent of them predate 1950. 90.0 percent are single family detached, owner occupancy runs 82.2 percent, and the ACS median value is 836,600 dollars. ZHVI is about 782,000 dollars, down 0.9 percent over the year.

So the first questions here are the old housing questions.

Pull the year built. On pre war stock, ask about foundation bolting, knob and tube wiring, galvanized supply lines, and the condition of the original windows and siding. Confirm the legal unit count with the city, not the listing, and get the permit history for the parcel.

Ask whether the building is nonconforming, because that limits what you can rebuild after a loss and it affects financing and insurance.

Get the tax bill for the address, not the listing estimate.

Then the part specific to this address. Pull the zoning for the parcel and its neighbours, and ask the city whether the address sits inside a downtown specific plan area.

And pull the tax bill for the exact address off the county roll before you write anything, not the listing estimate. Read which districts and assessments appear on it and what each one charges. That document is free, it takes a day to get, and it is the difference between the payment a buyer expects and the payment they get.

Then drive your commute at the hour you would really drive it, and walk the block after dinner.

What it costs to own here

Carry on the typical home here is roughly $7,568 monthly, or $90,814 annually. That covers the loan payment, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance at a stated budget. HOA dues are excluded, because they differ building to building.

On 2026-10-15 the 30-year fixed average was 7.4 percent, where the year-ago figure was 6.27 percent. In Contra Costa County the 2026 one-unit limit is $1,249,125. It serves as the conforming conventional ceiling and the FHA ceiling at once.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

At 1.10 dollars per thousand, a $782,072 sale in Downtown Brentwood carries about $860 in transfer tax. Custom is to split it, so about $430 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against El Cerrito at 13.10. Same county, same price, and the spread is $9,385.

What waiting costs

Waiting two years at this place's own trailing rate costs about -$16,333, counting the price increase and the payment increase together. Over five years it is -$48,634.

That rate is one observed year extrapolated forward, not a forecast, and compounding a single strong year out to five produces a large number by construction. Weight the two year figure more heavily.
