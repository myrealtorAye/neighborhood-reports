Danville: the October 2026 read

Danville lost four incorporation votes before it won one.

Danville took four defeats to become a town. Valley wide incorporation went to a vote in 1964, 1967, 1973 and 1976 and failed every time. A home rule campaign started in 1980 and the voters approved it on June 8, 1982.

Eugene O'Neill won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1936 and spent the money building a house above Danville. Tao House is a Monterey Colonial by the architect Frederick Confer, and O'Neill and his wife lived in it from 1937 to 1944. In 1965 three local women, among them Darlene Blair and Lois Sizoo, formed an association to save it, and the place became a National Historic Landmark in 1971, a National Historic Site in 1976, and a National Park Service property in 1980. Getting in is unusual: the site is reached through a private gated road owned by neighbouring residents and visitors cannot drive their own cars to it.

Danville has 16,004 housing units and 1.8 percent of them predate 1950. 77.4 percent are single family detached and owner occupancy runs 85.5 percent. The ACS median value is 1,681,700 dollars and ZHVI is about 1,821,000, up 0.2 percent over the year.

Only 1.8 percent of this housing stock predates 1950, so almost everything here is postwar or newer, on a detached lot, owner occupied. 85.5 percent owner occupancy is among the highest of any town in the county.

So the questions are postwar and newer questions. Pull the year built and the permit history. On anything recent, the association reserve study and the assessment districts, with the expiry year in writing. On the hill and eastern addresses toward Tassajara and Mount Diablo, soils, drainage, retaining walls, septic and well where they apply, and a written fire insurance quote on the exact address.

Out toward Tassajara, confirm whether the parcel is inside the town or in unincorporated county territory.

Inside it

This report covers 9 areas of Danville. What each one actually is:

  • Crow Canyon. Crow Canyon is the corridor on the southern side of Danville, along the road toward San Ramon.
  • Diablo Road Area. The Diablo Road area runs east out of Danville toward Mount Diablo, and Tao House sits above it.
  • Greenbrook. Greenbrook is a planned residential neighborhood in Danville with its own swim and tennis association.
  • Montair. Montair is a Danville neighborhood that shares its name with the elementary school.
  • Old Town Danville. Old Town Danville is the original village centre, and the town around it is younger than the houses.
  • Shadow Creek. Shadow Creek is a newer subdivision on the southern edge of Danville.
  • Sycamore. Sycamore is a residential neighborhood on the southern side of Danville.
  • Tassajara. Tassajara is the eastern edge of Danville, out where the town meets ranch ground, and much of the area beyond the line is unincorporated.
  • Westside Danville. Westside Danville is the older residential side of town, west of the main street.

What it costs to own here

Figure about $16,513 a month to own the typical home in Danville, $198,157 a year. Loan, property tax, insurance, utilities and maintenance are in that figure. HOA dues are not, and they vary too much to average.

The 30-year fixed average is 7.4 percent as of 2026-10-15, against 6.27 percent in the same week last year. Contra Costa County's 2026 one-unit loan limit sits at $1,249,125. That is the cap for conforming conventional and for FHA alike.

The transfer tax line that costs real money

Figure $2,004 in transfer tax on a $1,821,427 sale here, at 1.10 dollars per thousand. Custom is to split it, so about $1,002 a side, and who pays is set by the contract.

And it is set by the city, not the county. Antioch charges 1.10 per thousand against Richmond at 13.50. Same county, same price, and the spread is $22,586.

What waiting costs

Price and payment together, a two year wait here runs about $10,047. Stretch it to five and the figure is $30,434.

One year of data, extended. That is worth saying plainly, because compounding it out to five years makes the total look more certain than it is.
