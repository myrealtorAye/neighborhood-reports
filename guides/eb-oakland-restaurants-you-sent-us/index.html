Oakland Restaurants You Sent Us

Every Oakland food post we run, the comments pile on, so here is the East Bay city eating on its own terms. Eight Oakland spots you sent.

Mägo

3762 Piedmont Ave, Oakland. A Piedmont Avenue tasting menu room readers call fantastic. Book the counter. A @flygaivotafly add.

Mua

2442 Webster St, Oakland. A cavernous Uptown restaurant and bar with a happy Oakland vibe. Come for dinner, stay for the room. A @junkydawgs add.

Minto's Jamaican Restaurant and Bar

412 15th St, Oakland. Jerk and island plates downtown that readers said we forgot. Order the jerk chicken. A @momurdoing2much add.

Thanh Ky

659 E 12th St, Oakland. A Chaozhou and Cantonese kitchen a reader could not believe we skipped. Get the wonton noodle soup. A @papereetiger add.

Vegan Donut Gelato

411 E 18th St, Oakland. Six kinds of vegan banh mi and house gelato under one roof. Try a vegan banh mi. A @ginabobina77 add.

Boba Binge

2212 Broadway, Oakland. Banh mi around nine bucks plus boba to walk with. Grab a sandwich and a tea. A @ginabobina77 add.

Modigliani Cafe

3208 Grand Ave, Oakland. A Grand Lake sandwich shop where the meats are cured in house. Order the best sandwich in town. A @tremenjicalal add.

Peerless Coffee and Tea

260 Oak St, Oakland. A downtown roaster on Oak Street with more beans than you can name. Buy a bag fresh roasted. A @suzanjf3 add.

Eight in Oakland: Mägo, Mua, Minto's, Thanh Ky, Vegan Donut Gelato, Boba Binge, Modigliani, and Peerless Coffee. Where do you start?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
