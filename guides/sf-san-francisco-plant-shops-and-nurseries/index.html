San Francisco Plant Shops and Nurseries

Eight plant shops are the reason a foggy San Francisco apartment can still feel like a greenhouse, and the oldest of them works out of a converted 1904 dairy building on Clement Street. Most people grab a big box fiddle leaf that is brown by October and never meet the SF shops that keep one alive.

Flora Grubb Gardens — 1634 Jerrold Ave, San Francisco. A Bayview plant paradise with a coffee bar tucked among the succulents and a living wall that stops everyone at the door. Go on a Saturday and budget an hour longer than you planned. @floragrubbgardens

Clement Nursery — 1921 Clement St, San Francisco. San Francisco's oldest garden store still runs out of the old 1904 dairy buildings, and the staff will drill drainage holes and help you wrangle a six foot ficus into your Honda. Bring the awkward pot you have been avoiding. @clementnursery

Paxton Gate — 824 Valencia St, San Francisco. Part nursery, part cabinet of curiosities, this Mission shop sells carnivorous plants next to ethically sourced skulls and taxidermy. Come for a pitcher plant, leave wondering how a whole afternoon vanished. @paxtongate

Sloat Garden Center — 2700 Sloat Blvd, San Francisco. The Sunset flagship has anchored this corner near the zoo for more than fifty years, with rows of California natives that actually shrug off the coastal wind. Ask which ones survive an Outer Sunset balcony. @sloatgardens

The Plant Lady SF — 1637 Ocean Ave, San Francisco. An Ingleside jewel box packed wall to wall with tropicals and hard to find rarities you will not see at a chain. Go midweek when it is quiet and you can actually talk leaves with the owner. @theplantladysf

Flowercraft Garden Center — 550 Bayshore Blvd, San Francisco. Open since 1974, this sprawling yard on the Bernal edge is where you go for real dirt, edibles, and seedlings by the flat, not just a cute houseplant. Bring a car and a plan for spring tomatoes. @flowercraftgc

Savvy Nursery — 478 Union St, San Francisco. A tiny North Beach shop with a plant doctor on call for whatever you are slowly killing at home. Haul in the sad monstera and they will repot it and tell you the truth. @savvynursery

Houseplants and Plant Essentials — 283 9th St, San Francisco. A SoMa spot that stays open late enough to catch after work, stocked with statement plants and the soil, pots, and know how to match. Swing by after dinner and leave with a monstera and a pep talk. @houseplants_andplantessentials

Eight for a greener San Francisco: Flora Grubb, Clement Nursery, Paxton Gate, Sloat Garden Center, The Plant Lady SF, Flowercraft, Savvy Nursery, and Houseplants and Plant Essentials. Which windowsill are you starting with?
