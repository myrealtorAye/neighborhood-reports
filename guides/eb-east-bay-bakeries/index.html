East Bay Bakeries

The East Bay quietly out bakes San Francisco, home to the bagels a New York Times critic said rival the best in New York and a worker owned bakery with a cult following. Eight East Bay bakeries.

Boichik Bagels — 3170 College Ave, Berkeley. The Rockridge bagelry a New York Times critic said rivals the best New York bagels, boiled and blistered daily. Get there before they sell out.

Starter Bakery — 5804 College Ave, Oakland. The kouign amann specialist that went from wholesale to a beloved cafe, buttery caramelized pastry the star. Order the kouign amann.

La Farine — 6323 College Ave, Oakland. A French neighborhood bakery since 1979 for morning buns and baguettes, an Oakland institution. Get a morning bun.

Arizmendi Bakery — 3265 Lakeshore Ave, Oakland. A worker owned co-op famous for its daily changing gourmet pizza and sourdough by Lake Merritt. Come for the pizza of the day.

Sweet Bar Bakery — 2355 Broadway, Oakland. An Uptown bakery cafe for layer cakes, sticky buns, and a full breakfast. The sit down pastry stop. @sweetbarbakery

Fournée Bakery — 2912 Domingo Ave, Berkeley. A tiny bakery by the Claremont turning out some of the East Bay's best croissants and kouign amann. Worth the line.

Firebrand Artisan Breads — Alameda. The wood fired bread and pastry bakery, now baking in Alameda, croissants and challah among the best around. Grab a loaf. @firebrandbread

Sweet Adeline Bakeshop — 3350 Adeline St, Berkeley. A charming South Berkeley bakeshop for cupcakes, pies, and ice cream sandwiches. The neighborhood sweet.

Eight for the pastry: Boichik, Starter, La Farine, Arizmendi, Sweet Bar, Fournée, Firebrand, and Sweet Adeline. Where do you start?
