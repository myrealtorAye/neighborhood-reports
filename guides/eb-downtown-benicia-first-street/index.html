East Bay Downtown Benicia, First Street

Eight First Street stops, and the whole strip runs downhill to the Carquinez Strait. Most Bay Area day-trippers blow past the 780 exit chasing Napa and skip the North Bay town with the walkable waterfront downtown. Benicia, from the top of the hill to the marina.

One House Bakery — 918 First Street, Benicia. The spot that put this town on the pastry map, a family operation turning out sourdough and those showpiece bread sculptures people drive in for. Get there early on a weekend or the good stuff is gone by ten. @onehousebakery

The Rellik Tavern — 726 First Street, Benicia. Craft cocktails, local micro-brews and a deep wine list in a room that has anchored this block since 2009. Live music most nights, and the name is Killer spelled backward if you look twice. @relliktavern

First Street Taphouse — 440 First Street, Benicia. Mare Island Brewing's downtown outpost, pouring their own beer with cocktails on tap and trivia every Tuesday. The old ferry-town brewery finally planted a flag on this side of the strait. @mareislandbrewing_firststreet

Lucca Bar and Grill — 439 First Street, Benicia. Gastropub burgers, a lively bar and a back beer garden that earns its keep once the fog burns off. Named Benicia's business of the year in 2025, so the locals clearly agree. @luccabarandgrill

Drift Benicia — 366 First Street, Benicia. A European-style cafe pulling Oakland's Mr. Espresso in the morning and slinging Roman-style pizza by the slice with beer and wine after eleven. Coffee, then a slab of focaccia, then the nap you earned. @driftbenicia

Baxters Restaurant and Bar — 280 First Street, Benicia. Beef Wellington and scallops in a proper white-tablecloth room, with a hidden piano speakeasy tucked in the back. This is the one you book when the occasion needs a little theater. @baxters.restaurant

Bella Siena — 127 First Street, Benicia. Modern Italian-American with windows so big every table turns into a lookout over the water. Locals call it the nicest seat downtown, and the eggplant parm backs the claim up. @bellasienarestaurant

Sailor Jack's — 123 First Street, Benicia. The marina-end anchor, seafood with a California turn inside an 1868 sea captain's house right over the Carquinez Strait. Oyster Mondays and two floors of water views, the last stop before your feet hit the dock. @sailorjacks_benicia

Eight for a full First Street crawl: pastry at One House, drinks at Rellik and the Taphouse, dinner at Baxters or Bella Siena, and Sailor Jack's where the street meets the strait. Which end of Benicia do you start from, top of the hill or the water?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
