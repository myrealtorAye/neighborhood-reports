East Bay Dog Parks

Point Isabel is the largest off-leash dog park on the West Coast, 23 acres of Richmond shoreline with the San Francisco skyline across the water. Eight East Bay dog parks worth the drive.

Point Isabel Regional Shoreline — 2701 Isabel St, Richmond. Twenty-three acres of off-leash Bay shoreline, trails, and beach access, with a dog wash on site. @ebrpd

Cesar Chavez Park — 11 Spinnaker Way, Berkeley. A 90-acre off-leash hilltop on the Berkeley Marina, kite hill and Bay Bridge views, shorter lines than Point Isabel.

Hardy Dog Park — 491 Hardy St, Oakland. A small fenced run under the College Ave overpass where the regulars know each other's dogs by name.

Joaquin Miller Park — 3590 Sanborn Dr, Oakland. Off-leash trails inside the redwoods that most newcomers never explore. They are the move.

Hap Magee Ranch Park — 1025 La Gonda Way, Danville. Two fenced runs, small and large dogs separated, worth the drive even from Oakland.

Heather Farm Dog Park — 505 N San Carlos Dr, Walnut Creek. A big, well-kept run with a water station where most of the local dogs are regulars.

Albany Bulb — Foot of Buchanan St, Albany. Off-leash on the old landfill peninsula, with public art, Bay views, and a sculpture trail. The dog park with personality.

Lake Chabot — 17600 Lake Chabot Rd, Castro Valley. Miles of dog-friendly fire road along the water.

Eight for the dogs: Point Isabel, Cesar Chavez, Hardy, Joaquin Miller, Hap Magee, Heather Farm, Albany Bulb, and Lake Chabot. Where does yours pull the leash toward?
