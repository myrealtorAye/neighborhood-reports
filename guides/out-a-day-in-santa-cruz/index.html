Bay Area A Day in Santa Cruz

Each fall tens of thousands of monarch butterflies cluster in a eucalyptus grove at Natural Bridges, beside a wave carved sea arch, on the edge of Santa Cruz. Eight Santa Cruz day trip stops.

Natural Bridges State Beach — Santa Cruz. A sea arch, tide pools, and a monarch grove that draws thousands of butterflies October to January. Time it for the monarchs.

West Cliff Drive — Santa Cruz. A clifftop path above the surf from the lighthouse to Natural Bridges, walkers, bikers, and sea otters below. Walk or bike the whole cliff.

Steamer Lane — Santa Cruz. The legendary surf break below the surfing museum in the lighthouse, watch the pros from the point. Watch a session from the rail.

Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk — Santa Cruz. The 1907 seaside amusement park with the Giant Dipper wooden coaster on the sand. Ride the Giant Dipper.

Wilder Ranch State Park — Santa Cruz. The Old Cove Landing bluff trail, 2.4 miles and stroller friendly, past a fern grotto and sea coves. Walk the coastal bluff.

Henry Cowell Redwoods — Felton. An old growth redwood loop just up the hill with a steam train at Roaring Camp next door. Walk the flat grove.

Pacific Avenue — Santa Cruz. The walkable downtown spine of bookstores, cafes, and the historic Del Mar theater. Stroll and grab a coffee.

Santa Cruz Wharf — Santa Cruz. A half mile pier over the bay with sea lions barking below and seafood at the end. Walk out and watch the sea lions.

Eight in Santa Cruz: Natural Bridges, West Cliff Drive, Steamer Lane, the Boardwalk, Wilder Ranch, Henry Cowell, Pacific Avenue, and the Wharf. Where do you start?
