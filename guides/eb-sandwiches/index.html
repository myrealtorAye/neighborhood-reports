East Bay Sandwiches

Top Dog has grilled sausages on Durant since 1966, mustard self serve, cash only, and the line still forms at midnight. The East Bay counter sandwich is a whole religion. Eight worth standing in line for.

Top Dog — 2534 Durant Ave, Berkeley. Since 1966, a grilled sausage on a soft roll, the condiments are yours to pile on and they still only take cash. The after midnight order near campus.

Saul's Delicatessen — 1475 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. A proper Jewish deli on Shattuck since 1986, the matzo ball soup is the reflex but the pastrami Reuben is the one regulars guard. @sauls_delicatessen

Santoro's Italian Market and Deli — 475 Santa Clara Ave, Alameda. Mike and Heidi build supreme sandwiches to order on bread baked that morning and serve until it runs out. Call ahead, the lunch wait hits thirty minutes and they mean it. @santorositaliandeli

Kinder's Meats Deli BBQ — 1776 Arnold Industrial Way, Concord. A Contra Costa institution since 1946, three generations deep, and the Ball Tip sandwich is the one people cross the county for, tri tip carved straight off the smoker. @kindersbbq

Bakesale Betty — 5098 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The fried chicken sandwich on a slaw piled bun that wraps a line around the block, handed to you off an ironing board out front.

Brothers Deli — 2700 Park Blvd, Oakland. A tidy Park Boulevard counter where the Italian sub is the move, real crunch in the bread and the meats stacked the way they should be.

Ike's Love and Sandwiches — 2172 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. Dirty Sauce and a stacked sandwich with a menu longer than the receipt, every one named after an inside joke.

Gregoire — 2109 Cedar St, Berkeley. French technique out of a takeout window since 2002, home of the crispy potato puffs and a sandwich that rewrites itself every season.

Eight for the line: Top Dog, Saul's, Santoro's, Kinder's, Bakesale Betty, Brothers, Ike's, and Gregoire. Which counter do you wait at?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
