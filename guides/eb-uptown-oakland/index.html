Uptown Oakland

Eight Uptown Oakland spots inside a few walkable blocks, all under the Fox marquee where the East Bay comes to eat and stay out late. This Bay Area corridor along Telegraph and Broadway lets you park once and hit dinner, a show, and a nightcap on foot. Here's the local run.

Fox Theater — 1807 Telegraph Avenue, Oakland. The 1928 movie palace reopened in 2009 and now anchors the whole corridor, its glowing sign visible for blocks. Touring acts pack the 2,800-capacity room most nights, and the marquee alone is worth the walk over. Check the calendar before your crew picks a date. @foxoakland

Cafe Van Kleef — 1621 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The Uptown original, packed to the rafters with oddball art and lit by chandeliers, pouring its fresh-squeezed Greyhound with a cartoon-sized grapefruit slice. Live music on weekends, no cover, and the kind of room where strangers end up talking. Grab a stool early. @cafevankleef

Bar Shiru — 1611 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Right next door, the Bay Area's first hi-fi listening bar spins jazz and soul on vinyl, whole albums start to finish, through a wall of audiophile speakers. Craft cocktails, a deep Japanese whisky list, groups of six or fewer so the room stays calm. Come for the slow listen. @barshiruoakland

Make Westing — 1741 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Cavernous cocktail hall with an indoor bocce court, so your night has a built-in activity when conversation stalls. Happy hour runs 4 to 6 every day including weekends, the kitchen stays open late, and there's room for the whole group. Reserve a lane if you're rolling deep.

Itani Ramen — 1736 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Chef Kyle Itani's izakaya has been slinging bowls on this block for a decade, open till 9 most nights for a pre-show bowl or a late one after. Steamy, unfussy, and the kind of place regulars default to. Grab the Genki Room if your group runs big. @itaniramen

Aburaya — 362 17th Street, Oakland. Japanese fried chicken done loud, shio koji-marinated thighs fried crisp in potato starch, cold beer, and punk rock on the speakers since 2014. The whole kitchen is gluten-free with a stack of vegan options, so the picky friend has no excuse. Order the sando and the tots. @aburayaoakland

Alma Bar Cocina — 361 19th Street, Oakland. Small counter spot turning out street-style tacos at four bucks a piece, carne asada to pescado, with pozole verde on the board. Open late Thursday and Friday. That makes it a clutch stop after a show at the Fox. Split a few and add a super burrito. @almabarcocina

Modern Coffee — 381 19th Street, Oakland. The Leamington shop has poured quality coffee for the downtown crowd since 2009, with a pastry case worth arriving hungry for. Weekday mornings only, so it's the move before a matinee or a walk down Broadway. Established, unpretentious, exactly what a corridor needs. @moderncoffee

Eight for an Uptown Oakland night: Fox Theater, Cafe Van Kleef, Bar Shiru, Make Westing, Itani Ramen, Aburaya, Alma Bar Cocina, Modern Coffee. Dinner, a show, a nightcap, all walkable in one East Bay corridor. Which block do you start on?
