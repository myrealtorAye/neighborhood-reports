East Bay Pizza

The Cheese Board in Berkeley bakes a single pizza flavor a day, no menu, no choices, and the line still runs down Shattuck before the doors open. The East Bay takes its pizza personally. Eight pies worth planning a night around.

The Cheese Board Collective

1512 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. One vegetarian pizza a day, decided that morning, sold by the slice while a jazz trio plays in the corner. Check the board, get in line, thank me later.

Zachary's Chicago Pizza

5801 College Ave, Oakland. The stuffed deep dish that has run Rockridge since 1983, a molten pie you order before you sit because it bakes for half an hour. Worth every minute.

Pizzaiolo

5008 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The wood fired Temescal room where the crust chars blistered and the toppings change with the market. Book ahead for the weekend and sit at the counter.

Emilia's Pizzeria

2995 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. A tiny counter turning out New York style slices so good it sells out and closes early, cash and patience required. Thin, chewy, foldable, the real East Coast thing.

Little Star Pizza

1181 Solano Ave, Albany. Chicago deep dish with a cornmeal crust plus a thin crust for the table that cannot agree. The deep dish is the reason to cross into Albany. @littlestarsolano

Arizmendi Bakery

3265 Lakeshore Ave, Oakland. The worker owned co-op that bakes one gourmet pizza a day, corn and cheese one morning, roasted squash the next. Grab a slice and a scone. @arizmendilakeshore

June's Pizza

2408 Mandela Pkwy, Oakland. The West Oakland spot that came back, sourdough crust and a short rotating list, small batch and worth the wait. Watch for the day it fires the oven.

Lo Coco's

4270 Piedmont Ave, Oakland. Family run Sicilian since 1982, the thin crust with the garlicky red sauce your nonna wishes she made. BYO wine and settle in. @lococospiedmont

Eight pies: Cheese Board, Zachary's, Pizzaiolo, Emilia's, Little Star, Arizmendi, June's, and Lo Coco's. Which crust wins your Friday?
