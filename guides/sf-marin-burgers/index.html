San Francisco Marin Burgers

Eight Marin burgers that locals drive across town for, from a Tiburon dock deck to a Corte Madera park truck. Skip the chains at the mall. Here is the North Bay burger map you keep.

Phyllis' Giant Burgers

2202 4th St, San Rafael. Four decades in and the line still moves because the griddle never stops. Order the classic with cheese, grab a booth, and let the milkshake carry the conversation. The teriyaki burger sounds wrong and eats right. @phyllisgiantburgers

M&G Burgers

989 Magnolia Ave, Larkspur. A 1962 counter joint that never chased a trend, and the town loves it for exactly that. Burger, fries, thick shake, no reservation, no fuss. Old-school energy in a downtown that has otherwise gone boutique. @mandg_burgers

Buckeye Roadhouse

15 Shoreline Hwy, Mill Valley. Open off the 101 since 1937, this is the dressed-up burger with a white-tablecloth backbone. It took Best Burger in the county and did not get quiet about it. Sit at the bar, get the burger and a martini, thank yourself later. @thebuckeyeroadhouse

Sam's Anchor Cafe

27 Main St, Tiburon. The deck sits right on the water and half the boats tie up to eat. The burger is not reinventing anything. That's the point when the view does the heavy lifting. Ferry over from the city and make a day of it. @sams_cafe

Finnegan's Marin

877 Grant Ave, Novato. A downtown Irish pub that quietly won Best Burger in Marin and kept the receipt. Open 365 days, patio out back, a pint that arrives cold and correct. The kind of room where the bartender has your order by the third visit. @finnegansmarin

Napa Valley Burger Company

670 Bridgeway, Sausalito. Gourmet burgers a block off the ferry landing, tourists and locals sharing one patio. Wine-country toppings without the wine-country prices. Grab a window seat and watch the Bridgeway parade go by. @napavalleyburgercompany

Humble Cheeseburger

Town Park at Pixley Ave, Corte Madera. A halal smash-burger truck parked by the post office, and the picnic tables fill fast on a warm Saturday. Crispy edges, potato roll, no attitude. Closed Monday and Tuesday, so plan around it. @humblecheeseburger

Barefoot Cafe

1900 Sir Francis Drake Blvd, Fairfax. An Italian family cafe hiding a sleeper burger called the Barefoot Special, open from breakfast straight through dinner. Fairfax keeps it funky and this room proves it. Take the patio, order the burger, stay for the ravioli talk. @barefootcafefairfax

Eight for the Marin burger crawl: waterfront decks, a 1962 counter, a park truck, and a pub that keeps winning. Which stop is your Saturday?
