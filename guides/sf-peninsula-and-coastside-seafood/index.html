San Francisco Peninsula and Coastside Seafood

Seven Peninsula and coastside seafood spots worth skipping the Wharf for. From Pillar Point Harbor fry shacks to a Burlingame tank holding ten pound Maine lobsters, this is where locals eat crab, ceviche, and lobster rolls, coast to bayfront. Realtors call it location. You will call it lunch with a view.

Sam's Chowder House

4210 N. Cabrillo Hwy, Half Moon Bay. The lobster roll here got named one of America's five best sandwiches on national TV, and the oceanfront deck backs up the hype. Order it hot with butter, add a bowl of clam chowder, and settle onto the patio while the fog burns off. Weekends fill fast, so go early or reserve a table. @samschowderhouse

Barbara's Fishtrap

281 Capistrano Rd, Half Moon Bay. Frying since 1971, this Pillar Point Harbor shack does the fish and chips and homemade clam chowder you drove out here for, boats bobbing right past the patio. The hours bend to the weather, but they swear they will not close if there is a line. Come hungry, come casual, and time it for a clear day outside.

La Costanera

260 Capistrano Rd, Half Moon Bay. Same harbor, dressier end. Chef Carlos Altamirano's Peruvian kitchen sends out ceviche and handcrafted cocktails while 180 degree windows put the whole Pacific on your table. It is the special occasion pick of the coast, so save it for the anniversary, not the post hike hoodie run. Sunset reservations go first. @lacostanerarestaurant

Moss Beach Distillery

140 Beach Way, Moss Beach. Perched on a cliff with an ocean view from every table, this is where you take out of towners for crab cakes, cioppino, and a firepit on the dog friendly Seal Cove patio. Fair warning, it is a restaurant, not a distillery, and locals will happily tell you about the resident ghost. Book for sunset. @mossbeachdistillery

New England Lobster Market and Eatery

824 Cowan Rd, Burlingame. Hiding in a Burlingame industrial pocket near SFO sits the largest lobster tank on the West Coast, 75,000 gallons of Maine lobster up to ten pounds. Get the lobster roll, then hit the market counter for dinner. Weekday happy hour runs oysters and shrimp tacos cheap, 3 to 6. @newenglandlobster

Pacific Catch

243 South B Street, San Mateo. Downtown San Mateo's West Coast fish house is California born, and the sustainable poke, sushi, and grilled fish still hold up. Grab the B Street parklet for Aloha Hour, weekdays 3 to 6, when the fresh bites and tropical drinks go easy on the wallet. Good call before a movie downtown. @pacificcatch

Cook's Seafood

751 El Camino Real, Menlo Park. A fish market and counter restaurant since 1928, back when Menlo Park had orchards instead of venture funds. Order the fish and chips, then buy Dungeness at the counter, they will crack and clean it free. No ocean view, some of the freshest fish on the Peninsula and zero pretense. Eat it fried there or take it home to cook.

Seven for the coastside seafood run: Sam's Chowder House, Barbara's Fishtrap and La Costanera at Pillar Point, Moss Beach Distillery on the cliff, New England Lobster in Burlingame, Pacific Catch in San Mateo, Cook's in Menlo Park. Beach shack to white tablecloth, the Peninsula eats better than the Wharf. Which one is first?
