East Bay Poke Counters

Poke stopped being a fad and settled into a real East Bay habit, from a chef run counter by the Berkeley campus to a 680 corridor cluster where Walnut Creek alone has two. Sushi grade fish, build your own bowls, no bridge. Seven poke counters worth the lunch break.

Hashtag Poki — 3075 Telegraph Ave, Berkeley. A sushi chef run poke spot near the campus since 2016, sushi grade fish and build your own bowls, weekdays only. @hashtagpoki

Poke Parlor — 2485 Telegraph Ave, Berkeley. Build your own bowls with house made sauces and Asian and Latin twists, even poke tacos, on Telegraph near campus.

Oke Poke — 1529 Locust St, Walnut Creek. Seven signature bowls plus build your own and pokerritos, open daily on Locust. @okepokeco

Pokeatery — 1345 Newell Ave, Walnut Creek. A Walnut Creek poke shop since 2018 known for the Dole Whip float alongside the bowls. @pokeatery

Poke Don — 2491 San Ramon Valley Blvd, San Ramon. A San Ramon counter regulars praise for the ahi and albacore, lunch and early dinner, closed Sundays.

Poke Salad — 1213 W March Ln, Stockton. An independent Stockton poke shop, build your own bowls, Monday through Saturday. @pokesaladstockton

Ahipoki Bowl — 4932 Pacific Ave, Stockton. Open late daily on Pacific Ave for a build your own bowl past nine at night. @ahipoki

Seven bowls, Berkeley to Stockton: Hashtag Poki and Poke Parlor in Berkeley, Oke Poke and Pokeatery in Walnut Creek, Poke Don in San Ramon, and Poke Salad and Ahipoki in Stockton. What is in your bowl?
