San Francisco Peninsula Mediterranean and Greek

Eight Peninsula spots for Mediterranean and Greek, from white tablecloth Athens energy to a foil wrapped shawarma eaten over the steering wheel. Palo Alto hoards most of them, but Burlingame, San Mateo and San Carlos hold their own. This is the answer next time someone claims there is nowhere good to eat between showings.

Evvia Estiatorio — 420 Emerson St, Palo Alto. The grande dame of Peninsula Greek, sister to San Francisco's Kokkari, with an open kitchen, a cozy room and the lamb the founders grew up on. Reservations book out, so plan ahead, not wander in. Bring the client you want to close, order a whole grilled fish, and let the room do the talking.

Meyhouse — 640 Emerson St, Palo Alto. Two blocks from Evvia, a Turkish meyhane where meze, the grill and live jazz run late. It pulled a 2026 Diners' Choice nod, so it is not a secret anymore. Go with a table of friends, order a spread of small plates, and plan to linger past your reservation. @meyhouserestaurant

Oren's Hummus — 261 University Ave, Palo Alto. Hummus so silky it converted a generation of skeptics, scooped up with warm pita at a counter that is somehow always buzzing. Their own motto is rip, scoop, eat. That's about all the instruction you need. Grab a stool, then walk it off down University. @orenshummus

Mediterranean Wraps — 433 California Ave, Palo Alto. The California Ave lunch line regulars quietly guard, turning out falafel, shawarma and a loaded Greek salad for not much money. Parking is easy for once, and they will nudge you to order off their own site to dodge the app fees. Get the falafel deluxe wrap and eat it in the sun. @medwraps

Mykonos — 226 Lorton Ave, Burlingame. A meze house cooking over charcoal since 2020, where small plates and ouzo are meant to leave the table a happy mess. It anchors Burlingame's Lorton Ave, a walkable block worth the trip north. Bring a crew, order too much, and let someone else drive home.

Mediterranean Kitchen — 270 Lorton Ave, Burlingame. Same block as Mykonos, a warm room mixing Turkish, Greek and Middle Eastern plates with lamb and chicken turning on the rotisserie. Screens loop Mediterranean scenery. That's either soothing or a dare to book a flight. Come hungry, split the appetizer combo, save room for a gyro. @mkburlingame

SAJJ Mediterranean — 251 E 3rd Ave, San Mateo. Downtown San Mateo's build your own move, bowls and wraps loaded with shawarma, falafel and every sauce you can point at. They still run a food truck, so the flavors travel fine to a desk lunch or a park bench. Keep it simple with a chicken bowl, or go fully maximalist. Your call. @sajjstreeteats

Elia — 727 Laurel St, San Carlos. Aegean cooking with a floor show, think cheese set aflame tableside, live violin on weeknights and a DJ once the weekend hits. San Carlos keeps it a touch more low key than the city. That's the whole point. Go for a birthday, order the flaming cheese, and let them make a scene.

Eight for the Peninsula's Mediterranean and Greek: Evvia and Meyhouse for a real night out, Oren's and Med Wraps for a fast Palo Alto lunch, Mykonos and Mediterranean Kitchen for Burlingame's Lorton Ave, SAJJ for San Mateo, Elia for a San Carlos celebration.
