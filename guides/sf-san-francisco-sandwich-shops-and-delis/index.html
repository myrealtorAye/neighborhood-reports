San Francisco Sandwich Shops and Delis

Molinari has cured its own salami in San Francisco's North Beach since 1896, and the counter still draws a line out the door. Eight SF sandwich stops worth the wait, from a dutch crunch corner store to a Tenderloin banh mi window.

Molinari Delicatessen — 373 Columbus Ave, San Francisco. North Beach's Italian deli has cured its own salami since 1896 and still slices it to order for sandwiches that need two hands. Come before the lunch rush or you will meet the whole neighborhood in line. @molinaridelicatessen

Lucca Delicatessen — 2120 Chestnut St, San Francisco. The Bosco family has run this Marina deli since 1929, rolling their own ravioli in back and stacking cold cuts up front. Grab a sandwich and a tub of fresh pasta for later. @luccadeli

Deli Board — 1058 Folsom St, San Francisco. This SoMa shop builds gloriously overstuffed sandwiches with house brined meats and names you have to decode off the board. Order online ahead, the midday line is real. @deliboardsf

Palm City Wines — 4055 Irving St, San Francisco. Out in the Outer Sunset, this corner spot stacks East Coast style hoagies next to a natural wine list. Grab a hoagie and a bottle and post up at the sidewalk tables. @palmcitywines

Wise Sons Jewish Delicatessen — 3150 24th St, San Francisco. The Mission's Jewish deli house cures its pastrami and piles it on rye, with matzo ball soup that tastes like somebody's grandmother made it. Save room, or take half home. @wisesons

Rhea's Deli and Market — 800 Valencia St, San Francisco. This corner market's spicy pork sandwich has a cult following, and you are barely a block from Dolores Park. Grab one wrapped to go and claim a patch of grass. @rheassandwiches

Roxie Food Center — 1901 San Jose Ave, San Francisco. A tiny Excelsior corner store hiding one of the city's best sandwiches: the Simon Special stacks turkey, pastrami, and cheddar on dutch crunch. Get there around noon and expect a line of regulars. @roxiefoodcenter

Saigon Sandwich — 560 Larkin St, San Francisco. In the Tenderloin's Little Saigon, this tiny window turns out some of the best banh mi in the city for a handful of dollars. Bring cash, order the combo, and do not expect a seat. @saigon.sandwich

Eight for San Francisco sandwiches: Molinari, Lucca, Deli Board, Palm City Wines, Wise Sons, Rhea's, Roxie, and Saigon Sandwich. Which counter gets your first stop?
