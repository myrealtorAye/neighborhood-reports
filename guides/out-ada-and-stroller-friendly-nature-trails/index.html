Bay Area ADA and Stroller-friendly Nature Trails

Not every great East Bay walk needs hiking boots or good knees, some of the best are paved, flat, and open to every wheel and stroller. Six accessible nature trails for everyone.

Lafayette Reservoir Loop

Lafayette. A paved two and a quarter mile loop around the water under the oaks, gentle grades, benches, and restrooms, wheelchair and stroller friendly the whole way. The everyone trail.

Del Valle Shoreline

Livermore. A flat paved stretch along the lake with accessible picnic sites and a swim beach, wide and smooth for wheels. Shade and water in one stop.

Crab Cove

Alameda. A paved bay shoreline path with a visitor center, tide views, and interpretive signs at eye level, flat and short. Nature by the water without a climb.

Coyote Hills Boardwalk

Fremont. An accessible boardwalk out over the marsh with egrets and an ancient shellmound nearby, level and railed. Big nature, easy access.

Point Pinole Shoreline

Richmond. Paved paths through eucalyptus to a fishing pier over the bay, with a shuttle to the pier for those who need it. Flat, breezy, and wide open.

Tilden Environmental Education Trail

Berkeley. A short paved all access nature trail near the Little Farm with braille and low signage, built for every visitor. Pair it with feeding the farm animals.

Six for every wheel: Lafayette Reservoir, Del Valle, Crab Cove, Coyote Hills, Point Pinole, and Tilden. Which flat one is your regular?
