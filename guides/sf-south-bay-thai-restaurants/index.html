San Francisco South Bay Thai Restaurants

Eight South Bay Thai spots worth the drive, spread across San Jose, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, and Cupertino, the ones locals keep coming back to. Curries, khao soi, papaya salad, crispy wings.

Original Krung Thai

642 S Winchester Blvd, San Jose. Family run since 1988, which in South Bay restaurant years is basically ancient royalty. Generous portions, rich curries, the comfort food that outlasts every trend near Santana Row. Come as you are, they mean it. @krungthaisanjose

Lanna Thai Cuisine

2043 Camden Ave, San Jose. Run by the Singard family from Chiang Rai, so the khao soi and northern dishes carry real hometown weight. Tucked in a Cambrian strip mall you would drive past a hundred times. That's where the good stuff hides. Green curry, red dragon, done right. @lannathaisj

Khaosan Thai

2062 Curtner Ave, San Jose. Street food energy, bold spice, and a free Thai iced tea if you check in on Yelp, the most South Bay flex there is. Open all day on weekends, so it slots in whether you want a quick lunch or a lazy Sunday feast. Bring a spice tolerance. @khaosan_thai_restaurant

Amarin Thai Cuisine

147 Castro St, Mountain View. Twenty-six years on Castro Street and a wall of Best of Mountain View wins since 1999, so the locals settled this debate long ago. Family recipes, BBQ pork shoulder, curries with depth. Prime downtown perch for a walkable dinner. @amarin_thai_cuisine

Shana Thai

311A Moffett Blvd, Mountain View. Open since 2008 on a quiet stretch of Moffett, cooking every dish like it is headed to their own family table. Grilled eggplant salad, chili rib eye, the noodle soups that fix a gray day. A neighborhood keeper, patio seating and all.

Siam Taste

172 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. Right on historic Murphy Avenue, the walkable downtown strip where half of Sunnyvale ends up on a Friday night. Pompano, lamb chops, and the outdoor seating that makes the wait feel like part of the plan. Easy stroll from Caltrain. @siamtastethai

The Old Siam

1111 W El Camino Real, Sunnyvale. An El Camino mainstay that has quietly outlasted the strip malls reshuffling around it. Classic Thai done without fuss, big enough to feed a crew, dependable enough that regulars stop reading the menu. Catering if you are feeding a whole block. @_theoldsiam

Pineapple Thai

19369 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino. Silicon Valley crowd favorite with a seven-day happy hour, patio seats, and pineapple fried rice served with a bit of theater. Duck dynasty, chive cakes, green curry. The date-night pick when you want the room to feel a little lively. @pineapplethaiofficial

Eight for the South Bay Thai shortlist: Original Krung Thai, Lanna, Khaosan, Amarin, Shana, Siam Taste, The Old Siam, Pineapple Thai. Which one is your go-to, and who did we miss between Milpitas and Campbell?
