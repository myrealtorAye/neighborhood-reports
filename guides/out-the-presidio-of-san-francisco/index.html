The Presidio of San Francisco

The Presidio is a former army base turned into a 1,500 acre national park inside San Francisco, with forest trails and beaches right under the Golden Gate Bridge. Eight Presidio stops.

Presidio Tunnel Tops — Presidio. A new free park built over the highway tunnels with lawns, trails, and a jaw dropping bridge and bay view. Watch the sunset from the bluff.

Crissy Field — Presidio. A flat bayfront promenade and restored marsh with the Golden Gate Bridge straight ahead, walkers, kite surfers, and dogs. Walk the shoreline path.

Baker Beach — Presidio. A wide sand beach with the classic postcard view of the bridge, best at sunset, chilly and wild. Catch the sunset from the sand.

Batteries to Bluffs Trail — Presidio. A 2.7 mile coastal trail from Baker Beach past old gun batteries to the bridge on the cliffs. Hike the bluff to the bridge.

Fort Point — Presidio. A Civil War brick fort tucked directly under the south end of the Golden Gate Bridge, waves crashing outside. Stand under the bridge.

Mountain Lake — Presidio. A restored natural lake with a flat loop and turtles, a quiet corner of the park. Loop the lake.

Andy Goldsworthy's Spire and Wood Line — Presidio. Hidden land art by the famous sculptor tucked in the Presidio forest, free to find. Hunt down the sculptures.

Lovers' Lane — Presidio. The oldest footpath in the Presidio, a tree lined diagonal the soldiers once walked to town. Stroll the historic lane.

Eight in the Presidio: Tunnel Tops, Crissy Field, Baker Beach, Batteries to Bluffs, Fort Point, Mountain Lake, the Goldsworthy sculptures, and Lovers' Lane. Where do you start?
