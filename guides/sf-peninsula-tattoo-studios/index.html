San Francisco Peninsula Tattoo Studios

Five Peninsula tattoo studios worth parking for before you ever think about crossing a bridge. Everyone assumes the good work only happens in the city, but San Mateo County has quietly built its own scene, traditional to single needle. Burlingame down to Redwood City.

Royalty Tattoo Collective — 1009 California Dr, Burlingame. Downtown Burlingame's walkable anchor shop, five stations plus a private room, licensed through San Mateo County Health. Seasoned collector or first tattoo, the vibe is friendly, not gatekept. Book by phone or stop in, closed Mondays. @royaltytattoocollective

Godspeed Tattoo — 620 S Norfolk St, San Mateo. A San Mateo landmark since 1998 and still owner run, the kind of established shop where walk ins are welcome and the artists actually draw custom. Tattoos only, no piercings, bring a valid ID. Open Tuesday to Sunday. @godspeed.tattoo

Hello Tattoo — 13 Lodato Ave, San Mateo. A tucked away, appointment only studio built for fine line and single needle work, botanicals, script, tiny matching pieces. Pink walled and calm, one client at a time. Message first, no walk ins, and ask to see healed work. @hello.tattoo

Paw Tattoo — 502 2nd Ave, San Mateo. Jia Kim tattoos roughly 600 micro pet portraits a year here, ten minutes from SFO, so it is the move for memorializing a dog or cat in single needle detail. Small, calm studio that books out far ahead, so plan early. @paw.tattoo

Ink Dripped Tattoo Studio — 1741 Broadway, Redwood City. Eight artists under one roof on downtown Redwood City's main drag, so whatever the style, someone here is built for it. Every piece gets customized to the client, easy to pair with a Broadway coffee before your slot.

Five for the Peninsula: Royalty in Burlingame, Godspeed, Hello and Paw clustered in San Mateo, Ink Dripped down in Redwood City. Traditional to fine line to pet portraits, no bridge toll required. Which one fits the piece you keep putting off?
