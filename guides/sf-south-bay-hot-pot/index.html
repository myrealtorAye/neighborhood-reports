San Francisco South Bay Hot Pot

Eight South Bay hot pot rooms worth skipping the national chains for, from Santa Clara wagyu counters to a Japantown standby, plus two out on the Milpitas and Fremont edge. Cold night, big table, everyone cooks their own. Where the regulars go.

Mikiya Wagyu Shabu House — 3590 Homestead Rd, Santa Clara. The wagyu comes out marbled like a painting and you swish it through the broth about ten seconds before it is done. All you can eat runs three tiers, so the size of the splurge is yours to set. Bring someone who appreciates good beef. @mikiyabayarea

Taichi Pot Shabu Shabu — 2640 Homestead Rd, Santa Clara. Same street as Mikiya, quieter room, individual pots so nobody fights over the spicy side. They handcraft the broths and age the beef, and the after work crowd fills the bar first. Go early or wait. @taichipotshabu

One Pot Shabu Shabu — 19648 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino. Tucked in the Marketplace plaza and running since 2011, back when Cupertino shabu options were thin. Each person gets a pot and a broth, which ends the ordering negotiations. The sesame sauce is why regulars stay loyal. @onepotshabushabu

Revopot — 2934 Aborn Sq, San Jose. The plates ride a conveyor belt past your seat and you grab what looks good. That's either efficient or dangerous depending on your appetite. All you can eat, open late on weekends, easy on the East San Jose crowd. Pace yourself. @revopot_sj

Tasty Pot — 1143 Story Rd Ste 100, San Jose. Taiwanese style, so your pot arrives loaded and you are not building it from an empty broth. They added all you can eat, and the milk tea comes from the same kitchen. A solid weeknight call when you want hot pot without the ceremony. @tastypot.usa

Shabu Time — 171 Jackson St, San Jose. The Japantown pick, individual pots in a room that has fed the neighborhood for years. Certified meats, udon and ramen add ons, and a lunch that moves fast enough for a work break. Walk it off with a loop past the museum after. @shabutimesj

Shabu Shabu Kiwami — 79 S Abbott Ave, Milpitas. The new kid on Abbott, all you can eat with premium cuts and a broth list that rewards the curious. Booths fill on weekends, so put your name down and let the wait build the appetite. Come hungry, leave slow. @shabukiwami

Happiness Hot Pot — 37140 Fremont Blvd, Fremont. Over the Fremont line and worth the drive, a Chongqing room where the beef tallow broth carries real heat. Order the goose intestine if you know it, the house shrimp paste if you are easing in. Bring water and a friend who commits. @happiness_hot_pot2025

Eight for the coldest South Bay nights: Mikiya and Taichi on Homestead, One Pot in Cupertino, Revopot, Tasty Pot and Shabu Time in San Jose, Kiwami in Milpitas, Happiness over in Fremont. Which broth are you starting with?
