San Francisco Marin Sushi and Japanese

Five Marin sushi counters worth crossing a bridge for, and the most decorated of them sits in Sausalito, not San Francisco. Sushi Ran keeps landing on national best of lists while plenty of city spots get passed over. From the water in Sausalito up to Larkspur, this is the North Bay sushi run locals rotate through.

Sushi Ran

107 Caledonia Street, Sausalito. Forty years in and still the name people cite when they argue the best sushi near the city isn't in the city. Book the omakase if you want the full show, or sit at the bar and let the chef read the room. That 200 bottle wine and sake list is not a bluff. @sushiran

Robata Grill and Sushi

591 Redwood Highway, Mill Valley. Easy to blow past on the frontage road. That's half the reason regulars love it. The name is the tell: robata means the charcoal grill, so order past the rolls and get skewers and grilled fish too. Lunch on weekdays, dinner most nights. @robatagrillandsushi

Samurai Sushi

425 Miller Avenue, Mill Valley. The Miller Avenue neighborhood spot where the specialty rolls do the talking, the soft shell crab and the baked ones especially. Fair warning, they trimmed hours to Tuesday through Saturday evenings while short staffed, so a Monday craving stays a Monday problem.

Oyama Sushi

282 Bon Air Center, Greenbrae. Tucked into the Bon Air shopping center, the kind of place you would cruise past on a grocery run and never guess the nigiri is this clean. Lunch and dinner daily, with online pickup if you would rather eat it on your own deck. Steady and fresh, no theatrics.

King of the Roll

552 Magnolia Avenue, Larkspur. Right on Magnolia downtown, and the move is whatever is on the specials board that week, bluefin toro, uni, oysters on the half shell. Evenings only, and they take reservations for six or more, so a big group works here. Call ahead for takeout.

Five for a Marin sushi day: Sushi Ran in Sausalito, Robata and Samurai in Mill Valley, Oyama in Greenbrae, King of the Roll in Larkspur. Omakase splurge or specials board gamble, which counter earns your first reservation?
