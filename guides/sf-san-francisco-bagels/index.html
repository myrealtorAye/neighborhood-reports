San Francisco Bagels

Eight bagel shops in San Francisco, and not one of them is the chain by the freeway onramp. The best boil-and-bake in this city hides on side streets from NoPa to Bernal, and half the crowd still drives past them for the frozen stuff. Here is where the line is worth it.

Schlok's Bagels & Lox — 1263 Fell St, San Francisco. A takeout window in NoPa where the lox is cured and smoked in house, not pulled from a package. Get there before the sold-out sign goes up around 1:45, because it will. @schloksbagels

Boichik Bagels — 3665 Sacramento St, San Francisco. The Berkeley legend crossed the bridge and Presidio Heights got the payoff: a dense, chewy, Montreal-meets-New-York bagel that sells out most mornings. Open daily until 1:30 or gone. @boichikbagels

Wise Sons Jewish Delicatessen — 3150 24th St, San Francisco. The Mission deli that put SF on the modern Jewish-food map, and the bagels are kettle-boiled with malt syrup the old way. Order one with the pastrami and thank yourself later. @wisesons

The Bagel Bakery — 151 Townsend St, San Francisco. Boiling and brick-oven baking New York style since 1976, tucked near the ballpark in South Beach. It is the quiet old-school pick when everyone else is chasing the new sourdough spots. @the_bagel_bakery

The Laundromat — 3725 Balboa St, San Francisco. Bagels by morning, wood-fired pizza and natural wine by night, all in a converted Outer Richmond laundromat. Come for the everything bagel, stay because nobody wants to leave. @thelaundromatsf

Uncle Benny's Donut & Bagel — 2049 Irving St, San Francisco. The Inner Sunset spot that opens at 4am, so the fog-shift commuters and the after-hours crowd both get theirs warm. Donut in one hand, bagel in the other, no judgment. @unclebennysdonut

Bones Bagels — 741 Diamond St, San Francisco. Naturally leavened sourdough bagels in Noe Valley, crisp outside and tangy enough to eat plain from the bag. Open Thursday through Sunday. That's exactly when the line forms. @bonesbagels

Chicken Dog Bagels — 237 Cortland Ave, San Francisco. A one-man sourdough operation running out of Avedano's butcher shop in Bernal Heights, named after the owner's dog. Saturdays are for walk-up sandwiches, and the line down Cortland tells you it is worth it. @chicken_dog_bagels

The SF eight: Schlok's in NoPa, Boichik in Presidio Heights, Wise Sons in the Mission, The Bagel Bakery in South Beach, The Laundromat in the Richmond, Uncle Benny's in the Sunset, Bones in Noe Valley, Chicken Dog in Bernal. Which neighborhood gets your Sunday morning?
