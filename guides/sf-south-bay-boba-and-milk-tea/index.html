San Francisco South Bay Boba and Milk Tea

Eight South Bay boba stops worth the drive, all independent, zero chains in the mix. If your milk tea loyalty stops at the mall kiosk, this thread is your permission slip to wander a little. Save it before the next Friday craving hits.

Pekoe

3276 S White Rd, San Jose. The split drinks are the move here, half one tea half another in the same cup, so you stop losing the flavor debate with yourself. Open past midnight most nights, which your 11pm sweet tooth will remember fondly. @pekoeteabar

Lacaphe

740 Story Rd Ste 5, San Jose. A women-owned spot where the Vietnamese coffee does the heavy lifting and the boba tags along happily. Order the ca phe sua da with pearls and you understand why the Story Road line stays long. @lacaphe.sj

Tea Era

20916 Homestead Rd Ste F, Cupertino. Slinging Taiwanese bubble tea since 2001, back when boba in Cupertino meant one or two places, not one per block. Get the classic milk tea and a side of popcorn chicken, the old-school combo that never needed fixing. @teaeracafe

Boba Oasis

447 Great Mall Dr, Milpitas. Tucked in the Great Mall for when the outlet-shopping stamina runs out and you need something cold in hand. A tidy little independent counter that quietly outclasses the food-court usual suspects. @thebobaoasis

Boba Pup

1080 Kiely Blvd, Santa Clara. They pour into collectible glass bottles, so your matcha comes with a keepsake you end up hoarding. Organic tea, real Vietnamese coffee, and a name that earns every dog lover walking past. @bobapupca

MoonTea

513 S Pastoria Ave, Sunnyvale. Parked on the El Camino corner for a quick pull-off, with teas that lean aromatic and Chinese-tea forward instead of syrup-first. The kind of cup that reminds you tea was the point before the boba boom. @moontea.boba

ChaChaCha

3623 Thornton Ave, Fremont. A woman-owned corner of Fremont doing pistachio matcha, Nutella milk tea, and croffles for the days one vice will not cover it. Come for the boba, leave planning your return around the 10 Won Ppang. @chachacha_fremont

Milk & Honey Cafe

34265 Fremont Blvd, Fremont. A family Taiwanese kitchen where the boba shares a menu with hot pot and night-market bites, so the group with mixed cravings all wins. Closed Mondays, so plan the milk tea run for any other day. @milkandhoneyfremont

Eight independent South Bay boba stops, two in San Jose, two in Fremont, one each in Cupertino, Milpitas, Santa Clara, and Sunnyvale. Save this for the next craving and drop the independent shop that belongs on the list. The South Bay bench runs deep.
