East Bay A Day in Hayward

The East Bay sleeps on Hayward, but B Street hides America's first brewpub, a world famous dive, and the oldest Japanese garden in California. Eight Hayward stops.

Buffalo Bill's Brewery — 1082 B St, Hayward. The first brewpub in America, pouring on B Street since 1983. Order a pint and the pizza.

Turf Club — 22519 Main St, Hayward. A world famous neighborhood bar readers call a can't miss. Post up at the bar. A @gregtrevizo add.

Kansai — 954 B St, Hayward. A downtown sushi spot named alongside the Turf Club as a must. Sit at the sushi bar. A @gregtrevizo add.

Stein Lounge — 939 B St, Hayward. A locals only bar with a killer jukebox and B horror movies on the screen. Feed the jukebox. A @gregtrevizo add.

Chai Thai Noodles — 20955 Mission Blvd, Hayward. A Thai kitchen readers put up with the East Bay's best. Get the boat noodles. A @rajhasacamera add.

Round1 — 551 Southland Mall Dr, Hayward. Bowling, arcade, and karaoke, the entrance is outside the mall not in. Roll a game. A @klauztrophobik add.

Hayward Japanese Gardens — 22373 N 3rd St, Hayward. The oldest traditional Japanese garden in California, free and quiet. Walk the koi pond loop.

Century at Hayward — 1069 B Street Cinema Pl, Hayward. The downtown movie house anchoring the B Street strip. Catch an evening show.

Eight in Hayward: Buffalo Bill's, the Turf Club, Kansai, the Stein Lounge, Chai Thai, Round1, the Japanese Gardens, and Century. Where do you start?
