East Bay Late-night Eats

Most of the East Bay goes dark at nine and the only thing open is a chain drive-thru. But Top Dog has slung linguica on Durant since 1966 and stays open past 2am. Six East Bay spots open past 10pm worth the trip.

Top Dog — 2534 Durant Ave, Berkeley. A hot-dog institution since 1966, open to 2am weekdays and 3am on weekends; the linguica with mustard, eaten standing up.

Tacos Sinaloa — 3132 E 12th St, Oakland. Open to 1am, where the al pastor and the mulitas are why the line never quits. @tacos_sinaloa_oakland

Kingfish Pub — 5227 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. A dive in a 1920s former bait shop with Cal memorabilia, open to 2am on weekends with a real kitchen. @kingfishpub

Eli's Mile High Club — 3629 Martin Luther King Jr Way, Oakland. A dive bar and music venue with a proper kitchen running food till close, 2am on weekends. @elismilehighclub

June's Pizza — 2408 Mandela Pkwy, Oakland. A walk-ins-only New York slice spot, 4pm to midnight every day. @junes_pizza

MAMA Oakland — 388 Grand Ave, Oakland. Handmade-meatball Italian with a late-night sandwich window for the post-bar crowd.

Six for past-ten: Top Dog, Tacos Sinaloa, Kingfish, Eli's Mile High, June's Pizza, and MAMA. What is your post-midnight order?
