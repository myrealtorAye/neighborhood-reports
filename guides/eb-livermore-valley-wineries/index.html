East Bay Livermore Valley Wineries

Livermore Valley sits 45 minutes from Oakland with more than 50 wineries on one AVA, and Wente has poured there since 1883, the oldest continuously family-owned winery in California. Most Bay Area locals have never made the drive. Nine Livermore tasting rooms worth the Saturday.

Wente Vineyards — 5050 Arroyo Rd, Livermore. Family-owned and pouring since 1883, the oldest continuously operated family winery in California, with a 2026 concert season. @wentevineyards

Concannon Vineyard — 4590 Tesla Rd, Livermore. Established 1883, the same year as Wente; the Petite Sirah program built the Livermore reputation. @concannonvineyard

Murrieta's Well — 3005 Mines Rd, Livermore. A historic estate in the Wente family portfolio, with tastings on a stone patio and vineyard views in three directions. @murrietaswell

McGrail Vineyards — 5600 Greenville Rd, Livermore. A smaller family operation, Cabernet-forward, with a dog-friendly patio and the family often in the room. @mcgrail_vineyards

Cuda Ridge Wines — 2400 Arroyo Rd, Livermore. A Bordeaux-variety specialist; the Cab Franc and Petit Verdot are why regulars drive past the bigger names. @cudaridge

Las Positas Vineyards — 1828 Wetmore Rd, Livermore. An estate winery with a tasting room locals book for small events; the Chardonnay is the hot-day order. @laspositasvineyards

Retzlaff Estate Winery — 1356 S Livermore Ave, Livermore. An organic estate with picnic-friendly grounds and an 1885 farmhouse; they encourage you to bring lunch. @retzlaffvineyards

The Steven Kent Winery — 2245 S Vasco Rd, Livermore. A Cabernet-focused, single-vineyard program for the drinker who came for the reds.

Wood Family Vineyards — 2407 Research Dr, Livermore. A small-production family winery steps from Steven Kent, worth the stop on the same loop.

Nine Livermore tasting rooms: Wente, Concannon, Murrieta's Well, McGrail, Cuda Ridge, Las Positas, Retzlaff, Steven Kent, and Wood Family. Which one gets your first Saturday?
