East Bay Contra Costa Spots You Told Us to Fix

The East Bay past the tunnel gets slept on, so here are the Contra Costa spots readers told us to fix. Eight Contra Costa stops.

Lita — 1602 Bonanza St, Walnut Creek. A Caribbean kitchen readers rank among downtown Walnut Creek's best. Get the jerk and a rum punch. A @waltsego add.

Elia — 1520 Locust St, Walnut Creek. An upscale Greek room with small plates and live music. Share the mezze. A @waltsego add.

Limon — 1524 Locust St, Walnut Creek. The Peruvian rotisserie readers named right beside Lita and Elia. Get the pollo a la brasa. A @waltsego add.

Cantina Jack's — 40 Crescent Dr, Pleasant Hill. Mexican with a twist from the Jack's Restaurant crew. Order a margarita and tacos. A @jrzygrl_94510 add.

Isla Grand — 548 Contra Costa Blvd, Pleasant Hill. A Filipino and Kapampangan kitchen readers begged us to add for the sisig. Get the sisig. A @wijuman add.

Zachary's Chicago Pizza — 140 Crescent Dr, Pleasant Hill. The deep dish stop readers pair with a downtown movie. Order the stuffed pie. A @kathesaidwhat add.

Slow Hand BBQ — 601 Main St, Martinez. All wood smoked barbecue over California white oak in downtown Martinez. Get the brisket plate. A @johnnylove73212 add.

The Dead Fish — 20050 San Pablo Ave, Crockett. A crab house on the Carquinez Strait with a view of the bridge. Order the killer crab. A @catzergram add.

Eight in Contra Costa: Lita, Elia, Limon, Cantina Jack's, Isla Grand, Zachary's, Slow Hand, and the Dead Fish. Where do you start?
