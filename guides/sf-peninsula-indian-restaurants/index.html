San Francisco Peninsula Indian Restaurants

Eight Peninsula Indian restaurants worth crossing town for, and four have Michelin recognition. Most people default to the nearest strip mall tikka masala and miss the coastal South Indian cooking, the daal simmered overnight, the halal Memoni samosa a town over. Burlingame down to Los Altos, here is the run locals keep quiet.

Rasa — 209 Park Rd, Burlingame. The Peninsula's Michelin rated South Indian room, where dosa comes butter paper crisp and seafood steams in banana leaf. Coastal Kerala cooking that regulars will tell you is worth the reservation. Order the vada pav sliders to start. @rasa_indian

Rasoi — 1425 Burlingame Ave, Burlingame. The Aslam family cooks Northern Indian and Pakistani off a lounge menu right on Burlingame Ave, so the tandoor and the cocktails share a room. Chef Asif's kebabs are the anchor here. Come hungry and let the table order for you. @rasoiburlingame

Curry and Kabab — 1923 S El Camino Real, San Mateo. Chef Mandy's North Indian curries and tandoori are the draw, but the sleeper order is the Indian pizza, naan dough under spiced toppings. Casual, quick, open till 10 daily. Grab the soya chaap if you see it. @eatcurrykabab

Tabla — 450 S Norfolk St, San Mateo. A neighborhood standby for Hyderabadi biryani and dosa, tucked off Norfolk in a room that fills up fast at lunch. The kitchen came up through bigger houses and it shows in the rice. Go for the biryani and a paneer butter masala. @tablaindiansm

Saffron — 1143 San Carlos Ave, San Carlos. A Michelin listed kitchen giving San Carlos daal makhani cooked low for a full day and butter chicken done Old Delhi style. There is a 100 year communal table built for long dinners. Bring people and share the chef's platters. @saffron_restaurants

Dosa Point — 840 Willow Rd, Menlo Park. A North and South Indian spot on Willow that runs live dosa parties, the batter crisping to order in front of you. This is the move when you want a dosa fix without the Palo Alto wait. Bring a group and try the specialty dosas.

Zareen's — 365 S California Ave, Palo Alto. The Michelin Guide Pakistani Indian counter just off the California Ave scene, famous for chicken Memoni samosas and shami kebabs from a recipe only a handful of grandmothers know. Halal, always packed, worth the line. Get the samosas first. @zareensrestaurant

Aurum — 132 State St, Los Altos. Downtown Los Altos' contemporary Indian room, Michelin recommended five years running, plating lamb chops and gulab jamun cheesecake under a hand painted mural. This is the special occasion end of the list. Book ahead and order the tasting. @aurumlosaltos

Eight for the Peninsula Indian short list: Rasa and Rasoi in Burlingame, Curry and Kabab and Tabla in San Mateo, Saffron in San Carlos, Dosa Point in Menlo Park, Zareen's in Palo Alto, Aurum in Los Altos. Which one are you booking first?
