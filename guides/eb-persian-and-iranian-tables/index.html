East Bay Persian and Iranian Tables

Good Persian food in the East Bay is not where you would look for it. The 50-year anchor is a Berkeley grocery on San Pablo Avenue, not a white-tablecloth room, and it just opened its own cafe. Seven East Bay Persian and Iranian tables.

Middle East Market and Cafe — 2054 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley. The 1974 Persian grocery, now with a father-and-son cafe serving koobideh and stews behind it. @memberkeley

Alborz — 1829 Mt Diablo Blvd, Walnut Creek. Persian cooking in the Bay since 1988, baghali polo and fesenjoon done right. @alborz.us

Persian Nights — 248 Grand Ave, Oakland. Persian stews and brunch on Lake Merritt, across from Children's Fairyland. @_persiannights_

Daryoush Persian Cuisine — 1534 Locust St, Walnut Creek. Berkeley-rooted, now downtown Walnut Creek, known for its tahdig and charbroiled kabobs. @daryoushrestaurant

Kabob House — 1250 Newell Ave, Walnut Creek. A small family kabob house, koobideh and slow lamb shank over saffron basmati. @kabobhousewalnutcreek

Razi Foods — 1740 Monument Blvd, Concord. Family halal Persian kitchen, kababs and home-style plates, big on catering. @razi.foods

Mazeh Persian Cafe — Sunvalley Mall, Concord. Persian sandwiches like kotlet and kabob, plus home-style daily specials. @mazeh_persian_cafe

Seven from Berkeley to Concord: Middle East Market, Alborz, Persian Nights, Daryoush, Kabob House, Razi, and Mazeh. Whose tahdig gets the corner piece?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
