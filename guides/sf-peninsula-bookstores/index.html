San Francisco Peninsula Bookstores

Books Inc in Palo Alto traces its roots to 1851, which the chain bills as the oldest independent bookseller in the West. The Peninsula was a bookstore town long before it was Silicon Valley, and the good shops never left. Eight indie bookstores still standing, Menlo Park to the coast.

Kepler's Books — 1010 El Camino Real, Menlo Park. Open since 1955 and a hub for the Beat and folk crowd in its early days, it still works as the Peninsula's town square with a book section. The events calendar is stacked with author talks. Go browse, stay for a reading. @keplersbooks

Bell's Books — 536 Emerson St, Palo Alto. Family run since 1935, with something like 350,000 new, used, and rare books stacked floor to ceiling. This is where you hunt down the out of print title nobody else has. Downtown Palo Alto, and yes, that old book smell. @windowsofbells

Books Inc — 855 El Camino Real, Palo Alto. The Palo Alto branch of that 1851 bookseller, tucked into Town and Country Village across from the high school. Strong staff picks, a great kids section, and an easy afternoon of browsing. Come for one book, leave with a stack. @booksincstores

B Street Books — 1471 Burlingame Ave, Burlingame. When the Books Inc here closed, this used bookshop kept Burlingame Ave in the book business. Deep secondhand shelves, fair prices, and a staff that actually knows the stock. Bring a tote, you will fill it. @bstreetbooks

Feldman's Books — 1075 Curtis St, Menlo Park. A quirky used and vintage shop that turned into a genuine scene, with author talks, poetry, live music, even a bike repair night. Open 12 to 5 daily. Come for the books, stay for whatever is happening. @feldmansbooks

Reach and Teach — 1179 San Carlos Ave, San Carlos. Part bookstore, part toy shop, built around peace, justice, and sustainability. The spot for gifts you will not find at a big box, plus STEM kits and puzzles for the kids. Closed Mondays. @reachandteach1

Florey's Book Co — 2120 Palmetto Ave, Pacifica. A Pacifica fixture since 1977, a short walk from the beach, mixing new and used with a strong local and teacher section. The kind of coastside shop you duck into when the fog rolls in. Open daily, later on Fridays.

Coastside Books — 432 Main St, Half Moon Bay. Serving the coast since 1972, right on Main Street for a post beach browse. Well curated fiction, a lovely kids corner, and cards and gifts from local artists. The owner knows her stock cold, so ask. @coastsidebooksinc

Eight for the Peninsula bookshelf: Kepler's, Bell's, Books Inc, B Street, Feldman's, Reach and Teach, Florey's, and Coastside. Which one gets your Saturday?
