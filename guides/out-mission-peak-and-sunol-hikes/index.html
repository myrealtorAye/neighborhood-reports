Mission Peak and Sunol Hikes

Eight East Bay hikes around Mission Peak and Sunol, ranked from the lung-buster to the stroll. Fremont and Sunol hold some of the Bay Area's best ridge climbs, and locals know which trailhead to pick. Here's your map to the good stuff.

Mission Peak via Stanford Avenue

Mission Peak Regional Preserve, Fremont. The classic gut-check: a 3-plus-mile climb to the summit that eats about five hours round trip once you count the photo line at the pole. Only 43 spots in the lot, so it fills before the coffee kicks in. Come on a weekday and you dodge the resident parking rules. @ebrpd

Hidden Valley Trail

Mission Peak Regional Preserve, Fremont. This is the steep line the district itself files under its challenging hikes, the one that turns your calves into a talking point. Open grassland the whole way up and almost no shade, so save it for a cool morning. The reward is that same summit ridge.

Mission Peak via Ohlone College

Mission Peak Regional Preserve, Fremont. The Peak Trail here is the gentler grade and part of the Bay Area Ridge Trail, that 550-plus-mile ring around the bay. Over 900 parking spaces and a four dollar machine, so the walkable payoff is you find a spot without circling. Passes a horse corral and shaded woodland the Stanford side never sees. @ebrpd

Ohlone Wilderness Trail

Mission Peak to Sunol, Alameda County. Twenty-nine miles of ridgeline linking Mission Peak to Del Valle, and here's the 2026 news: as of January 1 you no longer need the old Ohlone permit to hike it. Overnight camping still wants a reservation. Do an out-and-back chunk from the Stanford staging area and turn around when your legs vote.

Flag Hill Trail

Sunol Wilderness Regional Preserve, Sunol. A short 1.26 miles one way, but the switchbacks are steep enough to make it honest work before the view opens on Calaveras Reservoir and the Alameda Creek watershed. Look down and you're standing on fossil-bearing sandstone, an old seabed parked in the hills. @ebrpd

Canyon View Trail

Sunol Wilderness Regional Preserve, Sunol. A gentle 1.39-mile rise through Jacob's Valley that hands you off to Little Yosemite, the scenic long way, not the grind. Weathered serpentine and sandstone outcrops line it, and the oak woodland keeps it civilized. Good pick when the peak crowd wants a mellower local loop.

Little Yosemite (Alameda Creek Overlook)

Sunol Wilderness Regional Preserve, Sunol. A scenic gorge on Alameda Creek about two miles upstream from the Visitor Center, all boulders and green pools. The district officially renamed it Alameda Creek Overlook, and no, you can't swim: endangered species live in that water. Pack a lunch and a flat rock to sit on.

Maguire Peaks Loop

Sunol Wilderness Regional Preserve, Sunol. A 3.9-mile loop off the beaten Sunol track, with views of San Antonio Reservoir and Mt. Diablo and plants that grow rare everywhere else in the park. Park on Welch Creek Road and note the weekend permit fee at the kiosk. Quiet is the whole point out here. @ebrpd

Eight for the Fremont and Sunol high country: Mission Peak from Stanford, Hidden Valley, Mission Peak from Ohlone, the Ohlone Wilderness Trail, Flag Hill, Canyon View, Little Yosemite and Maguire Peaks. Which East Bay climb gets your next cool Saturday?
