Oakland and Berkeley Hills Parks

The redwoods in the Oakland hills once grew so tall that ship captains used a pair of them as a landmark to steer safely into the Golden Gate, before every last redwood was logged. Eight Oakland and Berkeley hills parks.

Redwood Regional Park — Oakland. The Stream Trail winds through the regrown coast redwood forest where the famous navigation trees once stood. Walk the valley floor.

Joaquin Miller Park — Oakland. The Sequoia Bayview, or Sunset, trail traces a ridge with bay views, 4.5 miles and easy. The sunset walk.

Roberts Regional Rec Area — Oakland. A redwood shaded park with a summer swimming pool and picnic groves, family friendly. The kids day.

Sibley Volcanic Preserve — Oakland. An ancient extinct volcano with a hidden hilltop labyrinth to walk. The strange and quiet stop.

Huckleberry Botanic Preserve — Oakland. A rare plant preserve with a self guided path through pallid manzanita found almost nowhere else. The botanist's walk.

Claremont Canyon — Berkeley. A steep climb up Stonewall Panoramic to a ridge with one of the best views over the bay. The calf burner with a payoff.

Leona Canyon — Oakland. A hidden creek canyon preserve with a wildflower trail few people know. The local secret.

Reinhardt Redwood Peak — Oakland. A short climb through the tallest regrown redwoods to a wooded summit. The high point of the grove.
