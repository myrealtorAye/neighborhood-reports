San Francisco Pacifica Beach Day (Peninsula)

Pacifica is home to the only Taco Bell in the world with a beachfront patio, a fire pit, and a walk up window for surfers, unironically the prettiest fast food on earth. Eight stops for a Pacifica beach day.

Taco Bell Cantina — 5200 Coast Hwy. The famous beachfront Taco Bell right on Linda Mar Beach, a fire pit, beer and freezes, and a walk up window for surfers straight off the sand. Order a Baja Blast and sit on the wall.

Linda Mar Beach — Pacifica. A long gentle surf beach that is the Bay Area's favorite spot to learn, board rentals and lessons right there, the fog burning off by midday. Beginner waves and bonfires.

Nick's Rockaway — 100 Rockaway Beach Ave. An oceanfront institution on Rockaway Beach since the fifties, breakfast to dinner with the surf out the window and a lounge that time forgot. Right on the water.

Puerto 27 — 525 Crespi Dr. A bright Peruvian kitchen near Linda Mar, ceviche and pisco sours and lomo saltado, a step up dinner after the beach. Book a window table.

Gorilla BBQ — 2145 Coast Hwy. A roadside barbecue joint smoking brisket and ribs out of a bright orange train car parked on the highway, weekends only until it sells out. Line up for the tri tip.

A Grape in the Fog — Rockaway Beach Plaza. A cozy wine bar in the Rockaway pocket with flights, light bites, and live music, the grown up stop after the beach. Watch the fog roll in with a glass.

Rockaway Beach — Pacifica. A dramatic pocket cove of dark sand and jagged bluffs with a paved promenade and tide pools, quieter than Linda Mar. The scenic walk.

Mori Point — Pacifica. A GGNRA headland of bluff trails and spring wildflowers above the sea, the dramatic walk out to the point over Rockaway. Hike to the overlook.

Eight for the Pacifica day: Taco Bell Cantina, Linda Mar, Nick's, Puerto 27, Gorilla BBQ, A Grape in the Fog, Rockaway Beach, and Mori Point. Where do you post up?
