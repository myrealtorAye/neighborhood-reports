San Francisco Downtown Menlo Park (Peninsula)

Kepler's Books has been the literary heart of Menlo Park since 1955, a Beat era haunt now sitting a mile from the Sand Hill Road offices that fund Silicon Valley. Eight downtown Menlo Park stops.

Kepler's Books — 1010 El Camino Real. The independent bookstore since 1955, a deep selection and a packed author event calendar. Browse and catch a talk.

Cafe Borrone — 1010 El Camino Real. The European style cafe beside Kepler's, coffee and a croque monsieur on the patio, the town's living room. Sit outside with a book.

Bistro Vida — 641 Santa Cruz Ave. A neighborhood French bistro since 1998 for steak frites and mussels, sidewalk tables and a full bar. The reliable dinner.

Camper — 898 Santa Cruz Ave. A bright California Mediterranean spot for wood fired vegetables and pastas. The date night table.

Ann's Coffee Shop — 796 Santa Cruz Ave. A tiny old school diner counter for eggs and a milkshake, unchanged for decades. The retro breakfast.

Santa Cruz Avenue — Santa Cruz Ave. The walkable main street of boutiques, cafes, and a toy store under leafy trees. Stroll the strip.

Fremont Park — Santa Cruz Ave. A small downtown park with a gazebo and lawns, good for a picnic off the avenue. The green break.

Dutch Goose — 3567 Alameda de las Pulgas. A beloved dive bar since 1966 for deviled eggs, burgers, and graffiti carved booths. The old school hang.

Eight in Menlo Park: Kepler's, Cafe Borrone, Bistro Vida, Camper, Ann's, Santa Cruz Avenue, Fremont Park, and the Dutch Goose. Where do you start?
