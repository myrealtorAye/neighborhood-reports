San Francisco Brunch Spots

Five San Francisco brunch spots worth setting a Sunday alarm for, ranked by how fast the line forms and how little you care once the plate lands. Neighborhood by neighborhood, the ones locals text each other about before the fog burns off.

Plow — 1299 18th Street, San Francisco. Potrero Hill's farm-to-table breakfast that put a pig on the sign and the Plow potatoes on every table around you. Get there before 9 or the sidewalk becomes your waiting room, coffee in hand. The lemon ricotta pancakes are the move. @plowsf

Zazie — 941 Cole Street, San Francisco. Cole Valley's French bistro where the back garden patio is the whole point and brunch stays first come first serve, so no reservation saves you here. Gingerbread pancakes, a dog at the next table, sun if the fog cooperates. Worth the wander up the hill.

Brenda's French Soul Food — 652 Polk Street, San Francisco. The Tenderloin corner where New Orleans lands on your plate: crawfish beignets, cream biscuits, grits done four ways. Weekday mornings the five-dollar Kickass Breakfast Club sandwiches are the quiet local secret. Come hungry, leave plotting a return.

Sweet Maple — 2101 Sutter Street, San Francisco. Lower Pac Heights brunch famous for Millionaire's Bacon, that candied thick-cut strip people cross town for. First come first serve, no reservations, so bring patience and an appetite. The kind of block that turns renters into buyers once they eat here twice.

Just For You Cafe — 732 22nd Street, San Francisco. Dogpatch diner since 1990, beignets and Creole plates at a counter where the neighbors know each other's orders. There's a parklet out front the block built together. That tells you everything about the street. Cash-friendly, big portions, zero pretense. @justforyoucafe

Five for the best brunch in San Francisco: Potrero Hill pancakes, a Cole Valley garden, Tenderloin beignets, Millionaire's Bacon, and a Dogpatch counter. Which neighborhood gets your Sunday first?
