San Francisco's Excelsior, Mission Street

Eight stops down one stretch of Mission Street in San Francisco's Excelsior, where the cross streets are named Persia, Russia, Naples and Peru and the food follows suit. Most people ride BART past it to the fancier end of the Mission. Their loss. Silver to Geneva, no bridge required.

Excelsior Coffee — 4495 Mission St, San Francisco. Black and Brown owned, flying the flags of Barbados, the Philippines and Mexico over the espresso machine. Come for a cortado, leave with an ube brownie from Ube Area you did not plan on. The corner where the neighborhood wakes up. @excelsiorcoffeesf

Restaurante Familiar — 4499 Mission St, San Francisco. Salvadoran and Mexican under one roof, so the pupusas revueltas and the carne asada plate land on the same table. Cheese-crisped masa, a mound of curtido on top, horchata to wash it down. Family name, family portions.

Recovery Room — 4528 Mission St, San Francisco. A proper neighborhood bar with food, drinks and music all under one sign, open to 2am most nights. A kitchen collab with Hidden Spot EXL means you can eat well past when other kitchens quit. The Excelsior after dark, minus the pretense. @recoveryroomsf

Hong Kong Bakery — 4711 Mission St, San Francisco. A Cantonese buns counter where a dollar or two buys a warm cocktail bun or a BBQ pork bun still fogging its bag. Cash, quick, no frills, gone by mid afternoon on the good days. Regulars grab a pink box and keep walking.

Calabria Bros — 4763 Mission St, San Francisco. The Excelsior still has an old-school Italian deli, and this is it, subs from around 8 dollars on Mission between Russia and Persia. House vinegar and a garlic spread the regulars order extra of. One sandwich feeds you twice if you have any restraint. @calabriabros

El Farolito — 4817 Mission St, San Francisco. The Excelsior outpost of the taqueria that made the Mission-style burrito famous, and the line still moves fast. Super burrito about the length of a forearm, al pastor under ten bucks, salsa bar doing half the work. Cash only, ATM inside. @taquerias_elfarolito

Superstar Restaurant — 4919 Mission St, San Francisco. A Filipino silog counter where 5 to 8 dollars gets you longsilog or tosilog, garlic rice and a fried egg included. Cash only, closed Wednesdays and Sundays, so time it right. The kind of cheap, filling plate the Excelsior runs on.

Mission Comics and Art — 5196 Mission St, San Francisco. The longtime Mission comic shop opened a second store here in 2026, a block past Geneva in a former optometry office, likely the first comic shop the Excelsior has ever had. New releases, back issues and a reason to end the walk indoors. @missioncomics

Eight down Mission Street in the Excelsior: Excelsior Coffee, Restaurante Familiar, Recovery Room, Hong Kong Bakery, Calabria Bros, El Farolito, Superstar, and Mission Comics. Silver to Geneva, no bridge, no BART transfer. Which corner do you start on?
