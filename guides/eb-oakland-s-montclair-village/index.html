Oakland's Montclair Village

Montclair Village is Oakland's hidden hill town, a walkable pocket of a 1974 egg shop, a cheese and wine shop, and a Sunday market tucked in the redwood foothills. Eight Montclair Village stops.

Crogan's Montclair — 6101 La Salle Ave, Oakland. A clubby American bar and grill, the village's living room. Get the pot roast.

Montclair Egg Shop — 6126 Medau Pl, Oakland. A brunch institution since 1974 with rotating benedicts. Come for the eggs.

Farmstead Cheeses and Wines — 6218 La Salle Ave, Oakland. A cheese and wine shop built for a hillside picnic. Build a board.

Bica Coffeehouse — 2027 Mountain Blvd, Oakland. A neighborhood coffeehouse in the heart of the village. Grab a cortado.

Montclair Village Farmers Market — La Salle Ave, Oakland. A Sunday market in the center of the village. Go Sunday morning.

Montclair Park — 6300 Moraga Ave, Oakland. A shaded neighborhood park with a creek and courts. Bring the kids.

Montclair branch, Oakland Public Library — 1687 Mountain Blvd, Oakland. A cozy hill library and community hub. Browse a while.

Montclair Railroad Trail — Mountain Blvd, Oakland. A short trail on an old rail bed into the canyon. Walk it after lunch.

Eight in Montclair Village: Crogan's, the Egg Shop, Farmstead Cheeses and Wines, Bica Coffeehouse, the farmers market, Montclair Park, the library, and the Railroad Trail. Where do you start?
