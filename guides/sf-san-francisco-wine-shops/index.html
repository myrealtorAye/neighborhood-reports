San Francisco Wine Shops

D&M Wines has sold Champagne from the same Fillmore Street corner since 1935, older than almost everything around it. San Francisco quietly runs on independent bottle shops like that, so here are Eight worth a detour, not a grocery aisle among them.

Ferry Plaza Wine Merchant — 1 Ferry Building, San Francisco. Tucked in the Ferry Building with a curated global list and a bar counter, so you can taste a glass before you commit to the bottle. A cheese shop from the same crew opens next door in summer 2026. Grab one before the ferry. @ferryplazawine

K&L Wine Merchants — 855 Harrison Street, San Francisco. The big one in SoMa, a famously deep list running from twelve dollar weeknight reds to rare and collectable trophies. The staff notes taped to the shelves are actually worth reading. Go in with a budget and a plan. @klwines

Biondivino — 1415 Green Street, San Francisco. A jewel box of a shop on Russian Hill that has beaten the drum for Italian wine since 2006, twenty regions deep, from Barolo you know to grapes you cannot pronounce yet. Tell them what you like and trust the handsell. @biondivino

Arlequin Wine Merchant — 384 Hayes Street, San Francisco. Family run in Hayes Valley since 1998, the pre show stop for something fun, fine, and a little interesting without needing a sommelier degree. Prices are friendlier than the block suggests. Perfect before the symphony. @arlequinwine

D&M Wines and Liquors — 2200 Fillmore Street, San Francisco. The Champagne temple, in the family and the same spot since 1935. Also the place for the three great French brandies, Armagnac, Calvados, Cognac, plus a wall of single malt. Come for bubbles, stay for the education. @dandmwineandliquors

Plumpjack Wine and Spirits — 3201 Fillmore Street, San Francisco. A Cow Hollow fixture since 1992 that keeps rare library bottles for collectors and easy everyday wine for the rest of us on the same shelves. No attitude, real range. Ask what is drinking well right now. @plumpjackwineandspirits

William Cross Wine Merchants — 2253 Polk Street, San Francisco. Everything on the shelves is hand picked, small production, and a little opinionated. There is a tasting bar in back and a serious cheese case up front, and the Wednesday tastings are the neighborhood move. @wmcrosswine

Verve Wine — 2358 Fillmore Street, San Francisco. Co founded by a Master Sommelier from the Somm films, so the geekery runs deep, but the room is friendly, not fussy. Strong on Champagne and off the beaten path bottles. Say your price and let them run with it. @vervewinesf

Eight for the San Francisco wine run: Ferry Plaza, K&L, Biondivino, Arlequin, D&M, Plumpjack, William Cross, and Verve. Which one is closest to your front door?
