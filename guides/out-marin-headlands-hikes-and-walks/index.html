Bay Area Marin Headlands Hikes and Walks

The most famous photo of the Golden Gate Bridge is taken from the Marin Headlands, a wild sweep of ridges and old army bunkers a quick drive over the bridge. Eight Marin Headlands hikes and walks.

Tennessee Valley Trail — Marin Headlands. 3.5 miles, easy, out and back, 4.7 stars. A flat, stroller friendly walk down a green valley to a hidden pocket beach. The everyone can do it hike.

Rodeo Beach and the Coastal Miwok Loop — Marin Headlands. 5.2 miles, moderate, 4.8 stars. A ridge and coast loop above a dark sand beach with a lagoon full of birds. The classic headlands loop.

Kirby Cove — Marin Headlands. 3 miles, easy, out and back, 4.8 stars. A downhill walk to a cove with the single best framed view of the Golden Gate Bridge. Go for the photo.

Rodeo Beach to Hill 88 — Marin Headlands. 4 miles, moderate, 4.8 stars. A climb past graffiti covered Cold War radar ruins to a ridgetop of endless ocean. The bunker hike.

Slacker Hill — Marin Headlands. 4.4 miles, moderate, 4.9 stars. A ridge climb to a knob with a head on view of the bridge and the whole bay. Earn the panorama.

Point Bonita Lighthouse — Marin Headlands. A half mile walk through a hand cut tunnel to a lighthouse on a rock spit reached by a small suspension bridge. Check the open days.

Hawk Hill — Marin Headlands. A short climb to the spot where thousands of migrating raptors stream past each fall, the counters out with binoculars. Come in September.

Battery Spencer — Marin Headlands. A quick walk out to an old gun battery for the towering, look up view of the bridge from its north end. The drive up classic.

Eight in the Headlands: Tennessee Valley, Rodeo Beach, Kirby Cove, Hill 88, Slacker Hill, Point Bonita, Hawk Hill, and Battery Spencer. Which view first?
