East Bay Downtown Fremont, Capitol Avenue

Eight walkable stops on Capitol Ave, downtown Fremont, and most of the East Bay drives past it for a mall parking lot. The new downtown grew up around the event plaza, and the food quietly got good. Start at one end, work the block.

SLIVER Pizzeria — 3658 Capitol Ave, Fremont. Sourdough-crust pizza with a pie of the day that changes daily, so your usual order is a moving target. The Berkeley transplant that fits the walkable downtown. Grab a slice and eat it out on the plaza.

Honest Indian Vegetarian — 3658 Capitol Ave, Fremont. Mumbai street food, bhaji pav mopped up with buttery pav and a tawa pulav that earns the hype. All vegetarian, and the chaat counter is where regulars park. Come hungry, the portions do not apologize.

Din Ding Dumpling House — 3768 Capitol Ave, Fremont. Handmade xiao long bao and braised rib chop noodle in the Downtown Event Center plaza. Watch the crew fold soup dumplings through the window while you wait. That's its own dinner theater. @dindingdumplingbayarea

RAW Superfood Cafe — 3768 Capitol Ave, Fremont. Plant-based acai bowls, matcha, and a banh mi sando two doors from the dumplings, so the whole plaza turns into a meal crawl. The move when someone in the group swore off the fried stuff. @raw.superfoodcafe

Pour Decisions Craft — 3530 Beacon Ave, Fremont. Craft coffee and craft beer under one roof, open at 7am, with Vietnamese phin, matcha, and a rotating wall of local taps. One of the few spots you can get a good beer with breakfast and nobody blinks. @pourdecisionscraft

Ristorante il Porcino — 3339 Walnut Ave, Fremont. The old-guard Italian a block off Capitol, over 30 years in and still packed for the rack of lamb and the pasta. White tablecloths without the attitude, the established date-night anchor downtown. Book ahead on weekends.

Miga — 39033 State St, Fremont. Small Korean kitchen on State Street doing hot soups and cold noodles at a fair price, closed Sundays so plan around it. The walkable downtown pick when the group wants comfort food and no fuss. Low-key, cash-friendly, worth the wait.

Cakes and Bakes — 3143 Walnut Ave, Fremont. The Fremont branch of the Indian bakery for black forest pastry, butterscotch ice cream, and a cake counter that handles every birthday on the block. Dessert to close the crawl, or the reason you skip dinner and come straight here.

Eight for a downtown that stopped needing a bridge: SLIVER, Honest Indian, Din Ding, RAW, Pour Decisions, il Porcino, Miga, Cakes and Bakes. All walkable on and around Capitol Ave. Which end of the block are you starting on?
