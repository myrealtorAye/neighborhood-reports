East Bay A Day in Downtown Danville

Danville's downtown runs along Hartz Avenue, an East Bay small town main street of white tablecloth rooms, a 1913 theater, and a rail depot museum. Eight downtown Danville stops.

Bridges Restaurant and Bar

44 Church St, Danville. The white tablecloth room made famous by a scene in Mrs Doubtfire. Book the patio.

Esin Restaurant and Bar

750 Camino Ramon, Danville. A farm to table favorite serving since 1998. Save room for the dessert.

Piatti

Hartz Ave, Danville. A bustling Italian trattoria with outdoor seating. Sit outside for the pasta.

The Peasant and the Pear

Hartz Ave, Danville. A cozy American bistro downtown. Try the pear salad.

Sideboard

Hartz Ave, Danville. A popular breakfast and brunch cafe. Get there early for pancakes.

Museum of the San Ramon Valley

205 Railroad Ave, Danville. Local history in the restored 1891 Southern Pacific depot. Tour the old depot.

Village Theatre and Art Gallery

233 Front St, Danville. A 1913 theater and gallery downtown. Catch a show or an exhibit.

Iron Horse Trail

Danville. The rail trail running through downtown for a walk or a bike. Stroll the old rail line.

Eight in Danville: Bridges, Esin, Piatti, the Peasant and the Pear, Sideboard, the museum, the Village Theatre, and the Iron Horse Trail. Where do you start?
