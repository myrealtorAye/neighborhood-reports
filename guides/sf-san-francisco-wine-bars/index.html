San Francisco Wine Bars

Eight San Francisco wine bars worth crossing town for, and the Dogpatch one is run by a Master Sommelier, among only about two dozen women in the whole country to earn that title. The best glass in SF is not behind a white tablecloth downtown, it is spread across eight neighborhoods. Start here.

Amelie — 1754 Polk Street, San Francisco. A cozy French wine bar in Polk Gulch known for flights, three pours built around a theme, plus live music some nights and a genuinely good happy hour. Low lighting, easy dates, no wine snobbery. @ameliesanfrancisco

Verjus — 550 Washington Street, San Francisco. The candlelit Jackson Square wine bar from the Quince and Cotogna team, no reservations, walk in and graze charcuterie and cheese with an ever changing pour list. On the Chronicle Top 100 and the Michelin guide for 2026. @verjus_sf

Ungrafted — 2419 3rd Street, San Francisco. The Dogpatch wine bar run by Master Sommeliers Rebecca Fineman and Chris Gaither, with a nearly 1,000 bottle list and a front of house that is all somms. Order the smash burger with something rare by the glass. @ungraftedsf

Bar Part Time — 496 14th Street, San Francisco. Part natural wine bar, part dance floor in the Mission, complete with a disco ball and a record driven crowd that stays late. Living wines from small producers, then the DJ turns it into a party. @barparttime

High Treason — 443 Clement Street, San Francisco. A sommelier run wine bar and bottle shop in the Inner Richmond where you can wear a hoodie, hear punk and hip hop on the speakers, and still drink grand cru Burgundy. Up to 45 wines by the glass. @hightreasonsf

Birba — 458 Grove Street, San Francisco. A tiny 16 seat Hayes Valley wine bar with a back garden that feels hidden from the city. Classic and natural bottles from a sommelier owner, no reservations, so slip in early. @birbawine

Palm City Wines — 4055 Irving Street, San Francisco. The Outer Sunset corner spot for natural wine and arm length hoagies, with a list built by a former Nopa wine director. Grab a glass and an Italian American hoagie, or a bottle to take to the beach. @palmcitywines

Scopo Divino — 2800 California Street, San Francisco. A Pacific Heights wine bar with live music five nights a week, a wine shop up front, and weekend brunch. Come for a glass, stay for the set. @scopodivino

Eight for the San Francisco wine bar crawl: Amelie, Verjus, Ungrafted, Bar Part Time, High Treason, Birba, Palm City, and Scopo Divino. Which neighborhood pour is your regular, and which SF wine bar should be on this list?
