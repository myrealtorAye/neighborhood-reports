East Bay Independent Bookstores

Moe's has stacked four floors of used books on Telegraph since 1959, and the East Bay still has nine more indies holding the line. Ten independent bookstores still standing.

Moe's Books — 2476 Telegraph Ave, Berkeley. Four floors since 1959; the top floor is rare and out of print, and most students never get past the second. @moes_books

Pegasus Books — 1855 Solano Ave, Berkeley. Used and new since 1969, with a basement of deep stock most browsers miss. @pegasusbooks

Pegasus Books Downtown — 2349 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. The Solano store's downtown sister, smaller but the buy counter turns faster.

Mrs — Dalloway's. 2904 College Ave, Berkeley. An Elmwood indie with a literary garden section, open Monday through Saturday. @mrsdallowaysberkeley

Diesel, A Bookstore — 5433 College Ave, Oakland. A Rockridge shop of politics, art, and literary fiction with a packed reading series. @dieselabookstore

East Bay Booksellers — 6022 College Ave, Oakland. Reopened in 2024 after a fire, same crew, new Rockridge home. @eastbaybooksellers

Walden Pond Books — 3316 Grand Ave, Oakland. Independent since 1973, with a poetry section curated tighter than any chain. @waldenpondbooks

Builders Booksource — 1817 4th St, Berkeley. Architecture and construction since 1982; the trade shelves are unmatched in the Bay. @buildersbooksource

Marcus Books — 3900 Martin Luther King Jr Way, Oakland. Founded 1960, the oldest Black-owned bookstore in the country. @marcus.books

Orinda Books — 276 Village Sq, Orinda. A quieter community bookstore with weekly author events, out on the 24 corridor. @orindabooks

Ten East Bay indies: Moe's, both Pegasus stores, Mrs. Dalloway's, Diesel, East Bay Booksellers, Walden Pond, Builders, Marcus, and Orinda. Which section do you disappear into?
