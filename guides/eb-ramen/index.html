East Bay Ramen

Three Chez Panisse alums have run a ramen counter on College Ave since 2013, rotating the menu weekly. The East Bay broth bench does not need the bridge. Eight ramen counters, Oakland to Berkeley.

Ramen Shop

5812 College Ave, Oakland. A counter from three Chez Panisse alumni since 2013, weekly menu following Bay Area produce. @ramenshopoakland

Itani Ramen

1736 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Chef Kyle Itani's izakaya-ramen spot, a seasonal bowl that changes monthly. @itaniramen

Mensho

4258 Piedmont Ave, Oakland. The Oakland outpost of Tokyo's Mensho, farm-to-bowl, with the tori paitan as the flagship. @mensho_piedmont

Sobo Ramen

2000 Milvia St, Berkeley. Named to SFGATE's top five Bay Area ramen, with tasting nights on the monthly calendar. @soboramenberkeley

Noka Ramen

90 Franklin St, Oakland. Hakata-style tonkotsu and homemade noodles in Jack London Square, letting the broth do the talking. @nokaramensf

Ippudo

2015 Kala Bagai Wy, Berkeley. The Berkeley outpost of the Japanese legend; the shiromaru and akamaru are the two orders. @ippudo

Tsuruya

1926 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. A focused, simplified menu in a smaller room, with a shorter line than the Telegraph corridor. @tsuruyaramen_berkeley

Marufuku Ramen

4828 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Hakata-style tonkotsu from the SF original, the Temescal outpost open since 2023. @marufukuramenofficial

Eight East Bay bowls: Ramen Shop, Itani, Mensho, Sobo, Noka, Ippudo, Tsuruya, and Marufuku. Which is your standing order when the fog rolls in?
