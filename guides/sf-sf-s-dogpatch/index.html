San Francisco SF's Dogpatch

Dogpatch is a rare San Francisco neighborhood whose Victorians survived the 1906 fire, an old shipyard district reborn with breweries, bakeries, and art galleries. Eight Dogpatch stops.

Neighbor Bakehouse

2343 Third St. A destination bakery for everything croissants, kimchi danishes, and morning buns out of an old industrial space. Get there early.

Museum of Craft and Design

2569 Third St. A small, playful museum of craft, design, and making, with a great gift shop. See the current show.

Piccino

1001 Minnesota St. A sunny corner spot for thin crust pizza and pasta with a cafe next door. The neighborhood dinner.

Harmonic Brewing

1050 26th St. A warehouse brewery with long tables, house beers, and food trucks, dogs and kids welcome. Grab a pint at a picnic table.

Long Bridge Pizza

2347 3rd St. A local pizzeria for blistered pies in a laid back room. The casual slice.

Third Rail

628 20th St. A snug cocktail bar known for house made jerky and a great old fashioned. The nightcap.

Minnesota Street Project

1275 Minnesota St. A cluster of contemporary art galleries in a converted warehouse, free to browse. Gallery hop under one roof.

Crane Cove Park

Illinois St. A bayfront park on the old shipyard with a little beach, lawns, and the historic gantry cranes. Walk the waterfront.

Eight in Dogpatch: Neighbor Bakehouse, the Museum of Craft and Design, Piccino, Harmonic Brewing, Long Bridge Pizza, Third Rail, Minnesota Street Project, and Crane Cove Park. Where do you start?
