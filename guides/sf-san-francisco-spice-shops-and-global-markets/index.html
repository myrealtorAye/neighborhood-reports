San Francisco Spice Shops and Global Markets

The Oakland spice shop Food and Wine called among the world's best finally crossed the bridge, and Oaktown now grinds 300 spices fresh on Fillmore Street. Eight San Francisco spice shops and global markets worth the trip.

Oaktown Spice Shop — 2132 Fillmore St, San Francisco. The Oakland favorite's first SF outpost, a cayenne red storefront where 300 spices, house blends, and chiles are ground in small batches and scooped to order. Grab a blend and go heavy. @oaktownspice

San Francisco Herb Co — 250 14th St, San Francisco. A wholesale spice house since 1973 that quietly opens its warehouse to the public, bulk bins from allspice to za'atar at prices that embarrass the supermarket. Bring a long list and stock up.

La Palma Mexicatessen — 2884 24th St, San Francisco. The Mission masa mothership since 1953, dried chiles by the bin and corn nixtamalized in back for fresh masa by the kilo. Grab guajillos, ancho, and a bag of masa. @lapalmamex.sf

Casa Lucas Market — 2934 24th St, San Francisco. A family Latin grocery a block down 24th, stacked with dried chiles, fresh epazote, achiote, and Latin American produce the chains never carry. Ask for a chile you have never cooked.

Samiramis Imports — 2990 Mission St, San Francisco. A Middle Eastern grocery on Mission for 50 years, bulk bins of za'atar, sumac, and Aleppo pepper, with house made muhammara at the counter. Smell everything before you scoop.

Lehr's German Specialties — 1581 Church St, San Francisco. A Noe Valley German shop since 1974 for quark, mustard seed, juniper berries, and the spice blends for a proper sauerbraten. Closed Mondays, so plan the schnitzel around it. @lehrssf

Rainbow Grocery — 1745 Folsom St, San Francisco. The worker owned cooperative since 1975 with a bulk spice wall the size of a studio apartment, jars by the hundred and refills by the ounce. Bring your own jars and go slow. @rainbow_grocery

New India Bazar — 1107 Polk St, San Francisco. The city's oldest Indian grocery, a designated Legacy Business over 30 years on Polk, whole cumin, curry leaves, asafoetida, and every dal you need. Ask which garam masala they cook with. @newindiabazarsf

Eight for San Francisco spice: Oaktown, SF Herb Co, La Palma, Casa Lucas, Samiramis, Lehr's, Rainbow, and New India Bazar. Which bin do you raid first?
