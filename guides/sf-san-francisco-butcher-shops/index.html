San Francisco Butcher Shops

Guerra Quality Meats has been breaking down whole animals on Taraval since 1954, back when every San Francisco neighborhood kept its own butcher. A surprising number of them still do. Eight SF butcher shops worth the trip across town.

Guerra Quality Meats — 490 Taraval Street, San Francisco. A family butcher and Italian deli since 1954, custom cuts up front and housemade lasagna and sausages in the case. Go for the meat, walk out with dinner already half made. @guerrameatssf

Avedano's Holly Park Market — 235 Cortland Avenue, San Francisco. A Bernal Heights butcher in a storefront that has sold meat for close to a century, breaking down whole animals from small farms. It nearly closed in 2025 before a backer stepped in, so go give it a reason to stay. @avedanos

Marina Meats — 2395 Chestnut Street, San Francisco. The Marina's neighborhood butcher, stocking sustainable meat, poultry, and fish cut at a real counter instead of a supermarket foam tray. Tell them your plan and they will steer you right. @marinameats

Bryan's Quality Meats — 3445 California Street, San Francisco. On the same Laurel Village block since 1963, with roots in the Flannery beef family and a meat case regulars plan their menus around. This is the splurge butcher for the holiday roast. @bryansmarket

Olivier's Butchery — 1192 Illinois Street, San Francisco. A French butcher in Dogpatch since 2011, turning out European style cuts and charcuterie you will not find at the chain store. Closed Monday and Tuesday, so plan the visit. @oliviersbutchery

Fatted Calf — 1 Ferry Building, San Francisco. The whole animal butcher and salumi maker that settled into the Ferry Building, where the salami, pate, and terrines are the reason to come. Grab a sandwich and eat it by the water. @fattedcalf

Le Beau Nob Hill Market — 1263 Leavenworth Street, San Francisco. A family run corner market since 1984 with a real butcher case hiding inside, the kind of place Nob Hill locals rely on for a good steak on a Tuesday. Small store, better meat than it lets on. @lebeaumarketsf

Antonelli Brothers — 3585 California Street, San Francisco. Third generation butchers working the counter inside Cal-Mart since 1956, a block from Bryan's, so Laurel Village quietly has two of the city's best. Old school service, custom cuts, no attitude. @antonellibros

Eight for a San Francisco butcher run: Guerra, Avedano's, Marina Meats, Bryan's, Olivier's, Fatted Calf, Le Beau, and Antonelli Brothers. Who is your neighborhood butcher, and which one belongs here?
