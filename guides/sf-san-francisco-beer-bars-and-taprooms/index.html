San Francisco Beer Bars and Taprooms

Eight San Francisco beer bars and taprooms worth building a night around, from a Lower Haight institution to a Bernal Heights tank room. Think of it as the walkable version of a citywide crawl, one neighborhood anchor at a time. Bring a designated driver and an appetite.

Toronado — 547 Haight St, San Francisco. The Lower Haight beer temple that has poured obscure Belgians and hop bombs since 1987, cash-friendly and gloriously grumpy. The tap list changes daily, so trust the chalkboard over whatever you planned. Grab a sausage from Rosamunde next door and post up. @toronadosf

Zeitgeist — 199 Valencia St, San Francisco. A walled beer garden where the picnic tables run long into the fog. Forty-plus taps, tall pours, and a cash policy that keeps the line moving. Claim a bench on a sunny afternoon before the after-work crowd descends. @zeitgeist_sf

Cellarmaker House of Pizza — 3193 Mission St, San Francisco. The Mission outpost of a beloved SF brewery, pairing hazy IPAs and barrel-aged stouts with Detroit-style square pies. New beers drop weekly, so freshness is the whole point here. Reserve ahead on weekends, then split a Classic Pepperoni. @cellarmakerbrewing

Standard Deviant Brewing — 280 14th St, San Francisco. A scrappy Mission taproom that bills itself as the coldest tasting beer in SF and leans into cheeky, self-deprecating service. Clean, true-to-style lagers and ales, plus a rotating list you can nurse all evening. Dog-friendly and low-key, the move on a mellow weeknight. @standarddeviantbrewing

Southern Pacific Brewing — 620 Treat Ave, San Francisco. A cavernous 10,000-square-foot warehouse turned beer hall, all soaring ceilings and industrial steel in the heart of the Mission. House lagers and IPAs pour alongside a solid food menu, with live music some nights. Big enough that you could lose your group on the mezzanine, so pick a landmark table. @southernpacificbrewing

Harmonic Brewing — 7 Warriors Way, San Francisco. Harmonic's Thrive City taproom sits right at Chase Center with a wide patio built for pregame pints and sunny afternoons. Approachable lagers, pale ales, and a full food and drink menu anchor the Mission Bay scene. Time your visit off game nights unless crowds are the goal. @harmonicbrewing

Laughing Monk — 1785 Fulton St, San Francisco. A NoPa brewery and gastropub known for Belgian-inspired ales and vibrant IPAs, now paired with Chef Reina Montenegro's bold, plant-forward Filipino plates. The lumpia and a fresh IPA is a combination you will think about later. Book a table on weekends, this corner fills up. @thelaughingmonksf

Barebottle Brewing — 1525 Cortland Ave, San Francisco. The Bernal Heights mothership, a working brewery and taproom where the fermentation tanks double as decor and the recipes come from homebrew contests. Award-winning hazies, plus wine, coffee, and NA options for the non-beer crowd. Tucked up on Cortland, it rewards the climb. @barebottle

Eight anchors for a San Francisco beer crawl: Toronado, Zeitgeist, Cellarmaker, Standard Deviant, Southern Pacific, Harmonic, Laughing Monk, and Barebottle. Which neighborhood taproom is your regular, and who is missing from this list?
