San Francisco Salvadoran Restaurants and Pupuserias

Six San Francisco pupuserias where the comal never really cools off. Everyone crowds the taqueria line and walks right past the Salvadoran counter next door. That's where the loroco, curtido and revueltas actually live. A mostly walkable Mission to Excelsior crawl.

Balompie Cafe — 3349 18th St, San Francisco. The soccer jersey pupuseria locals defend like a home team, handmaking revueltas and chicharron since the neighborhood rent was cheaper. Come hungry, get the pupusa platter, add platanos with crema. Closed Monday and Tuesday, so plan around it. @balompiecafe.1

Panchita's — 3091 16th St, San Francisco. Family run since 1989 and open stupid late, till 2:30am on weekends. That makes it the move after a show. Pupusas fresh off the comal, curtido with real bite, zero frills. A true Mission pocket institution. @panchitaspupuseria

Sarita's Grill and Beer — 995 Valencia St, San Francisco. The Valencia anchor that pairs handmade pupusas with pinchos, cebiches and a cold beer, which the pupusa only counters cannot touch. Order a carne asada pupusa and the crazy corn. Open till 11 on weekends.

D'maize — 2778 24th St, San Francisco. A tucked away 24th St counter that does genuinely vegan friendly pupusas without losing the plot, masa taken seriously and catering on lock. Family run. Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 3 only, so treat it as a breakfast and lunch move. @dmaizerestaurant

Reina's Restaurant — 5479 Mission St, San Francisco. Reina opened this Crocker Amazon room in 1991 and SFGate once called it the best pupusas around. Salvadoran and Mexican, breakfast through dinner, the established kind of place where regulars get greeted by name. @reinas_sf

Pupuseria Metapan — 4769 Mission St, San Francisco. Named for Lilian's hometown in El Salvador, this family kitchen reaches past pupusas into sopa de gallina india and sopa mariscada that outer Mission St regulars swear by. Open daily till 10:30, a proper sit down.

Six for the pupusa crawl: Balompie on 18th, Panchita's on 16th, Sarita's on Valencia, D'maize on 24th, then Reina's and Metapan down Mission St. Which one is your ride or die?
