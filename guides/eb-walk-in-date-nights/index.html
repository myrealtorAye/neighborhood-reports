East Bay Walk-in Date Nights

The Chez Panisse cafe upstairs runs the same kitchen as the dining room for half the bill, and it is friendlier to a walk-in. Eight East Bay date nights that need no reservation.

Chez Panisse Cafe — 1517 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. The Alice Waters cafe upstairs, the same kitchen as downstairs at half the price. @chezpanisse

Bar Panisse — 1515 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. The Chez Panisse bar next door, a late walk-in spot most diners never notice. @barpanisse_

Heinold's First and Last Chance Saloon — 48 Webster St, Oakland. Built in 1883 from a whaling ship, with a floor that still slants from the 1906 quake. @firstandlastchanceoakland

Drake's Dealership — 2325 Broadway, Oakland. A beer garden inside an old Dodge dealership, 30-plus taps and room to walk in. @drakesdealership

The UC Theatre — 2036 University Ave, Berkeley. Dark-night film screenings, five dollars with any prior show stub. @theuctheatre

Fox Theater lobby — 1807 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. A restored 1928 Moorish-revival palace; the mezzanine standing room is the move. @foxoakland

Albany Bulb — Foot of Buchanan St, Albany. A decommissioned landfill turned sculpture park, free and open, best at sunset.

Faction Brewing — 2501 Monarch St, Alameda. Bay views with the city skyline lined up off the deck at Alameda Point. @factionbrewing
