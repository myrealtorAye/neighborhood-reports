San Francisco Korean Restaurants

Eight San Francisco Korean spots worth crossing town for, from Michelin charcoal to 1am soju. Kimchi, bulgogi, pork belly, corn cheese hot off the grill, mapped from the Mission out to the beach. Your next craving now has a plan.

San Ho Won — 2170 Bryant St, San Francisco. Corey Lee's Mission charcoal BBQ is the rare Korean room wearing a Michelin star, and it stays low-key about it. Wood smoke, sharp banchan, a small dining room that books out early, so plan the reservation the way you'd plan an open house. @sanhowon

SSAL — 2226 Polk St, San Francisco. Chef Junsoo Bae serves one Michelin-starred tasting menu a night on Polk, Korean flavors through a French lens, all facing the open kitchen. It is a splurge at around $270 a head before wine, so treat it like the anniversary, not the Tuesday. @ssalsf

Um.Ma — 1220 9th Ave, San Francisco. Homestyle Korean a block off Golden Gate Park, named for the Korean word for mom and cooking the part. Come for bubbling soondubu and gochujang pork belly with sliced apple, stay for the back patio under 80-year-old trees, 90s hip hop, and cold soju.

Zzan — 643 Post St, San Francisco. Zzan means cheers, and this narrow Lower Nob Hill room near Union Square runs on soju, chicken wings, and K-pop videos on the projector until 1am. Reasonable prices, trendy crowd, the after-shopping spot you wish your own block had. @zzan_sf

707 Sutter — 707 Sutter St, San Francisco. A modern industrial Korean room hiding in plain sight downtown, built for the long group dinner that slides into drinks. The kitchen runs to 2am most nights. That's either a feature or a warning depending on your morning. @707sutter

Jijime — 5524 Geary Blvd, San Francisco. Outer Richmond Korean fusion where the boneless fried chicken lands in honey butter, soy garlic, or spicy, and the okonomiyaki fries have no business being that good. A low-key Geary storefront and a neighborhood workhorse you can bank on. @jijime_official

Toyose — 3814 Noriega St, San Francisco. Buried in the Outer Sunset between 45th and 46th, this is the phone-in Korean takeout the avenue locals would rather keep quiet. No frills, no scene, order over the phone and eat where the fog rolls in. The far edge of the city earns the drive.

Jin Mi — 366 Golden Gate Ave, San Francisco. Civic Center's honest Korean lunch counter on the Golden Gate and Larkin corner, big on portions and easy on the wallet. Order the soy sauce wings and a tofu soup, skip Saturday when they close, and walk out with leftovers you'll fight over.

Eight for every SF Korean mood, from Michelin counters to fog-line takeout and 1am soju: which neighborhood are you eating your way through first?
