San Francisco South Bay Seafood Restaurants

Eight South Bay seafood spots worth the drive, and not one is a chain crab bucket. San Jose keeps its best fish quiet, from a downtown room running since 1985 to strip-mall Cantonese tanks the neighbors guard, with a Milpitas halal kitchen and a Berryessa boil shack in the mix. Skip the freeway crawl to the coast. The good stuff sits closer than you think, and here is where locals go.

Scott's Seafood

200 S First St Ste 10, San Jose. Downtown's grown-up seafood room since 1985, white tablecloths, a raw bar, and a seafood boil that ignores the calendar. Book it for the anniversary you keep forgetting, or slide in for oysters at Social Hour, 3 to 6. @scottsseafoodsj

EMC Seafood and Raw Bar

378 Santana Row Ste 1100, San Jose. The uni pasta gets all the Instagram love, but the move is a seafood tower and a full raw bar tucked into Santana Row's shopping bustle. Open Fridays and Saturdays to midnight, so it survives a movie-then-dinner change of plans. @emcseafood

Blue Water Seafood and Crab

4180 N 1st St Unit 10, San Jose. An East Coast style crab house up in North San Jose, all cracked crab, fish and chips, and oyster shooters with or without the vodka. Open to midnight daily, patio heaters running, and yes, there are burgers for the one friend who fears the sea. @bluewaternorthsj

Dynasty Seafood

1001 Story Rd, San Jose. Second floor of Grand Century Mall, this is Cantonese banquet seafood done right, live tanks, carts of dim sum, and big round tables built for a loud family Sunday. Go with a crowd so you can order the whole steamed fish and still have room for har gow.

East Lake Chinese Seafood

2850 Quimby Rd, San Jose. An Evergreen strip-mall room where the seafood comes straight from the tank and weekend dim sum starts at 10:30. No frills, no view, the kind of neighborhood Cantonese kitchen you'd want three blocks from a new place. @eastlakechineseseafood

Darda Seafood

296 Barber Ct, Milpitas. Halal Chinese seafood with a Northwest China accent, hand-pulled noodles and clay-pot dishes alongside the fish. Tucked in a Milpitas office park where you'd never look. That's exactly why regulars keep the tables full. @dardaseafoodrestaurant

Claw Shack

1696 Berryessa Rd, San Jose. Viet-Cajun boil in a bag off Berryessa, where you pick your seafood, pick your sauce, pick your heat, then tie on a plastic bib and dig in with both hands. The Shackie's butter over shrimp and garlic noodles is the order. Closed Mondays, cash-friendly, napkins by the fistful. @clawshack

Mariscos Costa Alegre

855 N 13th St, San Jose. Nayarit style mariscos on the north side, aguachile, ceviche tostadas, and a molcajete of seafood that arrives steaming and dramatic. Casual, cash-friendly, and open for a long lazy weekend lunch. @costaalegresj

Eight for South Bay seafood: Scott's, EMC, Blue Water, Dynasty, East Lake, Darda, Claw Shack, and Costa Alegre. Raw bar to crab house to Cantonese tank to Viet-Cajun boil, all without crossing a bridge. Which one is landing on your Friday list first?

I'm an East Bay agent who eats all over the Bay Area. When you want the East Bay version of any of this, Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties are my home turf.
