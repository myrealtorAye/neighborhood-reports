San Francisco San Jose Wine Bars and Tasting Rooms

Five San Jose wine bars and tasting rooms worth the drive, from downtown pours to a Los Gatos patio under the vines. Locals skip the chain-restaurant list and go where a somm picks the bottle. Worth a detour on date night or a house-hunting break in the South Bay.

Enoteca La Storia

416 North Santa Cruz Ave, Los Gatos. Open since 2010, this is the Italian wine bar people mean when they say Los Gatos date spot: a vine-draped patio, sfizi small plates, and a wine list voted the best in Silicon Valley. Grab the patio if the fog stays north, and let the somm steer you off the usual Cabernet. @enoteca_la_storia

Ancora Vino San Jose

320 West St John St, San Jose. The Enoteca family's downtown tasting bar and bottle shop, tucked into the old Little Italy blocks most commuters blow past. Sommeliers stock it, so you can taste something obscure, then carry the bottle home. The name means more wine. That tells you how the afternoon tends to go. @_ancoravino_

Nomikai

48 South First Street, San Jose. A downtown drinkery where the wine list shares the room with sake, and the happy hour runs nine dollars before nine, Thursday through Saturday. It is a 21-plus, later-night kind of spot, so save it for after the open house, not before. @nomikaisj

Testarossa Winery

300 College Avenue, Los Gatos. Tastings pour daily inside the stone Novitiate cellars, a working winery making Pinot Noir and Chardonnay since 1993, with the 107 Bistro and Wine Bar next door for when a flight turns into dinner. Book the patio, wear layers, and pretend you are not eyeing the hillside homes above town. @testarossawinery

Tessora's Barra di Vino

234 East Campbell Avenue, Campbell. Downtown Campbell's only dedicated wine bar leans women-owned, organic, and a few Mexican labels you will not find on the grocery shelf, plus alcohol-removed pours for the designated driver. Seasonal small plates, a walkable main street, and none of the Santana Row parking drama. @tessorasbarradivino

Five for a South Bay wine crawl: downtown pours at Ancora Vino and Nomikai, a Los Gatos patio at Enoteca and Testarossa, and Campbell's own at Tessora's. Which corner of San Jose gets your next pour?
