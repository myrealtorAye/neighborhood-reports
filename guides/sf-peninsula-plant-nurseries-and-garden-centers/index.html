San Francisco Peninsula Plant Nurseries and Garden Centers

Eight Peninsula nurseries worth the weekend, led by the oldest native plant nursery in California, growing natives since 1960 and now parked out on the Half Moon Bay coast. Everyone defaults to a big box rack or a drive over the bridge. The good stuff hides on your side of the Bay Area, Palo Alto to Highway 92.

SummerWinds Nursery

725 San Antonio Rd, Palo Alto. The dependable all rounder, open 9 to 6 every day when the smaller shops are dark, with a deep bench of annuals, veggie starts, and houseplants. The staff actually know their zones, so bring a photo of your sad corner and ask. Good first stop for a new garden. @summerwindsca

Wegman's Nursery

492 Woodside Rd, Redwood City. Family run on the same corner for more than 50 years, now led by the founder's son Mark Wegman. It is the classic full service nursery, strong on indoor and outdoor plants and honest advice. Open daily 8 to 5. Go when you want a real person, not a QR code. @wegmansnursery

Golden Nursery

1122 2nd Ave, San Mateo. Family owned since 1976 and beloved by plant geeks for roses, Japanese maples, rare shrubs, and an annual citrus tasting where you sample before you plant. Weekends run 10 to 5. Bring a list and leave room in the trunk. @goldennursery

Ladera Garden and Gifts

3130 Alpine Rd, Portola Valley. Run by the Navarro family since 1981, part nursery, part gift shop, part florist tucked in the Ladera neighborhood. Come for the plants and leave with a pot, a candle, and a bouquet you did not plan on. Closed Mondays, otherwise 9 to 5. @laderagardenandgifts

Little Green A Plant Bar

1101 Main St, Redwood City. The fun one downtown, a coffee bar wrapped in houseplants with a build your own succulent bar in the back. Grab a latte, pot up a little guy, and catch a maker's market or an evening class. Open weekdays, shorter hours on weekends. @littlegreenaplantbar

Half Moon Bay Nursery

11691 San Mateo Rd, Half Moon Bay. Three acres of coastside growing since 1957, where the fog does half the watering and the stock runs from citrus to bamboo to color you will not find inland. Open daily, gate closes at 4:50. Make it the reward at the end of a Highway 92 drive. @hmbnurseryca

Yerba Buena Nursery

12511 San Mateo Rd, Half Moon Bay. The oldest native plant nursery in California, started in 1960, now off Highway 92 with natives from every corner of the state plus seeds and garden art. Plant something the local bees and birds actually want. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 to 4. @yerbabuenanursery

Shelldance Orchid Gardens

2000 Hwy 1, Pacifica. Historic greenhouses from 1949, now a rare orchid and bromeliad haven perched above the coast and partnered with the national park next door. Open weekends only, 11 to 4, so plan around it. Worth the trip just to wander the warm rooms and gawk. @shelldance_gardens

Eight for planting up your Peninsula yard: SummerWinds, Wegman's, Golden, Ladera, Little Green, Half Moon Bay Nursery, Yerba Buena, and Shelldance. Which one is getting your spring budget?
