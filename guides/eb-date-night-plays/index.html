East Bay Date-night Plays

Chabot opens its historic telescopes to the public free every Friday and Saturday night, 1,500 feet above the Bay. Hardly anyone plans a date around it. Most couples just rerun the same dinner reservation. Here are the East Bay date nights actually worth calling the sitter for. Seven of them.

Sunset hike at Inspiration Point — Wildcat Canyon Rd, Orinda, CA 94563 (Tilden Regional Park). The paved Nimitz Way stays nearly level while the Golden Gate, both bays, and the reservoirs open up on either side. Bring a thermos and time it for golden hour. @ebrpd

First Friday art walk — Telegraph Ave, 22nd to 27th St, KONO District, Oakland, CA. Galleries stay open and the street fills with music, makers, and food on the first Friday of every month, 5 to 9pm. The city's most underrated standing date. @oakfirstfridays.

Live jazz at Yoshi's — 510 Embarcadero W, Oakland, CA 94607. A serious jazz room and a full Japanese restaurant under one roof at Jack London Square, booking touring acts since 1972. Dinner and the show without moving the car. @yoshis_oak.

Bouldering at Berkeley Ironworks — 800 Potter St, Berkeley, CA 94710. Even if neither of you climbs, an hour on the boulder wall beats another bar. Watching is fun, falling is funnier, and it stays open to 10pm on weeknights. @berkeleyironworks.

Dance night at Ashkenaz — 1317 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley, CA 94702. A worn wooden floor and live world music since 1973, with a beginner dance lesson often built in before the band starts. Zero experience required. @ashkenazberkeley.

Tiki cocktails at Forbidden Island — 1304 Lincoln Ave, Alameda, CA 94501. A dim, fully themed tiki lounge pouring proper rum drinks since 2006, open to midnight. The kind of room that turns a Tuesday into a trip. @forbiddenisland.

Stargazing at Chabot Space and Science Center — 10000 Skyline Blvd, Oakland, CA 94619. Free telescope viewing on the observatory deck every Friday and Saturday, 7:30 to 10:30pm, weather permitting, through refractors from 1883 and 1916. The night sky does the talking. @chabotspace.

Seven East Bay date nights worth the sitter: Inspiration Point at sunset, Oakland First Fridays, jazz at Yoshi's, bouldering at Ironworks, a dance floor at Ashkenaz, tiki at Forbidden Island, telescopes at Chabot. Which one are you booking first?
