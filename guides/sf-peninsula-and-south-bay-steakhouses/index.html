San Francisco Peninsula and South Bay Steakhouses

Seven Peninsula and South Bay steakhouses worth the drive, and not a single national chain among them. Everyone books the big mall names, then wonders why dinner felt like an airport lounge. These are the local rooms that know their cuts and greet regulars by name. Where the good steak lives.

Alexander's Steakhouse

19379 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino. Open since 2005 and still the South Bay's special-occasion heavyweight, the kind of tasting-menu night that ends in Wagyu and a hot-towel sigh. Bring the appetite and the person you are trying to impress. @alexanderssteakhousesv

Sundance The Steakhouse

1921 El Camino Real, Palo Alto. Fifty years on El Camino and still packed with Silicon Valley lifers closing deals over prime rib. Old-school booths, a serious wine list, zero interest in trends. Book the early happy hour if you want a shot at parking. @sundancethesteakhouse

Arya Steakhouse

140 University Ave, Palo Alto. A Persian steakhouse that butchers in house and dry-ages its ribeye 28 days, then sends out saffron rice and kebabs alongside it. The whole menu is halal, the tomahawk a two-hands affair. Go hungry, downtown parking is its own sport. @aryasteakhouse

LB Steak

334 Santana Row, San Jose. The polished modern room on the Row, all marble and martinis, where the shopping crowd lands for a proper filet after a day of browsing. Sit on the patio, order the truffle fries, watch the promenade go by.

BE.STEAK.A

1887 S Bascom Ave, Campbell. An Italian-leaning steakhouse in the Michelin guide, plating wagyu next to handmade pasta and a Sunday all-you-can-eat sparkling buffet locals guard like a secret. Odd name, serious kitchen. Save room, or don't, the buffet forgives. @be.steak.a

Willard Hicks

280 E Campbell Ave, Campbell. Downtown Campbell's handsome corner spot for 100% Angus and a blackberry-glazed filet that regulars order without glancing at the menu. Deep cocktail list, weekend brunch, easy to love. Grab a patio table when the avenue lights up. @willardhicks

Wild Rose

200 E Campbell Ave, Campbell. Two doors down the same avenue, a candlelit eatery and bar built around Tomahawk Tuesday, a 40 oz flame-broiled monster with sides and dessert for the table. Craft cocktails carry the rest of the week. Bring friends, split the beast. @wildrosecampbell

Seven for the South Bay steak run: Alexander's, Sundance, Arya, LB Steak, BE.STEAK.A, Willard Hicks, Wild Rose. Skip the chain at the mall, the independents plate a better night. Which one is booking your next celebration?
