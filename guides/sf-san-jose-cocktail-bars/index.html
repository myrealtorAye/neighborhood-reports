San Francisco San Jose Cocktail Bars

Seven San Jose cocktail bars worth leaving the couch for, from a downtown speakeasy basement to a tequila patio on Santana Row. Most people default to the same two spots downtown. Locals work the whole map. Here's the route, neighborhood by neighborhood.

Paper Plane

72 S 1st St, San Jose. Downtown's craft cocktail anchor since 2013, with one of the biggest bottle collections in the Bay Area, so the move is to tell the bartender what you like and let them go off-menu. Order one of everything from the bar bites if you brought the group. Closed Mondays, open till 2am on weekends when downtown finally wakes up. @paperplanesjc

Haberdasher

43 W San Salvador St, San Jose. A basement speakeasy that grew out of the old Singlebarrel, now a full craft cocktail den with a deep archive of recipes and a whiskey club for the obsessed. No reservations, so weekends mean a wait, the kind of line that tells you the neighborhood is alive. Come Wednesday through Sunday, closed the first two days of the week. @haberdashersj

Miniboss

52 E Santa Clara St, San Jose. An arcade bar where the cocktails hold their own next to the Street Fighter cabinets, 90s nostalgia and a photo booth included. Free Play Thursdays turn it into the loudest happy hour downtown, and the $10 after-work menu is your friend. Bring quarters, or don't, and lose to a stranger who becomes a friend by round three. @sjminiboss

Dr

Funk. 29 N San Pedro St, San Jose. A serious rum and tiki house on the San Pedro Square strip, all Trader Vic escapism and fresh-juice cocktails without the kitsch tax. The rum list runs deep enough to have its own club. Grab a booth before a game or a show at the arena a few blocks over, and let the South Pacific do the rest. @dr.funksj

El Jardin

368 Santana Row, San Jose. Santana Row's tequila and mezcal patio, where Latin flavors meet a cocktail list built for a slow evening under the string lights. Weekend brunch stretches into afternoon agave, and the DJ programming picks up after. It's the rare Row spot that feels like a night out, not a mall stop. @eljardin.sj

Cardiff & Cocktails

260 E Campbell Ave, Campbell. Downtown Campbell's late-night cocktail bar and dance floor, a house-music institution since 2002 that turns into the best floor in the South Bay after dark. No cover most weeknights, tables for the weekends you want to remember. Dress to dance, and know Campbell's walkable core is why people keep moving here. @cardiffandcocktails

Cash Only

78 E Santa Clara St, San Jose. Half dive, half cocktail bar, open 2pm to 2am every single day of the year, with free pool early in the week and a whiskey list longer than most menus. The $7 Sazerac is the local's tell. Come for a proper Daiquiri, stay because nobody is rushing you out. @cashonlysanjose

Seven for a San Jose night that moves: Paper Plane, Haberdasher, Miniboss, Dr. Funk, El Jardin, Cardiff, and Cash Only. Speakeasy to arcade to agave to after-hours in Campbell. Which one is your first round?
