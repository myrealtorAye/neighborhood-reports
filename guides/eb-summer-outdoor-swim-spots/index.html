East Bay Summer, Outdoor Swim Spots

The East Bay does not cool off in the ocean, it cools off in lakes. Lake Anza alone has a sandy beach and summer lifeguards tucked inside the Berkeley Hills. Eight outdoor swim spots families keep coming back to.

Lake Anza — Off Canon Dr, Tilden Regional Park, Berkeley. A sandy beach inside a Berkeley Hills lake, lifeguards through summer, a swim test for kids over seven. The Berkeley summer ritual. @ebrpd

Lake Temescal Recreation Area — 6500 Broadway, Oakland. A roadside lake at the foot of Highway 13 with a sandy swim beach, picnic tables, and easy parking. The drive-up summer day.

Don Castro Regional Recreation Area — 22400 Woodroe Ave, Hayward. A reservoir with a fenced swim lagoon, lifeguards, and no boats in the swim area. The family safety pick.

Cull Canyon Regional Recreation Area — 18825 Cull Canyon Rd, Castro Valley. The same EBRPD format, a fenced lagoon and a sand beach, seasonal. The 580 corridor go-to.

Quarry Lakes Regional Recreation Area — 2100 Isherwood Way, Fremont. Two big lakes, a designated swim area, and a fishing pier. The southeast Bay summer escape.

Crown Memorial State Beach — 8th St and Otis Dr, Alameda. Calm Bay water, a long flat beach, and the San Francisco skyline across the water. Best for beach play, not deep swimming.

Clarke Memorial Swim Center — Heather Farm Park, 1750 Heather Dr, Walnut Creek. An Olympic-size lap pool plus a kids' pool with splash features, open year-round. The all-weather option.

Roberts Pool — 10570 Skyline Blvd, Oakland. A renovated, heated outdoor pool set inside a redwood grove, open weekends and holidays June 20 to September 27. Swimming when lifeguards are on.

Eight ways to cool off: Lake Anza, Lake Temescal, Don Castro, Cull Canyon, Quarry Lakes, Crown Beach, the Clarke Center at Heather Farm, and Roberts Pool. Where does your family go?
