San Francisco Downtown Mill Valley

Seven stops in downtown Mill Valley, the walkable Marin core where Throckmorton, Miller and Corte Madera meet at Lytton Square. Park once and you can hit a coffee roaster, a bakery, a market from 1929, and a historic theater without touching your car again. This is the redwood town with a stage. Here's the loop.

Equator Coffees

2 Miller Avenue, Mill Valley. The downtown cafe from a Marin roaster that's been at it since 1995, and the patio catches the morning sun before the fog gives up. Order a pour-over, grab an outside seat, and watch the town wake up. The dog crowd claims the benches early. @equatorcoffees

Flour Craft Bakery

129 Miller Avenue, Mill Valley. Fully gluten free and you would not guess it from the flaky morning buns and focaccia flying off the shelf. Tucked in the Lumber Yard plaza, it's Marin's answer for anyone who thought good pastry was off the table. Get there before the scones vanish. @flourcraft

Mill Valley Market

12 Corte Madera Avenue, Mill Valley. Family run since 1929 and still the last true neighborhood grocer in town, with a deli counter locals will happily line up for. Build a picnic here, carry it two blocks to the square, and eat like you live here. The sandwich order form is a local secret. @millvalleymarket

Piazza D'Angelo

22 Miller Avenue, Mill Valley. Southern Italian named for the piazza it sits on, family owned, with wood-fired pizzas and a house tiramisu people plan around. The bar is where the regulars land and the bartender knows them all. Book ahead on weekends or wait it out with a negroni. @piazzadangelo

Bungalow 44

44 East Blithedale Avenue, Mill Valley. Modern American cooking that's anchored downtown dinner since 2005, from the chili-honey fried chicken to a properly stirred martini at the bar. Fireside tables in winter, patio under the lights in summer. The Kobe burger has a quiet cult following. @bungalow44

Sweetwater Music Hall

19 Corte Madera Avenue, Mill Valley. A local institution since 1972 where you might catch a national act one night and a hometown songwriter the next, all ages, with a full kitchen so dinner and the show share one room. Check the calendar, then walk over early for a table. @sweetwatermusichall

Throckmorton Theatre

142 Throckmorton Avenue, Mill Valley. The town's performing arts anchor, running Tuesday night comedy, noon concerts, gallery shows, and the DjangoFest gypsy jazz weekend every fall. It's the kind of stage a small town has no business having, and somehow does. Grab seats before they go. @throckmortontheatre

Seven for a car-free day downtown: Equator, Flour Craft, Mill Valley Market, Piazza D'Angelo, Bungalow 44, Sweetwater, Throckmorton Theatre. Which one anchors your Mill Valley afternoon?
