San Francisco Downtown Burlingame (Peninsula)

Burlingame's downtown is built around the Peninsula's prettiest, most walkable shopping avenue, a mile of independent shops, bakeries, and cafes a quick train ride south from San Francisco. Eight stops in downtown Burlingame.

Copenhagen Bakery and Cafe

1216 Burlingame Ave. A Scandinavian bakery and cafe since the 1970s, Danish pastries and a marzipan cake case. Get a Danish.

Mingalaba

1213 Burlingame Ave. A beloved Burmese and Chinese spot, the tea leaf salad and samusa soup the draws. Order the tea leaf salad.

Il Fornaio

327 Lorton Ave. A handsome Italian trattoria and bakery, house pasta and wood fired bread. The reliable dinner.

Burlingame Avenue

Burlingame Ave. The tree lined main shopping street of boutiques, cafes, and ice cream, made for a stroll. Walk the whole strip.

Broadway Burlingame

Broadway. A second, older dining street with family run restaurants and bakeries a bit north. The local's block.

Therapy Stores

1348 Burlingame Ave. A playful indie boutique for gifts, jewelry, and home goods, a fun browse right on the avenue. Find a gift.

Washington Park

Burlingame Ave. A big green park with old oaks, a playground, and picnic tables just off the avenue. Picnic under the trees.

Kabuki Sushi

Burlingame Ave. A dependable neighborhood sushi bar for a fresh lunch off the strip. The easy sushi stop.

Eight in Burlingame: Copenhagen Bakery, Mingalaba, Il Fornaio, the Avenue, Broadway, Therapy Stores, Washington Park, and Kabuki. Where do you start?
