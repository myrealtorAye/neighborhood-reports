San Francisco Toy Stores

Eight independent San Francisco toy stores worth crossing town for, including a Castro variety store that has sold toys and costumes since 1936. The famous downtown shops closed, and the best ones turned out to be neighborhood corners all along. Kites, Gundam, blind boxes, Playmobil.

Tantrum — 248 Clement Street, San Francisco. A mom and pop toy shop since 2012, now on Clement with a vintage carnival look and shelves of European brands you will not find at a big box. Free gift wrap, which quietly saves you every birthday. @tantrumsf

Cliff's Variety — 479 Castro Street, San Francisco. Ninety years in the Castro and still the place for a last minute costume, a science kit, and a rubber chicken in the same trip. Part hardware store, part toy box, all institution. Go for the Halloween windows alone. @cliffsvariety

Woot Bear — 1512 Haight Street, San Francisco. Blind box heaven on Haight, stacked with Sonny Angel, Smiski, and designer vinyl, plus a gallery wall that turns over monthly. Genuinely dangerous if you have a Sonny Angel habit. Open daily. @wootbearbelly

Japantown Collectibles — 1581 Webster Street, San Francisco. Tucked in the Japan Center mall, packed floor to ceiling with Gundam kits, Godzilla, Nendoroids, and Pokemon. The model builders' clubhouse. Come knowing what you want or lose a happy hour to it. @japantownfun

Ying Hobbies and Toys — 63 Wentworth Place, San Francisco. Down a narrow Chinatown alley near Portsmouth Square, a tiny model and hobby shop that rewards anyone willing to find the door. Plastic kits, paints, and old school hobby gear. Come in with a project in mind.

Chinatown Kite Shop — 717 Grant Avenue, San Francisco. A whole store devoted to flight on the main drag of Chinatown, from paper dragons to stunt kites built to actually fly on Marina Green. The rare tourist stop locals still root for. Buy the dragon.

Just For Fun — 3982 24th Street, San Francisco. A Noe Valley classic crammed with toys, cards, puzzles, and gifts, plus weekly Magic and Pokemon tables in the back. Bring the kid who needs a booster pack and the adult who needs a birthday card.

Small Frys — 3985 24th Street, San Francisco. Right across 24th from Just For Fun, a Noe Valley shop stocking Playmobil, LEGO, Green Toys, and Haba for the little ones. Two toy stores facing off across one street is peak Noe Valley. Open seven days. @smallfrys24

Eight for San Francisco toy lovers: Tantrum, Cliff's Variety, Woot Bear, Japantown Collectibles, Ying Hobbies, Chinatown Kite Shop, Just For Fun, and Small Frys. Which neighborhood shop raised your inner kid?
