San Francisco Comic and Game Shops

Eight San Francisco comic and game shops worth crossing town for, and the Hayes Valley lounge on this list keeps a wall of toilet seat lids hand painted and signed by comic royalty like Jim Lee and Brian K. Vaughan. The real SF comic crawl is not one store, it is a loop across eight neighborhoods. Here they are.

Isotope Comics — 326 Fell Street, San Francisco. The self styled Comic Book Lounge in Hayes Valley, red sofas, gallery lighting, and that famous wall of signed toilet seat lids. Owner James Sime has judged the Eisner Awards, so the staff picks actually land. Go for the graphic novels, stay for the wall. @isotopecomics

Comix Experience — 305 Divisadero Street, San Francisco. Open since April Fools Day 1989 and now an official SF Legacy Business, this NoPa corner runs two real graphic novel clubs with author signings. Ask about a pull list and they will actually remember your taste. @comixexperience

Mission Comics and Art — 2250 Mission Street, San Francisco. On Mission between 18th and 19th since 2008, part shop and part gallery with a back room art space. The city liked it enough to grant funding for a second location in the Excelsior. New Wednesday books, deep back issues. @missioncomics

Amazing Fantasy — 650 Irving Street, San Francisco. The Inner Sunset neighborhood comic shop since the late 1970s, named for the issue where Spider-Man first showed up. Long boxes to dig through, a tidy new release wall, and an owner who has kept the lights on through every downturn.

Sour Cherry Comics — 3187 16th Street, San Francisco. A queer, women owned shop near Valencia that leans indie, small press, and all ages, with a real community table feel. The kind of place that hand sells you a book you did not know you needed. @sourcherrycomics

Silver Sprocket — 1685 Haight Street, San Francisco. Indie publisher and punk hearted comic shop on Upper Haight, open seven days, heavy on zines, stickers, and socially conscious small press books you will not find at a chain. Part gallery, part clubhouse. @silversprocket

Invisible Jet Comics — 159 West Portal Avenue, San Francisco. A Wonder Woman themed, women owned shop in West Portal with the front of the store built for kids and YA readers. New singles every Wednesday, pull boxes with no minimum. A great first comic shop trip for a family. @invisiblejetcomicsinc

Gamescape — 333 Divisadero Street, San Francisco. Not a comic shop but the city's tabletop institution since 1985, up the block from Comix Experience. Board games, RPGs, Warhammer minis, and the widest puzzle wall in town, plus game nights that pull a real crowd. @gamescapesf

Eight for the San Francisco comic and game crawl: Isotope, Comix Experience, Mission Comics and Art, Amazing Fantasy, Sour Cherry, Silver Sprocket, Invisible Jet, and Gamescape. Which counter gets your Wednesday pull, and which SF shop should be on this list?
