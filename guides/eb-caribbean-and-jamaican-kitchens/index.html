East Bay Caribbean and Jamaican Kitchens

Oakland jerk did not disappear when the famous names changed hands. The chef behind the old Kingston 11 fired his griddle back up in Uptown, and Trinidad and Jamaica still run this town, oxtail off the bone, curry goat, escovitch snapper. Five Caribbean kitchens, Oakland to Stockton.

Calabash

2300 Valdez St, Oakland. Chef Nigel Jones, Kingston born and the name behind the old Kingston 11, reopened in Uptown in 2022 with jerk and curry done right. @calabasheats

Cocobreeze Caribbean Restaurant and Bakery

2370 High St, Oakland. A Trinidadian kitchen from chef Annabelle Goodridge, curry goat and roti with a bakery case on the side, open limited days so check first. @cocobreezeco

Sweet Fingers

1707 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The long running Jamaican spot for jerk chicken and oxtail, just reopened at a new Telegraph Avenue address in 2026. @sweetfingersjamaican

Fiya Spice Caribbean

1343 Buchanan Rd, Pittsburg. A husband and wife kitchen in East County, oxtail and curry goat and jerk with escovitch red snapper, open daily. @fiyaspice

CJ's Jamaican and BBQ Kitchen

4368 N Sutter St, Stockton. Jerk chicken and Jamaican curry up in San Joaquin, lunch hours only Monday through Wednesday so time it right.

Five for the oxtail: Calabash and Cocobreeze in Oakland, Sweet Fingers back on Telegraph, Fiya Spice in Pittsburg, and CJ's up in Stockton. Which plate do you order, oxtail or curry goat?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
