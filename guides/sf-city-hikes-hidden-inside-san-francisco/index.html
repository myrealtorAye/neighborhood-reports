City Hikes Hidden Inside San Francisco

In the dead center of San Francisco there is a dripping eucalyptus cloud forest most residents have never walked into, silent and green a block off the freeway. Five hikes hidden inside the city.

Mount Sutro

Inner Sunset. A misty eucalyptus forest laced with soft trails in the middle of the city, fog dripping off the branches and the traffic gone silent. Enter from the Stanyan or Medical Center gates.

Lands End

Outer Richmond. The Coastal Trail around the city's wild northwest corner, cypress tunnels, shipwrecks at low tide, and the Sutro Baths ruins. The best walk in San Francisco, hands down.

Glen Canyon

Glen Park. A rugged creek canyon with rock outcrops and red tailed hawks, wild and steep right off the BART line. The city's hidden backcountry.

Batteries to Bluffs

Presidio. A short steep coastal trail past old gun batteries to a pocket beach under the Golden Gate, wildflowers in spring and the bridge overhead. Stairs and big views.

Mount Davidson

Miraloma. The highest point in the city topped by a hundred foot cross and a eucalyptus forest, a foggy summit loop with a downtown view when it clears. The quiet giant.

Five hidden in the city: Mount Sutro, Lands End, Glen Canyon, Batteries to Bluffs, and Mount Davidson. Which trail surprised you?
