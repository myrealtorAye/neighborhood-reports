San Francisco North Beach Italian

Molinari has cured salami and stacked sandwiches on Columbus Avenue since 1896, back when North Beach was the landing spot for every Italian in the city. Five North Beach classics that never changed.

Molinari Delicatessen — 373 Columbus Ave. The Italian deli since 1896, dry salami hanging from the ceiling and a Renzo Special sandwich big enough for two. Take a number and a hunk of parmigiano.

Tony's Pizza Napoletana — 1570 Stockton St. Tony Gemignani's world championship pizzeria on Washington Square, seven ovens firing every style, the margherita capped at 73 a day. Get there early for one.

Original Joe's — 601 Union St. The maroon booth institution frozen in the fifties, tuxedoed waiters, spicy rigatoni, and a proper martini. Old San Francisco you can still order off a menu.

Sotto Mare — 552 Green St. The tiny loud seafood joint with the best cioppino in North Beach, the Lazy Man's crab and a wait worth taking. Family style and no pretense.

Golden Boy Pizza — 542 Green St. The cash only slice window since 1978, square Sicilian focaccia pizza and cold beer, the after bar tradition. The clam and garlic is the one to get.

Five that never changed: Molinari, Tony's, Original Joe's, Sotto Mare, and Golden Boy. Where do you start a North Beach night?
