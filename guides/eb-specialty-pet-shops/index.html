East Bay Specialty Pet Shops

Everyone knows the big-box pet chains. Nobody mentions that the oldest and largest reptile store in the entire country sits on Fifth Street in Berkeley, open since 1970. Six East Bay specialty pet shops.

East Bay Vivarium — 1827 5th St, Berkeley. The oldest and largest reptile and amphibian store in the country, open since 1970. @theeastbayvivarium

Your Basic Bird — 2940 College Ave, Berkeley. A rare thing, a shop dedicated entirely to birds and their keeping, in the Elmwood. @yourbasicbird

Alameda See Spot Run — 1320 Park St, Alameda. Family-run natural and holistic food with full grooming, and kitten adoptions on weekends. @alameda.seespotrun

Concord Aquarium — 1776 Arnold Industrial Way, Concord. The Bay Area's oldest fish store, since 1988, where you grab a bucket and net your own fish. @concordaquarium

Bay Bridge Aquarium and Pet — 2400 Filbert St, Oakland. Runs a 2000-gallon quarantine system with a microscope before any fish is sold. @baybridgeaquarium

Aquatic Collection — 1212 W Winton Ave, Hayward. A saltwater and freshwater shop heavy on coral, in business since 2006. @aquaticcollection

Six that go deep: the Vivarium, Your Basic Bird, See Spot Run, Concord Aquarium, Bay Bridge, and Aquatic Collection. What is in your tank or terrarium?
