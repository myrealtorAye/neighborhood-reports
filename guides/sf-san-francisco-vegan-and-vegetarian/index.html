San Francisco Vegan and Vegetarian

Eight San Francisco vegan and vegetarian spots that turn skeptics into regulars. The city has been cooking meatless since Greens opened at Fort Mason in 1979, and the range now runs from Buddhist temple food to vegan barbecue. Bring the friend who insists they need meat on the plate and watch them go quiet halfway through.

Greens — 2 Marina Blvd, San Francisco. The one that started it all, tucked into a Fort Mason warehouse with the Golden Gate framed in the windows like a postcard nobody staged. Order the mesquite grilled brochettes and grab a window table before the sunset crowd claims them. Weekend brunch is the quiet secret. @greensrestaurant

Shizen — 370 14th Street, San Francisco. Vegan sushi that sounds like a dare and eats like a revelation, ten years deep in the Mission and still packed. The rolls lean on marinated tomato and eggplant where the fish would be, and you stop missing it by the second piece. Go early or brace for the wait. @shizensf

Wildseed — 2000 Union Street, San Francisco. Cow Hollow's airy plant based hangout where the patio does most of the talking on a sunny afternoon. The menu wanders the Mediterranean, the wine list is all vegan, and the crowd skews date night. Split the flatbreads and a carafe, thank yourself later. @wildseedsf

Golden Era — 395 Golden Gate Avenue, San Francisco. A Tenderloin fixture serving Asian comfort food that happens to be entirely vegan, no announcement necessary. The mock meats have converted more carnivores than any pamphlet ever could, and the portions forgive a big appetite. Get the Dragon Phoenix. @goldenerasf

Cha-Ya — 762 Valencia Street, San Francisco. Family run Japanese vegetarian cooking rooted in Buddhist shojin tradition, small room, big flavors, gentle prices. The tempura and the agedashi tofu arrive quiet and confident, the kind of meal that makes vegetables feel like the whole point. Come hungry, leave calm. @chayasanfrancisco

Vegan Mob — 90 Charter Oak Avenue, San Francisco. Toriano Gordon's plant based barbecue landed in the Bayview and the rib tips have a waitlist for a reason. Brisket burritos, garlic noodles, smoked everything, all vegan, none of it apologizing. Bring napkins and low expectations for leftovers. @officialveganmob

Aiso — 4068 18th Street, San Francisco. The Castro newcomer plating vegan tapas that look like they wandered off a fine dining tasting menu. Small sharing plates, a proper cocktail list, and a room that hums on weekends without shouting. Book ahead, order wide, let the table graze. @welcometoaiso

Om Sabor — 370 Grove Street, San Francisco. Cosmic vegan comfort food tucked inside Phonobar in Hayes Valley, hopping across Mexican, Korean, Indian and Venezuelan in one sitting. It started as a pop up and earned a home. That tells you plenty. Pregame the symphony here and skip the tourist traps. @omsabor

Eight for meatless done right: Greens for the view, Shizen for the sushi, Wildseed for the patio, Golden Era for the converts, Cha-Ya for the calm, Vegan Mob for the smoke, Aiso for the date, Om Sabor for the road trip on a plate. Which one wins your Friday?
