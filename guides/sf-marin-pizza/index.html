San Francisco Marin Pizza

Five Marin pizzas that hold their own against any slice in the city, most of them wood fired with the mozzarella pulled in house that morning. From a Sausalito fire pit on the water to a Mill Valley counter slinging pies since 1972, this is the North Bay pizza run locals keep on rotation.

Pizzalina — 914 Sir Francis Drake Boulevard, San Anselmo. Tucked into the Red Hill center, this is the neighborhood wood fired spot locals default to, hand stretched dough, Bianco di Napoli tomatoes, and mozzarella made in house. It lands on Bay Area best pizza lists for a reason. Ask about the pie of the month. @eatpizzalina

Bar Bocce — 1250 Bridgeway, Sausalito. Wood fired pies on a little back patio right on the water, with a fire pit and some of the best sunset seats in town. It runs first come with a waitlist, so put your name in and grab a drink while the harbor does its thing. Pure Sausalito hang.

Pizzeria Picco — 316 Magnolia Ave, Larkspur. A downtown Larkspur favorite doing traditional Neapolitan thin crust in a wood burning oven, mozzarella pulled fresh every day. Get the Marin pizza, then close it out with Double 8 Dairy soft serve. Small spot, big following, so time it. @restaurantpicco

Boca Pizzeria — 1544 Redwood Highway, Corte Madera. Rome meets Emilia Romagna pizzeria firing 12 inch Neapolitan pies in a 900 degree oven, two minutes flat, on long fermented Caputo dough with San Marzano tomatoes. Indoor or patio, seven days a week, and an easy family move on a weeknight. @bocapizzeria

Stefano's Pizza — 11 East Blithedale Ave, Mill Valley. The local classic since 1972, the solar powered corner that has slung something like ten million pies to Marin kids, teams, and late Friday nights. Not fancy, just the hometown standard for delivery and takeout. Grab a pie for the couch. @stefanos.pizza

Five for the Marin pizza rotation: Pizzalina in San Anselmo, Bar Bocce on the Sausalito water, Pizzeria Picco in Larkspur, Boca in Corte Madera, Stefano's in Mill Valley. Wood fired, harbor side, or straight to the couch. Which one is tonight?
