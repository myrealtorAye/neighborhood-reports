San Francisco South Bay Vietnamese, Pho and Banh Mi (San Jose)

San Jose has among the largest Vietnamese communities outside Vietnam, and the banh mi on Story Road runs a few dollars and rivals anything in Saigon. Five South Bay stops for pho and banh mi.

Pho Ha Noi — 969 Story Rd. The Little Saigon pho house with the line out the door, a clean northern style broth and rare beef, in the rebuilt Vietnam Town plaza. Follow the crowd.

Huong Lan Sandwiches — 1655 Tully Rd. A banh mi and deli powerhouse, the cold cut combo and grilled pork on a crackly baguette for a few bucks, plus a case of Vietnamese sides. Stock the fridge.

Duc Huong Sandwiches — 1020 Story Rd. A Story Road banh mi legend, house made cha and pate on airy bread, cheap, fast, and always right. Get the special. @duchuongsandwiches

Cali Sandwiches — 972 Story Rd. A local favorite for grilled pork and lemongrass chicken banh mi with pickled everything, a quick Little Saigon stop. Two will feed you for a fiver.

Lee's Sandwiches — 2307 McKee Rd. The San Jose born chain that made banh mi mainstream, the croissant combo and a Vietnamese iced coffee to go. The reliable road trip fuel.

Five for pho and banh mi: Pho Ha Noi, Huong Lan, Duc Huong, Cali, and Lee's. Where is your Story Road order?
