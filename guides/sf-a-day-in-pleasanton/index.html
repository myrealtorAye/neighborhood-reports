San Francisco A Day in Pleasanton

Pleasanton hides a picture book Main Street in the Tri-Valley, where a drive through dairy has scooped soft serve since before the freeways arrived. Eight Pleasanton stops.

Gay Nineties Pizza Co — 288 Main St, Pleasanton. A beloved old school parlor slinging award winning pies for decades. Get a pitcher and a pepperoni.

Meadowlark Dairy — 57 W Neal St, Pleasanton. A drive through dairy famous for soft serve, busy even on cold days. Roll up for a cone.

Baci Bistro and Bar — 500 Main St, Pleasanton. A downtown Italian room with a retro glow and a lively bar. Book a booth.

Towne Center Books — 555 Main St, Pleasanton. An independent bookstore that anchors the downtown, author events and staff picks. Ask for a rec.

Museum on Main — 603 Main St, Pleasanton. A local history museum in an old storefront telling the valley's story. Catch a walking tour.

Firehouse Arts Center — 4444 Railroad Ave, Pleasanton. A restored firehouse turned theater and gallery. Check the marquee.

The Rose Hotel — 807 Main St, Pleasanton. A boutique hotel on Main Street with old world rooms and a downtown perch. Stay the weekend.

Alameda County Fairgrounds — 4501 Pleasanton Ave, Pleasanton. The historic fairgrounds and horse track that hosts the summer county fair. Time a visit to the fair.

Eight in Pleasanton: Gay Nineties Pizza, Meadowlark Dairy, Baci Bistro, Towne Center Books, Museum on Main, the Firehouse, the Rose Hotel, and the Fairgrounds. Where do you start?
