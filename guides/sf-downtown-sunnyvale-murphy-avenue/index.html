San Francisco Downtown Sunnyvale, Murphy Avenue

Eight downtown Sunnyvale spots that make Murphy Avenue the South Bay's most walkable block. One historic street, the old Murphy Station, and a lineup you can cover on foot from the Caltrain platform. Here's the walk, anchor to nightcap.

Dishdash

190 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. The Levantine anchor of the block, packed at lunch since long before the tech crowd found it. Order the crab falafel and the toum, and pretend you'll share the toum. Weekend dinner books up, so reserve or wander over early. @dishdashrestaurants

Siam Taste

172 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. Downtown's steady Thai kitchen, the kind of local spot regulars guard like a secret they keep telling everyone. The pompano and the lamb chops go past the usual pad thai run. Patio seats fill first on warm evenings. @siamtastethai

Gumba's Italian Foods

176 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. A slice shop and red sauce institution that has fed Murphy Avenue for decades, no white tablecloth required. Grab a sausage slice at the counter or sit down for the chicken marsala. Open till 9:30pm, your call. @gumbas.sunnyvale

Roberto's Cantina

168 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. Jalisco-style plates and a bar stocked with more than 150 tequilas. That explains the Friday crowd spilling toward the 1am last call. Get the birria and a Cadillac margarita. Cozy inside, so a reservation helps. @robertos_cantina

Fibbar MaGees

156 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. An established Irish pub since 1994, holding the South Bay's largest whiskey list and sixteen screens for the match. Two patios, trivia Tuesdays, DJs on weekends. Thursday steak and potato runs ten bucks, no catch. @fibbarmagees

Burma Taste

124 S Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale. The block's Burmese corner, where the tea leaf salad wins over the friends who swear they never try anything new. Get that and the coconut chicken noodles, then thank whoever dragged you. Small room, so off-peak is the move. @burmataste

Moods Wine Bar and Bistro

111 W Evelyn Ave, Sunnyvale. Tucked in the Murphy Square building across from the historic strip, pairing a curated wine list with Afghan small plates and live music. Try a flight with the bolani. Happy hour runs 4 to 6, Tuesday through Sunday. @moods_wine_bar

Philz Coffee

125 S Frances St, Sunnyvale. Half a block off Murphy in Plaza del Sol, across from the Caltrain station. That makes it the downtown morning move before the platform fills. Order a Mint Mojito made one cup at a time. Opens at 5am on weekdays. @philzcoffee

Eight for one walkable block: Levantine anchor, Thai, Italian, Mexican, Irish whiskey, Burmese, a wine flight, and coffee for the train. Which Murphy Avenue stop gets your first weekend?
