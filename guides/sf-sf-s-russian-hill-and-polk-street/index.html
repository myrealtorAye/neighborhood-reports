San Francisco SF's Russian Hill and Polk Street

Swan Oyster Depot has served oysters over the same marble counter on Polk Street since 1903, just down the hill from the crookedest street in the world. Eight Russian Hill and Polk Street stops.

Swan Oyster Depot — 1517 Polk St. A narrow marble counter slinging the freshest oysters and crab since 1903, cash and a line out front. Slurp a dozen at the counter.

Bob's Donuts — 1720 Polk St. A 24 hour donut institution since 1953, home of the giant apple fritter and a late night line. Get a warm fritter at midnight.

Swensen's Ice Cream — 1999 Hyde St. The original Swensen's from 1948 on a Russian Hill corner, scooped from the shop that started a chain. Ride the cable car up to it.

Cheese Plus — 2001 Polk St. A gourmet cheese shop and deli famous for its grilled cheese, a wall of wedges and a good sandwich. Order the grilled cheese.

Nick's Crispy Tacos — 1500 Broadway. A fun Russian Hill spot for the crispy fried tacos, cheap and always packed. Get them Nick's Way.

Lombard Street — Lombard St at Hyde. The famous crooked block switchbacking down through the hydrangeas, cars creeping and cameras clicking. Walk down the brick curves.

Hyde Street Cable Car — Hyde St. The Powell Hyde line climbing over Russian Hill with the bay bursting into view at the crest. Ride it to the top.

Ina Coolbrith Park — Vallejo St. A steep terraced hillside park with one of the best quiet bay views in the city. Climb the steps for the view.

Eight on Russian Hill: Swan Oyster Depot, Bob's Donuts, Swensen's, Cheese Plus, Nick's, Lombard Street, the cable car, and Ina Coolbrith Park. Where do you start?
