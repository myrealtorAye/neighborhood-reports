Bay Area Backpacking and Overnight Trips

The Ohlone Wilderness Regional Trail runs about 28 miles from Mission Peak through Sunol to Del Valle with backcountry camps you reserve ahead, and most East Bay hikers have no idea they can sleep out this close to home. These are the overnight routes hiding in our own hills. Six East Bay backpacking and overnight trips.

Ohlone Wilderness Regional Trail — Mission Peak Staging, Stanford Ave, Fremont, through Sunol to Del Valle, Livermore. About 28 miles point to point, trail camps by reservation, permit required. The Bay Area's classic multi-day walk. @ebrpd

Rose Peak via Sunol — Sunol Regional Wilderness, 1895 Geary Rd, Sunol. 17.9 miles, 3,927 feet of gain, hard, 4.7 stars. The high country of the Ohlone Wilderness, a long day or a first night out.

Little Yosemite and Backpack Road Loop — Sunol Regional Wilderness, 1895 Geary Rd, Sunol. 5.9 miles, 1,230 feet of gain, moderate, 4.7 stars. Past the Little Yosemite gorge to the Sunol backpack area.

Mission Peak via Ohlone Wilderness Trail — Sunol Regional Wilderness, 1895 Geary Rd, Sunol. 10.2 miles, 2,221 feet of gain, hard, 4.6 stars. The quiet back way up Mission Peak from the Sunol side.

Venados Camp via East Shore Trail — Del Valle Regional Park, 7000 Del Valle Rd, Livermore. 2.4 miles, 144 feet of gain, easy, 4.6 stars. A short walk-in to a lakeside camp, the gentle first backpack.

East Shore and Hetch Hetchy Loop — Del Valle Regional Park, 7000 Del Valle Rd, Livermore. 3.7 miles, 436 feet of gain, moderate, 4.7 stars. Lakeshore miles from the Del Valle base camp.

Six for the overnight: the Ohlone Wilderness Trail, Rose Peak, Little Yosemite, the Mission Peak back way, Venados Camp, and the Del Valle lakeshore. Which one gets your first night under the stars?
