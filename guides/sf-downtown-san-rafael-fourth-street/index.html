San Francisco Downtown San Rafael, Fourth Street

Eight reasons Downtown San Rafael runs on Fourth Street, the most walkable stretch in Marin. Locals treat this corridor as the heart of the county seat: coffee roasted since 1935, a historic movie palace, and dinners worth the drive. Here's the honest walk, storefront by storefront, no tourist filler.

Sol Food — 903 Lincoln Ave, San Rafael. The Puerto Rican spot that put this downtown on the map, and yes, the line out the door moves faster than it looks. Order the garlicky pollo al horno, grab a jibarito, and plan to leave humming. A Marin classic that earned its crowd. @solfoodca

Il Davide — 901 A Street, San Rafael. An established Italian room feeding Marin since 1995, one block off Fourth and worth the half-block detour. The truffle ravioli has a quiet cult following, and the patio on a cool evening is the move. Book ahead on weekends, the locals do. @ildavidesanrafaelca

Lotus Cuisine of India — 812 Fourth Street, San Rafael. Organic-leaning Indian that leans harder into fresh than most, from clay-pit naan to a chicken tikka masala regulars order on repeat. Vegans and gluten-free folks get a real menu here, not an afterthought. A downtown anchor for a reason. @lotuscuisineofindia

Aroma Cafe — 1122 Fourth Street, San Rafael. Mediterranean fusion that's held down this block since 2002, pouring Graffeo espresso from up the street. Soups and dressings are made in house, the salads show up fresh daily, and there's beer and wine if lunch runs long. A dependable walkable stop mid-corridor. @aromacafesanrafael

Christopher B — Smith Rafael Film Center. 1118 Fourth Street, San Rafael. The historic movie palace that anchors downtown, restored and running as Marin's arthouse home for the Mill Valley Film Festival. Catch a foreign gem or a screening-and-conversation night under that lit-up neon tower. The blade sign alone is worth the walk. @cafilm

Graffeo Coffee Roastery — 1314 Fourth Street, San Rafael. One of North America's oldest artisan roasters, family-run since 1935, and yes, those are the beans served at big-name hotels. One dark, one light, one decaf, no gimmicks. Grab a pound whole-bean and you've got the local barista secret. @graffeocoffee

Johnny Doughnuts — 1617 Fourth Street, San Rafael. The Marin original, handcrafting doughnuts from a 1920s potato-dough recipe since the food-truck days. The apple fritter is a downtown institution, the crodough sells out, and the Johnny Joe coffee holds its own. Open daily at 7am, so beat the after-school rush. @johnnydoughnuts

Pond Farm Brewing Co — 1848 Fourth Street, San Rafael. A husband-and-wife brewpub at the walkable west end, with award-winning beer brewed on site, wine and cider for the non-beer crowd, and a real kitchen. Live music Saturdays, trivia Mondays, big airy taproom. The neighborhood living room that happens to make great beer. @pondfarmbrewing

Eight for a Fourth Street afternoon: Sol Food, Il Davide, Lotus Cuisine, Aroma Cafe, the Rafael Film Center, Graffeo, Johnny Doughnuts, and Pond Farm. Roaster, arthouse, brewpub, and three cuisines inside a few walkable blocks. Where do you start?
