San Francisco San Jose Pizza

Eight San Jose pizza spots locals drive across town for, downtown to Willow Glen to a Campbell corner, and not one national chain in the list. The tourist grabs a slice at the mall. You know better.

Pizza Bocca Lupo — 87 N San Pedro Street, San Jose. Tucked inside San Pedro Square Market, this family run spot fires a wood oven with Neapolitan pies, 00 flour and fresh mozzarella. Grab the Joe's Special, snag a patio table on the historic plaza, and yes, the garage across the street gives you 90 free minutes. Dogs welcome outside. @pizzaboccalupo

Sammy G's — 330 S 10th Street, San Jose. Steps from SJSU, this is where innovative pies meet craft beer and their famous loaded tots. Order the Buffalo Mac or the El Presidente with carnitas and orange sauce, then settle in for gameday on the big screens. The kind of corner that makes a neighborhood feel lived in. @sammygspizza

Jtown Pizza — 625 North 6th Street, San Jose. Deep in Japantown, one of the last three historic Japantowns in the country, they run Godzilla sized combos and a Signature pie with okonomiyaki flavors, furikake, nori, sweet mayo. Half the fun is the monster movie menu names. No web handle to tag, so wander in and see for yourself.

Bibo's NY Pizza — 1431 Bird Ave, San Jose. Fresh dough made daily at every location, and this Bird Avenue shop near Willow Glen is the sit down flagship. Fold a wide New York slice, or go big with a Pizza and Pasta pie. Bird Ave has quietly become one of the tastier stretches to live near. @bibosnypizza

Pizza Antica — Santana Row, San Jose. The grown up option on the Row, thin crust blistered in a wood oven, house made pastas, an approachable wine list. The chef leans on local farms, so the menu shifts with the season. Pair a pie with people watching on the promenade. That's half the reason anyone comes to Santana Row anyway. @pizzaantica

A Slice of New York — 3443 Stevens Creek Blvd, San Jose. A worker owned cooperative slinging real New York slices since 2006, awards stacked from the Merc to the Metro. Foldable, wide, blistered underneath, open late near Santana Row when the craving hits at 10pm. Proof that good pizza and shared ownership can live on the same block. @asliceofny

Giovanni's Pizza — 862 North 13th Street, San Jose. Same owners, one neighborhood, more than 17 years. Hand tossed pies baked to order in an antique brick oven, dough made fresh daily, closed Mondays like the old school places should be. No handle to tag, so let the brick oven do the talking.

Blue Line Pizza — 415 E Campbell Ave, Campbell. Downtown Campbell's local favorite in a made over historic building, famous for cornmeal crust deep dish alongside NY thin. Big bar, heated dog friendly patio, walkable to the farmers market strip. The sort of anchor tenant that tells you a downtown is doing fine. @bluelinepizza

Eight for the South Bay pizza run: Bocca Lupo and Sammy G's downtown, Jtown in Japantown, Bibo's on Bird, Antica and A Slice on the Santana Row side, Giovanni's off 13th, Blue Line in Campbell. Which slice wins your Friday?
