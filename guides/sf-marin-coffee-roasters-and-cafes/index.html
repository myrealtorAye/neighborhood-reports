San Francisco Marin Coffee Roasters and Cafes

Six Marin coffee stops worth planning a morning around, and the best part is the county roasts its own. Everyone grabs a freeway chain cup and misses the cafes locals actually linger in, from a San Rafael roastery to a Tiburon espresso bar steps off the ferry. Here is a walkable pocket of the North Bay, a cortado at a time.

Equator Coffees — 115 Jordan Street, San Rafael. The certified B Corp roaster behind a lot of the good cups poured across Marin, based right here in San Rafael. Order their beans at a cafe anywhere in the county and this Jordan Street roastery is where they were actually roasted. Local coffee, roasted local. @equatorcoffees

M.H — Bread and Butter. 101 San Anselmo Avenue, San Anselmo. A small, established San Anselmo bakery cafe turning organic flour and local farm ingredients into some of the best morning pastry in Marin. Order a coffee and a warm bun, grab a sidewalk seat, and watch the avenue wake up. This corner is the walkable heart of town. @mhbreadbutter

Emporio Rulli — 464 Magnolia Ave, Larkspur. Gary and Jeannie Rulli have run this old world Italian pasticceria and caffe in downtown Larkspur since 1988. Order an espresso and a real Milanese pastry at the counter and you could be in Turin, not twenty minutes off the Golden Gate Bridge. A proper Larkspur institution. @emporiorulli

Caffe Acri — 1 Main Street, Tiburon. Italian inspired cafe on the corner of Main Street and Tiburon Boulevard, steps from the ferry landing, pouring elevated coffee since 2003. Grab a cappuccino and a pastry, take a seat by the water, and watch the boats come in off the bay. There is live piano on weekend nights if you linger. @caffe_acri

Comforts — 335 San Anselmo Ave, San Anselmo. The Miwa family opened this cafe and marketplace in 1986, and the second generation runs it now. That's why it still feels like the whole town's kitchen. Sit down for weekend brunch or grab a coffee and something from the deli case on your way through. A San Anselmo anchor, generations deep. @comfortscafe

Cafe del Soul — 247 Shoreline Highway, Mill Valley. A counter service organic cafe out at Tam Junction where everything is made to order and, they will tell you, made with love. Order a coffee and a bowl, and you have found the mellow, local Mill Valley the guidebooks skip. Easy, tucked away, no fuss. @cafedelsoul

Six for your Marin coffee run: Equator Coffees, M.H. Bread and Butter, Emporio Rulli, Caffe Acri, Comforts, and Cafe del Soul. From a San Rafael roastery to a Tiburon espresso steps off the ferry, that is the whole county in a cup. Which one are you starting with?
