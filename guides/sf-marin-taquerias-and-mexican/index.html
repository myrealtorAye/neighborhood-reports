San Francisco Marin Taquerias and Mexican

Eight Marin taquerias and Mexican kitchens locals keep in rotation, from a Sausalito waterfront table to a Novato cantina running to 2am on weekends. Most visitors grab a taco and call it. You want the whole map.

Los Moles

912 Lincoln Ave, San Rafael. Chef Lito Saldana makes eight different moles here, poblano to a pineapple one, each built on family recipes and thirty plus ingredients. The weekend buffet is the local move, and the beer list leans on breweries a few blocks away. Come hungry, wear the forgiving pants. @losmoles

Playa

41 Throckmorton Ave, Mill Valley. Contemporary Mexican that pulled a Michelin Bib Gourmand, sitting a few doors from the Sequoia theater. Club Cuatro happy hour runs Tuesday through Friday from 4, and Taco Tuesday drops street tacos to a few bucks a piece. Angle for the back deck if the fog lifts. @playamillvalley

Mas Masa

31 Bolinas Road, Fairfax. They nixtamalize corn and press masa fresh daily, so the tortillas taste like their own food group. Cali Mexican, plenty for vegans, no reservations, first come first served on the little indoor outdoor patio. Downtown Fairfax parking is a rumor, so walk over if you can. @eatmasmasa

Marinitas

218 Sir Francis Drake Blvd, San Anselmo. Guacamole mashed to order, tacos on house made corn tortillas, and a tequila and mezcal list that goes deep. It anchors downtown San Anselmo, half restaurant and half cantina, the back end turning into music and dancing as the night runs on. Post up at the bar for a Casa Margarita. @marinitas_marin

Flores

301 Corte Madera Town Center, Corte Madera. The San Francisco favorite carried its traditional plates north, mole poblano, birria enchiladas, and a tequila and mezcal flight menu that reads like a novel. Tucked into the shopping center and far better than that address suggests. Happy hour starts at 3. @flores_sf

Joe's Taco Lounge and Salsaria

382 Miller Ave, Mill Valley. The scrappy Mill Valley classic, all Mexican comfort food and street cart favorites, the kind of counter regulars keep on speed dial. Snag a seat on the front deck and work through a plate of tacos. Kitchen tends to close around nine, so do not dawdle.

Copita

739 Bridgeway, Sausalito. Upscale, agave forward Mexican right on the Sausalito waterfront, a tequileria where the cocktail list runs as long as the food menu. Weekend brunch goes 11 to 2, prime real estate for watching the ferry crowd spill off Bridgeway. Grab a table and order a mezcal you cannot pronounce. @copitasausalito

Maya Palenque

349 Enfrente Road, Novato. The Novato cantina that refuses to call it a night, kitchen and bar open to midnight most nights and 2am Thursday through Saturday. A DJ takes Saturdays, karaoke lands on Thursdays, happy hour weekdays from 2. When the rest of Marin rolls up the sidewalks, this is where you land.

Eight for the Marin Mexican rotation: Los Moles, Playa, Mas Masa, Marinitas, Flores, Joe's Taco Lounge, Copita, and Maya Palenque. Which Marin spot got left off that you would go to bat for?
