East Bay Fried Chicken

A few lunch hours a day. That is the whole window to get Bakesale Betty's buttermilk fried chicken sandwich, a cult order in Oakland since 2005 from a Chez Panisse alum. Seven East Bay fried chicken spots.

Bakesale Betty — 5098 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The buttermilk fried chicken sandwich with jalapeno slaw, a cult item since 2005, served only a few lunch hours a day. @bakesalebetty

World Famous HotBoys — 1601 San Pablo Ave, Oakland. Nashville hot chicken that began as a 2017 backyard pop-up, rated the top chicken sandwich in California. @worldfamoushotboys

Angeline's Louisiana Kitchen — 2261 Shattuck Ave, Berkeley. New Orleans Creole cooking, the buttermilk fried chicken the signature. @angelineslouisianakitchen

Lois the Pie Queen — 851 60th St, Oakland. A soul-food landmark since 1951, now third generation, fried chicken and homemade pie. @loisthepiequeen_510

Home of Chicken and Waffles — 247 4th St, Oakland. The Jack London staple since 2004, chicken and waffles from a West Oakland native. @the_homeofchickenandwaffles

Noori Chicken — 366 Grand Ave, Oakland. Korean double-fried chicken in yangnyeom sauce, made fresh in Grand Lake. @noori_chicken_oakland

Pyeong Chang Tofu House — 4701 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. A longtime Koreatown tofu house that also fries a mean soy-garlic Korean chicken. @pyeongchangtofu

Seven birds: Bakesale Betty, HotBoys, Angeline's, Lois the Pie Queen, Home of Chicken and Waffles, Noori, and Pyeong Chang. Whose chicken wins your Friday?
