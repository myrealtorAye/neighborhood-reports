Oakland's Dimond District

The Dimond District grew up around an 1870s German beer garden and still hides a redwood creek canyon right behind its MacArthur Boulevard storefronts. Eight Dimond stops.

Dimond Slice Pizza — 2208 MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. A vegetarian slice shop and neighborhood hang. Grab a slice and a salad.

La Farine Fruitvale — 3411 Fruitvale Ave, Oakland. A French bakery institution for morning buns and baguettes. Get a morning bun.

Two Star Market — 2020 MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. A corner market secretly serving some of the city's best late night soul food. Order a soul food plate.

Grand Lake Kitchen Dimond — MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. A cheery all day cafe and deli with a full bar. Do weekend brunch.

Farmer Joe's Marketplace — 3501 MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. A beloved independent grocery with a great deli. Build a picnic.

Dimond Park — 3860 Hanly Rd, Oakland. A shaded park with a creek, a pool, and a stone rec building. Bring the kids.

Dimond Branch Library — 3565 Fruitvale Ave, Oakland. A historic hillside library and community hub. Browse a while.

Dimond Canyon Trail — Dimond Park, Oakland. A redwood creek canyon trail hidden right behind the storefronts. Walk up Sausal Creek.

Eight in the Dimond: Dimond Slice, La Farine, Two Star Market, Grand Lake Kitchen, Farmer Joe's, Dimond Park, the library, and the Dimond Canyon Trail. Where do you start?
