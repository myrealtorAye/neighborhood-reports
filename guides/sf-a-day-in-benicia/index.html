San Francisco A Day in Benicia

Benicia is the Bay Area's overlooked waterfront town, a former state capital where First Street runs straight down to the Carquinez Strait. Eight Benicia stops.

Sailor Jack's — 123 First St, Benicia. A waterfront seafood spot at the bottom of First Street. Get a window table.

Drift Benicia — 366 First St, Benicia. A downtown pizza and coffee stop. Grab a slice and an espresso.

Lucca Bar and Grill — 439 First St, Benicia. A gastropub with a beer garden and live music. Sit in the garden.

Rellik Tavern — 726 First St, Benicia. A cozy tavern in a historic downtown building. Order a cocktail.

One House Bakery — 918 First St, Benicia. A wildly popular bakery on upper First Street. Get there before the pastries sell out.

Benicia Capitol State Historic Park — 115 West G St, Benicia. The only surviving pre Sacramento state capitol. Tour the 1852 chamber.

Benicia Historical Museum — 2060 Camel Rd, Benicia. A museum in 1850s sandstone buildings in the old Arsenal. Walk the Arsenal grounds.

Matthew Turner Shipyard Park — 1001 West 12th St, Benicia. A waterfront park honoring the busiest shipbuilder in North America. Watch the boats.

Eight in Benicia: Sailor Jack's, Drift, Lucca, Rellik Tavern, One House Bakery, the Capitol, the Historical Museum, and Shipyard Park. Where do you start?
