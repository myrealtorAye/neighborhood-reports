Oakland Rockridge, College Avenue

Eight stops on College Avenue, the Rockridge stretch of Oakland most people ride the BART straight past. You can walk the whole run in about fifteen minutes and eat, read, and shop your way from a candlelit French bar to a 1987 market hall without once moving your car. Start here.

A Cote — 5478 College Ave, Oakland. A candlelit Provencal small-plates room that has fed Rockridge since 2000, best known for mussels in Pernod and a wine list built for grazing. Come with three friends, order nine plates, argue over the last one. @acoterestaurant

Pegasus Books — 5560 College Ave, Oakland. A new-and-used bookshop that has held this corner since 1971, with a card and blank-book selection locals will tell you is the best in the East Bay. The kind of place you duck into for one thing and leave forty minutes later with four. @pegasusbooks

Belly — 5634 College Ave, Oakland. An Asian-Mexican kitchen whose breakfast burrito tucks tater tots inside for crunch and chipotle aioli for smoke, and this Rockridge counter is now its only location. Order it wrapped to go and eat it on the walk to BART.

Rockridge Market Hall — 5655 College Ave, Oakland. A European-style food hall open since 1987, nine shops under one roof: a fishmonger, a butcher, a cheese counter, a flower stall, a bakery, and Highwire coffee. You can build a whole dinner run without stepping back outside. @markethallfoods

Zachary's Chicago Pizza — 5801 College Ave, Oakland. The employee-owned deep-dish institution that has pulled weekend lines out the door for over forty years, stuffed pies so deep you eat one with a fork. Put your name in, walk the block, come back when it buzzes. @zacharyspizza

Ramen Shop — 5812 College Ave, Oakland. Three Chez Panisse alums turned this narrow room into one of the Bay Area's best bowls, broth and noodles made in house and produce that shifts with the season. Sit at the bar, watch the pass, order whatever special they push. @ramenshopoakland

Cole Coffee — 6255 College Ave, Oakland. A tiny roaster anchoring the College and 63rd corner since 2005, no laptops encouraged, regulars stacked two deep for the drip. Grab a cup, take the outdoor bench, watch the whole neighborhood walk by. @colecoffeeinc

La Farine — 6323 College Ave, Oakland. A French boulangerie on the upper end of College that locals line up at for the morning bun, a caramel-crackled spiral worth beating the crowd for. Get there early; the good pastries go by mid-morning. @lafarinebakery

Eight stops, one walkable stretch of Oakland's Rockridge: A Cote, Pegasus Books, Belly, Rockridge Market Hall, Zachary's, Ramen Shop, Cole Coffee, La Farine. Coffee to deep dish to a bag of books, all without moving your car. Which corner do you start on?

If you're weighing a move near any of these, that walkability is a real factor, and it's the kind of neighborhood detail I cover street by street. I work across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Joaquin counties.
