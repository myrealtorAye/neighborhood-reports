East Bay Mural Walks

The Richmond Greenway hides three miles of murals, a fresh one about every quarter mile, where the bike route doubles as the art route. The best East Bay art is not behind a gallery door. Eight East Bay mural walks where the walls do the curating.

Downtown Oakland murals

Broadway and Telegraph, Downtown Oakland. Dozens of pieces across the Art Murmur core; the First Friday walk is the way to see them.

Lake Merritt

Around Lake Merritt, Oakland. Murals and public art along the three-mile pedestrian loop, refreshed as new artists are commissioned.

Mandela Parkway

Mandela Pkwy, West Oakland. The West Oakland corridor of community murals, walkable from West Oakland BART.

Fruitvale

International Blvd, Fruitvale District, Oakland. Decades of Latino and Chicano muralism along the International corridor, some of it dating back generations.

Telegraph and People's Park

Telegraph Ave to Dwight Way, Berkeley. A concentrated stretch of Berkeley street art with People's Park as the historic anchor.

Albany Bulb

Foot of Buchanan St, Albany. Ever-changing community art on driftwood and concrete, best on a sunset walk, open dawn to dusk.

Emeryville Greenway

Horton St and the Bay Street area, Emeryville. Rotating public art along the greenway, easy to combine with the marina walk.

Richmond Greenway

Richmond Greenway rail-trail, Richmond. Three miles of murals every quarter mile, where the bike route doubles as the art route.

Eight walks where the walls do the curating: Downtown Oakland, Lake Merritt, Mandela Parkway, Fruitvale, Berkeley's Telegraph, the Albany Bulb, the Emeryville Greenway, and the Richmond Greenway. Which mural stops you every time?
