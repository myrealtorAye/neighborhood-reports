East Bay Tattoo Studios

Serious collectors do not pick a tattoo shop by walking past it, they travel for the artist. Temple Tattoo on Telegraph has been Freddy Corbin's institution for decades. Eight East Bay studios worth the appointment.

Premium Tattoo — 4130 Broadway, Oakland. A custom appointment shop since 2008 with a deep bench of artists and rotating walk-in flash days. @premiumtattoooakland

Temple Tattoo — 4228 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Freddy Corbin's institution; black-and-grey lettering and religious iconography are the house specialty. @templetattoo13

Old Crow Tattoo — 2812 Telegraph Ave, Berkeley. An award-winning shop with rotating guest artists across blackwork, botanical, and ornamental. @oldcrowtattoo

Dark Water — 2035 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley. An appointment-only atelier known for fine-line and blackout work, with lead artists booked months out. @darkwater.ink

Studio Noir — 471B 9th St, Oakland. A woman-owned Old Oakland collective doing custom traditional plus permanent cosmetics and piercings, walk-ins welcome. @studionoiroakland

East Bay Tattoo — 516 E 18th St, Oakland. Joe Parker and Barnaby Williams, heavy on Traditional Japanese and Neotraditional, walk-ins welcome. @eastbaytattoo

Sacred Rose Tattoo — 1960 San Pablo Ave, Berkeley. Woman-owned since 1998, a bench of artists with art degrees, walk-ins 12 to 8 daily. @sacredrosetattoo

Zebra Tattoo and Piercing — Walnut Creek. A Bay Area institution since 1984; the Walnut Creek piercing counter is a regional destination for jewelry. @zebratattoos

Eight worth the appointment: Premium, Temple, Old Crow, Dark Water, Studio Noir, East Bay Tattoo, Sacred Rose, and Zebra. What was your first piece, and where?
