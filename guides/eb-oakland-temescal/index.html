Oakland Temescal

Eight stops that prove Oakland's Temescal is the most walkable food corridor in the East Bay. Most people treat Telegraph Avenue as the road to somewhere else and miss that a brewery, a Burmese institution from 1992, and a whole hidden alley of shops sit in a few blocks. Here's the walk.

Temescal Brewing — 4115 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The taproom and beer garden anchor the south end of the corridor, open every day with a rotating cast of food trucks out front, so dinner changes with your mood. Their motto is Fresh Beer, No Jerks. That tells you the room. Come for the patio on a warm evening.

Burma Superstar — 4721 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The Temescal flagship of a Burmese kitchen that's fed the Bay since 1992, and the tea leaf salad tossed at your table is still the order everyone leans across for. The room reopened polished in 2026. Get the platha and thank yourself later.

Forma Bakery — 4920 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. A tiny corner storefront turning out a serious croissant and a proper cup of coffee, the kind of morning stop that makes a walkable block feel like a gift. Pastries move fast, so the early walk wins the good stuff. @forma_bakery

Pizzaiolo — 5008 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Wood-fired pizza and pasta from an all-organic, all-local kitchen where the menu shifts with the season, so no two visits match. It's a Temescal institution, walk-ins only, dinner nightly. Grab a patio seat and let them decide. @pizzaiolooakland

Bakesale Betty — 5098 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. The buttermilk fried chicken sandwich piled with jalapeno slaw is the East Bay lunch people plan their whole day around, and yes, there's a line. Open Thursday through Sunday only, so mark the calendar before the craving hits.

Curbside Creamery — 482 49th St, Oakland. Duck into Temescal Alley and this is the tiny parlor scooping house-made ice cream and fresh waffle cones, with cashew and coconut vegan options for the table that can't agree. The ice cream sandwich is the move. Open late nightly. @curbsidecreamery

Womb House Books — 470 49th St, Oakland. An inclusive feminist bookshop tucked in the alley, stacked with vintage first editions and a book club worth joining. Browse the plotless-fiction shelf, then the well-worn author hats. The kind of local shop that makes a neighborhood feel established. @wombhousebooks

Mira Flores — 486 49th St, Oakland. An Oakland embroidery label and tailor shop in the alley, designing workwear made in Peru and housing local vintage vendors under one roof. Bring a jacket and they will chain-stitch it on the spot. Closed Monday and Tuesday, so plan the stroll. @shopmiraflores

Eight for a Temescal afternoon: beer garden, Burmese classics, croissants, wood-fired pizza, that fried chicken sandwich, alley ice cream, a feminist bookshelf, and custom stitching, all on foot in a few Oakland blocks. Which stop starts your walk?
