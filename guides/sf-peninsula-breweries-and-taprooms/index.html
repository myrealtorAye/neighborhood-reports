San Francisco Peninsula Breweries and Taprooms

Everyone drives over the hill to Napa or the city for a good pint and skips the whole Peninsula. Their loss. Freewheel in Redwood City still hand pulls traditional English cask ale off a real beer engine, the only brewery making it on the Peninsula. Eight Peninsula breweries and taprooms worth staying home for.

47 Hills Brewing Co

137 S Linden Ave, South San Francisco. A dog friendly brewery and beer garden tucked into South City's industrial flats. That's exactly why the picnic tables never feel like a scene. Grab a flight, let the kids and the dog loose, and pretend you planned it. @47hillsbrewing

Fieldwork Brewing

3030 S Delaware St, San Mateo. The Bay Meadows taproom rotates through a genuinely dizzying number of beers, so the hazy IPA you loved last week may already be gone. Come with a flexible plan and check the board before you commit. @drinkfieldwork

Alpha Acid Brewing

121 Industrial Rd, Belmont. This tiny Belmont operation has brewed more than 500 recipes and often taps something new every week. That makes having a usual almost impossible. Ask what just went on, and trust whoever is pouring. @alphaacidbrewingco

Devil's Canyon Brewing

935 Washington St, San Carlos. Every Friday afternoon this San Carlos mainstay throws open the taproom, barrel room, and beer garden for a family and pet friendly party with food trucks and live music. Show up before five if you want a table. @devilscanyon

Out of the Barrel

555 El Camino Real, San Carlos. Not a brewery but a taproom with more than 30 rotating taps right at the San Carlos train plaza, so the whole table can disagree and everyone still wins. Kid and dog friendly, with empanadas to keep the peace. @outofthebarrelsancarlos

Freewheel Brewing

3736 Florence St, Redwood City. Here is that English cask ale from up top, hand pulled off a beer engine and served at cellar temperature the way a proper pub would. Order the London Calling bitter, low in alcohol, high in flavor, built for a long afternoon. @freewheelbrew_redwoodcity

Hop Dogma Brewing

270 Capistrano Rd, Half Moon Bay. Tucked into the Pillar Point Harbor row at Princeton, this small brewery pours hop forward ales a few steps from the fishing boats. Time it for late afternoon, walk the harbor first, then let the coast fog find you. @hopdogma

Half Moon Bay Brewing Co

390 Capistrano Rd, Half Moon Bay. The waterfront original across from the harbor, all surf shack vibe and salt air, with its own beer beside sustainable local seafood. Post beach or post whale watching, this is where a coast day is meant to end. @hmbbrewingco

Eight for Peninsula beer: 47 Hills, Fieldwork, Alpha Acid, Devil's Canyon, Out of the Barrel, Freewheel, Hop Dogma, and Half Moon Bay Brewing. Which one is your Friday?
