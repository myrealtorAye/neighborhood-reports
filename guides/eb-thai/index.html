East Bay Thai

Jo's Modern Thai pulled a Michelin Bib Gourmand into the Laurel District, and it is one of several East Bay Thai kitchens the guide now sends people across the bridge for. The bridge is optional. Ten East Bay Thai spots that earn the standing reservation.

Jo's Modern Thai — 3725 MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. The Laurel District Bib Gourmand where the crispy rice salad and the massaman beef are why the reservation stays standing. @josmodernthai

Bird and Buffalo — 4659 Telegraph Ave, Oakland. Isaan soul food out of Thailand's northeast, charred grilled meats and green papaya somtum, on the Michelin Guide since 2022. @birdandbuffalothai

Daughter Thai Kitchen — 6118 Medau Pl, Oakland. Southern Thai in Montclair from a Food Network Thai Takedown winner, order the crab curry and the southern style fried chicken. @daughterthaikitchen

Wawa Thai Food — 3009 MacArthur Blvd, Oakland. A women owned Northern Thai kitchen in the Dimond grinding its own curry paste daily, the khao soi is the one to get. @wawathaifood

Farmhouse Kitchen Thai — 336 Water St, Oakland. A big patio and a full bar in Jack London, boat noodles and khao soi that run authentic while the cocktails hold their own. @thaifarmhouseoak

Khao Hom — 22545 Foothill Blvd, Hayward. Small, family run, regional Thai, the pad kra pao with crispy pork and the tom yum the regulars never skip. @khaohomhayward

Andaman Thai — 1560 Newell Ave, Walnut Creek. A neighborhood Thai with the kind of consistency that mints regulars, garlic prawns and panang the standing orders. @andaman_thai_restaurant

Praaw Thai — 1277 Gilman St, Berkeley. Bright and modern off Gilman, the som tum papaya salad and the basil fried rice with prawns are the lunch picks. @praawthai

Royal Siam — 512 Center St, Moraga. Three decades of Moraga regulars, the pad thai draws them in and the larb keeps them coming back.

Saneh Bangkok — 2634 Pleasant Hill Rd, Pleasant Hill. Bangkok street food the bigger rooms skip, the pork belly over sticky rice is the move.

Ten for the reservation: Jo's, Bird and Buffalo, Daughter, Wawa, Farmhouse, Khao Hom, Andaman, Praaw, Royal Siam, and Saneh Bangkok. What is your go to Thai dish?
