East Bay Woodworking Classes

Before you buy a garage full of tools, know this: a West Oakland shop built out of stacked shipping containers will teach you to build. Lower 48 is woman owned and aimed at the makers woodworking usually skips. Seven East Bay places to learn the craft.

Lower 48 Woodshop — 1212 19th St, Oakland. A woman owned nonprofit grown out of stacked shipping containers, heavy on woodturning, pens to bowls, and on the makers the craft overlooks. @lower48woodshop

Strong Wood Studio — 2225 6th St, Berkeley. Sara Strong, a third generation woodworker, runs a two day beginner workshop where you build a solid hardwood cutting board and meet the table saw, router, and sander.

Laney College Wood Technology — 900 Fallon St, Oakland. One of the best equipped teaching shops in Northern California, avocational furniture and cabinetmaking classes that fit around a job, hand tools to CAD. @laneywoodtech

Ace Makerspace — 6050 Lowell St, Oakland. A member run wood and metal shop where a one hour shop basics class unlocks the tools to anyone. @ace_makerspace

Hands On Woodshop — West Oakland. Shop class for adults, a membership community woodshop with classes on the kind of tools most people never get to touch. @handson_wood

Mt — Diablo Adult Education. One Santa Barbara Rd, Pleasant Hill. A public adult ed woodshop running tuition woodworking and woodturning for every level.

Diablo Woodworkers — One Santa Barbara Rd, Pleasant Hill. A volunteer club since 1997 on the same campus, beginning and advanced classes open to any adult.

Seven to learn the craft: Lower 48, Strong Wood, Laney, Ace Makerspace, Hands On, Mt. Diablo Adult Ed, and Diablo Woodworkers. What do you build first?
