San Francisco Mezcal and Agave Bars

San Francisco keeps its best tequila list in a low key Yucatecan spot on Geary. Tommy's has poured only 100% agave since 1965, over 300 bottles deep, and gave the world the Tommy's Margarita everyone copies. Eight SF bars where mezcal and tequila are the whole point, not a dusty top shelf.

Tommy's Mexican Restaurant — 5929 Geary Blvd, San Francisco. A family Yucatecan room in the Richmond hiding over 300 tequilas, all 100% agave, since 1965. This is where the Tommy's Margarita was born and where Julio Bermejo turned SF into a tequila town. Sip an anejo neat and skip the sugary stuff. @tommysmexican

Mezcalito — 2323 Polk St, San Francisco. Russian Hill's neighborhood mezcaleria since 2016, dim and easy, with a long agave list and regional Mexican plates to slow you down. Get a smoky mezcal flight before dinner and grab a sidewalk table when the fog lifts. @mezcalitobarsf

Mosto — 741 Valencia St, San Francisco. The little agave bar next to Tacolicious, all tequila, mezcal and tacos al pastor off the trompo, now with a disco ball overhead. It quietly keeps one of the deeper mezcal lists on Valencia. Ask the bartender to take you off menu. @mosto_sf

Loló — 974 Valencia St, San Francisco. Jaliscan Californian cooking with an actual agave program, so a server can walk you through what separates a good tequila from a good mezcal. The room is a color explosion and the shrimp tacos earn the hype. Order a flight and take notes. @lolorestaurantsf

El Techo de Lolinda — 2518 Mission St, San Francisco. The Mission rooftop with 360 degree city views, heaters and a retractable roof, so it works nearly every day of the year. Agave cocktails carry the menu, from smoky palomas to pitchers for a crowd. Go at golden hour and stay for the lights. @eltechosf

Cavaña — 100 Channel St, San Francisco. A 17th floor Mission Bay rooftop leaning hard into agave, with mezcal and tequila cocktails and seasonal pours like watermelon and hibiscus. Views run over the ballpark and the bay. Walk ins welcome or book on Resy, and come for sunset. @cavana.sf

Santeria — 2251 Market St, San Francisco. A Castro cantina that calls itself a margarita bar but keeps mezcal on the rocks and bold agave cocktails like the Santera with jalapeno cordial and lava salt. Easy, loud and fun. Start here before a night out on the block. @santeria_sf

Flores — 2030 Union St, San Francisco. The Cow Hollow spot with a Michelin Bib Gourmand and a serious tequila and mezcal list behind fresh masa tortillas and molcajete guac. Margaritas come balanced, not sweet, and the sipping pours reward a slow night. Post up at the bar. @flores_sf

Eight for San Francisco agave: Tommy's, Mezcalito, Mosto, Loló, El Techo, Cavaña, Santeria, and Flores. Which one gets your first flight?
