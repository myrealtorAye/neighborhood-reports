East Bay A Day in Old Town Pittsburg

Pittsburg's Old Town sits right on the delta, an Italian fishing town turned Railroad Avenue strip of red sauce joints, a 1920 theater, and a marina where the water taxi still runs. Eight Old Town Pittsburg stops.

La Veranda Ristorante — Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. A downtown Italian room carrying the town's fishing village roots. Get the linguine and clams.

Wahsing — 2222 Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. A longtime Chinese American spot on the strip. Order the chow mein.

Johnny's Market — 625 Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. An old school deli and market for a big sandwich. Grab a sub.

Blue Saigon — Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. A neighborhood Vietnamese spot for pho and rice plates. Get a bowl of pho.

Touch of Soul — Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. A soul food kitchen for ribs and cornbread. Order the ribs.

Waterfront Grill and Cafe — 51 Marina Blvd, Pittsburg. A casual spot right on the marina water. Sit out by the boats.

California Theatre — 351 Railroad Ave, Pittsburg. A restored 1920 downtown theater and stage. Catch a show.

Pittsburg Marina — Marina Blvd, Pittsburg. A delta marina with a boardwalk and a summer water taxi. Walk the waterfront.

Eight in Old Town Pittsburg: La Veranda, Wahsing, Johnny's Market, Blue Saigon, Touch of Soul, the Waterfront Grill, the California Theatre, and the marina. Where do you start?
