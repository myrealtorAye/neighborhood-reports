San Francisco Dumplings and Xiao Long Bao

Eight San Francisco spots where the xiao long bao arrive heavy with soup, and the best of them aren't the Chinatown windows tourists queue at. A 1958 pushcart hall, a Sunset counter with 20 kinds, a Richmond room doing rainbow soup dumplings, and the crawl runs Hayes Valley to Bernal.

Dumpling Home

298 Gough Street, San Francisco. The Hayes Valley sit down where the pork xiao long bao come six ways, from plain to crab and pork to a numb and spicy batch that earns every napkin. Add the pan fried juicy pork bao, and time it before the symphony crowd claims the tables.

Dumpling Time

11 Division Street, San Francisco. Watch the crew pleat soup dumplings behind glass in the Design District, from the team behind the city's Michelin starred Omakase and Niku. Xiao long bao, sharable plates, and a full bar, so put your name in before the showroom crowd beats you to it at lunch. @itsdumplingtime

Kingdom of Dumpling

1713 Taraval Street, San Francisco. A no frills Sunset room turning out 20 kinds of dumpling, including Shanghai soup dumplings and a pan fried batch worth the grease spotted bag. Grab a sack of the frozen ones on the way out, since your freezer deserves a backup plan.

Dragon Beaux

5700 Geary Boulevard, San Francisco. The Outer Richmond dim sum room from the Koi Palace family, known for rainbow skinned soup dumplings that taste as good as they photograph. Come hungry at lunch for the carts, or after dark when the tables flip to hot pot. @dragonbeaux

United Dumplings

525 Cortland Avenue, San Francisco. The mom owned original on Bernal's Cortland strip, where traditional xiao long bao share the menu with Korean BBQ beef dumplings and Mission chicken potstickers. Corner spot, family recipes, and a stroller parked at half the sidewalk tables. @uniteddumplings

Dumpling Zone

408 Dewey Boulevard, San Francisco. Tucked on a quiet Forest Hill block most people only pass on the way to West Portal, this counter hand folds crab roe xiao long bao, three fresh potstickers, and wontons in chili red oil. Park once, order the trio, and thank the TikTok that outed it.

Dumpling Kitchen

3913 24th Street, San Francisco. The Noe Valley branch of a homegrown dumpling group, steaming soup dumplings and pan fried pork buns on a 24th Street stretch better known for strollers and slow brunch lines. Slide in for an early lunch and let the bamboo baskets stack up. @dumplingkitchenca

Yank Sing

101 Spear Street, San Francisco. The 1958 Rincon Center institution where the Shanghai Kurobuta pork soup dumplings ride the pushcarts past your table, one of 100 plus rotating dishes. Third generation and family run, it is the special occasion splurge locals book when the whole table shows up hungry. @yanksing

Eight for your San Francisco soup dumpling map: Dumpling Home, Dumpling Time, Kingdom of Dumpling, Dragon Beaux, United Dumplings, Dumpling Zone, Dumpling Kitchen, Yank Sing. Which one gets your first order of xiao long bao?
