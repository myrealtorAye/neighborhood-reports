San Francisco Marin Indian and Himalayan

Five Marin Indian kitchens worth crossing a bridge for, and most Bay Area folks can only name the one on San Rafael's Fourth Street. The good stuff hides in Fairfax, up in Novato, even down a back road in Lagunitas. Think organic curries, Goan shrimp, momos steamed to order. Here's the Marin run.

Lotus Cuisine of India

812 Fourth Street, San Rafael. The Sroa family has been doing organic Indian downtown for years, naan baked in a clay pit over charcoal and samosas folded from organic dough in house. Order the vegetable momos too, a quiet nod to Nepal on an Indian menu. Then ask for it as spicy as you mean it.

Cafe Lotus

1912 Sir Francis Drake Blvd, Fairfax. Same family as the San Rafael spot, gone casual and all organic in the middle of downtown Fairfax. Northern Indian home cooking, many of the spices carried in from India, and a patio for when Fairfax does its sunny thing. The kind of main street anchor a town gets lucky to keep.

Prabh Indian Kitchen

24 Sunnyside Ave, Mill Valley. Tucked off the main drag with a patio and an easy, warm room. The butter chicken and tandoori do the heavy lifting, but regulars quietly order the achari soya chap and won't tell you why. Open late for Mill Valley, kitchen going till 10 most nights. @prabhindiankitchen

Little Goan Cafe

2007 Novato Blvd, Novato. Not only Indian, Goan, the coastal state where the curries lean on clove, cinnamon and the sea. Get the shrimp xacuti and you taste the difference a coastline makes. Family run, closed Mondays, and yes there's cold Indian beer for the heat. Worth the drive up to Novato on its own.

Arti Cafe

7282 Sir Francis Drake Blvd, Lagunitas. Yes, real tandoori this far out the San Geronimo Valley, past where the cell signal gives up. This is the West Marin village Indian spot, curries and plenty of gluten free options, even an Indian pizza the food writers keep chasing. Make it a Saturday and stay for the whole valley.

Five for a Marin curry crawl: Lotus Cuisine in San Rafael, Cafe Lotus in Fairfax, Prabh in Mill Valley, Little Goan in Novato, Arti out in Lagunitas. Organic curries, Goan seafood, momos for the Himalayan side. Which is your regular order, and who's missing from this Marin map?
