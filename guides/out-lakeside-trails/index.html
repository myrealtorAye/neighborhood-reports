Bay Area Lakeside Trails

Lake Chabot hides an 8.9-mile shoreline loop that most East Bay walkers never take past the first cove. When the hills go gold and hot in summer, the water trails stay cool. Six East Bay lakeside trails.

Lake Chabot Loop — Lake Chabot Regional Park, Castro Valley. 8.9 miles, 770 feet of gain, 4.8 stars. The full shoreline loop, part paved, with boat rentals at the marina. @ebrpd

Lafayette Reservoir Rim Trail — Lafayette Reservoir Recreation Area, Lafayette. 5 miles, 938 feet of gain, 4.7 stars. A rim loop above the water, with a flat paved lakeside path below.

Lake Anza loop — Tilden Regional Park, Berkeley. About 1.4 miles around the swim lake, sandy beach and summer lifeguards. The one you can actually swim after.

Rocky Ridge and Kings Canyon Loop — EBMUD Lands, Moraga, above Upper San Leandro Reservoir. 6.7 miles, 928 feet of gain, 4.6 stars. Reservoir views the whole ridge, an EBMUD trail permit required.

Jordan Pond via High Ridge — Dry Creek Pioneer Regional Park, Union City. 3.3 miles, 413 feet of gain, 4.7 stars. A pond-and-ridge loop in the south-county hills.

Wildcat Creek Trail to Jewel Lake — Tilden Nature Area, Berkeley. A 1.7-mile flat boardwalk to a little lake, heavy on birds, easy for strollers.

Six for the water: Lake Chabot, Lafayette Reservoir, Lake Anza, Rocky Ridge, Jordan Pond, and Jewel Lake. Which lake gets your summer?
