San Francisco Marin Italian Restaurants

Six Marin Italian kitchens worth crossing the bridge for, where the pasta is still rolled by hand and most of the room is regulars, not tourists. Everyone lines up in North Beach and drives right past the North Bay. From a Sausalito harbor patio to Fairfax wine poured straight from the barrel, here is the lineup locals actually book.

Poggio Trattoria — 777 Bridgeway, Sausalito. The harbor view patio gets the postcard, but the housemade pasta and wood fired everything are why it has held down this Bridgeway corner since 2003. Northern Italian, and one of the few spots doing a real sit down breakfast on the water. Come hungry, stay a while. @poggiotrattoria

Piazza D'Angelo — 22 Miller Avenue, Mill Valley. It anchors the downtown plaza, so grab a bar seat and watch the regulars roll in by name. Southern Italian, wood fired pizzas and fresh pasta, but the tiramisu is the one people quietly drive over for. Walkable to the whole village after. @piazzadangelo

Il Davide — 901 A Street, San Rafael. Downtown San Rafael's established Italian since 1995, the kind of room families book for weddings and weeknights alike. Order the truffle ravioli and grab the patio on a cool Marin night. Closed Mondays, so plan around it. @ildavidesanrafaelca

Piatti — 625 Redwood Highway, Mill Valley. The pasta is rolled and cut by hand every morning, and the patio looks straight at Mt. Tamalpais. A neighborhood trattoria since 1987 that just took Best Happy Hour in Marin, so come at 3 for the bar menu and the view. @piattimillvalley

Servino — 114 Main Street, Tiburon. Back on Tiburon's historic Ark Row where the family started in 1977, now with an Enoteca wine bar next door in the old Yellow Ark and a garden patio out back. Southern Italian plates and an all Italian wine list worth getting lost in. Walk it off along Main after. @servino_tiburon

Fradelizio's — 35 Broadway Blvd, Fairfax. Funky, friendly, and unmistakably Fairfax, with homemade organic pasta and gnocchi and six wines poured straight from the barrel, not the bottle. A downtown fixture for 20 plus years. Closed Tuesday and Wednesday, so aim for a weekend table. @fradeliziosinfairfax

Six for the Marin Italian shortlist: Poggio on the Sausalito water, Piazza D'Angelo and Piatti in Mill Valley, Il Davide in San Rafael, Servino in Tiburon, Fradelizio's in Fairfax. Handmade pasta, harbor patios, wine from the barrel. Which North Bay table are you booking first?
