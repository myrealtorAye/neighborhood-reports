San Francisco Peninsula Korean Restaurants and BBQ

Five Peninsula Korean spots worth crossing a bridge for, from a brand new Daly City megamart to a downtown San Mateo kalbijjim counter that runs a line out the door. Skip the chain court at the mall this time.

Jagalchi

63 Serramonte Center, Daly City. The Korean megamart that turned a dead mall anchor into a weekend destination, live seafood tanks, a real butcher, and Basquia's gluten free rice bread up front. Come hungry and you leave with banchan and a full trunk. Weekend lots fill fast, so go before noon. @jagalchi_official

Pogu

63 Serramonte Center, Daly City. Inside the same complex, a Michelin starred chef runs a 160 seat room built around hansang charim, the full table spread of premium meats and seafood at a low Korean table. This is your anniversary reservation, not your Tuesday. Book ahead and bring the makgeolli crowd. @jagalchi_official

Mom's Tofu House

133 El Camino Real, Millbrae. The soondubu specialist where the soft tofu stew lands still bubbling like lava and the banchan keeps refilling. Get the combination tofu soup with a side of yang nyum wings, and crack the egg in while it is still roaring. No frills, cash friendly, exactly the point.

Whisper Fusion

310 Baldwin Ave, San Mateo. Chef Nick Yoon's brunch room where Korean flavor goes fine dining, gochujang fried chicken, truffle wagyu yukke, a seafood jjambbong pasta that has no business working this well. It opened early 2026 and downtown San Mateo has not stopped talking. Budget a wait on weekends. @whisper.sanmateo

Daeho Kalbijjim and Beef Soup

213 2nd Ave, San Mateo. The kalbijjim locals cross town for, braised short ribs so tender they slide off the bone, with the cheese pull option that made it a Goldbelly star. The seolleongtang is the quiet cure for a cold morning. Order it with cheese once and skip the debate. @daeho_official

Five for your Peninsula Korean rotation: Jagalchi to shop, Pogu to celebrate, Mom's for the soondubu, Whisper for brunch, Daeho for the short ribs. Which counter earns your first drive, and who did I miss between Burlingame and Redwood City?
