Bay Area Mount Diablo State Park Hikes

From the summit of Mount Diablo you can see more of the earth's surface than from almost any peak on the planet, some 40 counties on a clear day, from the Sierra to the sea. Eight Mount Diablo hikes for the whole mountain.

Waterfalls of Mount Diablo Loop — Mount Diablo SP. 8.1 miles, hard, loop, 4.7 stars. A winter and spring loop past a chain of seasonal falls most people never know exist. Go after a rain.

Donner Canyon and Falls — Mount Diablo SP. 5.5 miles, moderate, 4.7 stars. A shorter route to the same falls through a wildflower canyon, best February to April. The spring classic.

Grand Loop — Mount Diablo SP. 6.4 miles, hard, loop, 4.7 stars. A high loop near the summit past wind carved outcrops and huge views. The greatest hits ring.

Summit Trail — Mount Diablo SP. 7.4 miles, hard, out and back, 4.7 stars. The full climb to the 3,849 foot summit and its stone visitor center. Bag the peak on foot.

Juniper and Summit Loop — Mount Diablo SP. 3.9 miles, moderate, loop, 4.7 stars. A shorter high country loop with big views without the full summit slog. The half day option.

Rock City to the Summit — Mount Diablo SP. 8 miles, hard, 4.7 stars. Start among wind carved sandstone caves kids love to scramble, then climb to the top. Play in the rocks first.

Mitchell Canyon to Eagle Peak — Mount Diablo SP. 8.2 miles, hard, loop, 4.7 stars. A quieter north side climb up a ridge to a sharp peak, wildflowers in spring. The locals' side.

Rock City — Mount Diablo SP. A cluster of sandstone formations and wind caves near the south gate, short scrambles and a gymnasium of rock for families. The easy stop.

Eight on the mountain: the Waterfall Loop, Donner Canyon, the Grand Loop, the Summit Trail, the Juniper Loop, Rock City to the top, Mitchell Canyon, and Rock City. Which trail first?
