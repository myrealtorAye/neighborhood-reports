San Francisco Tattoo Studios

Eight San Francisco tattoo shops worth planning a trip around, and the oldest has been inking the city since 1995. Say tattoo in SF and people picture the Haight. Plenty of the best needles are down Valencia in the Mission too. Both strips made this list.

Black and Blue Tattoo — 3290 22nd St, San Francisco. Queer and woman owned since 1995, which in tattoo years makes it an institution. Walk in Tuesday through Sunday, noon to 6, and the crew covers everything from fine script to bold color. Ask for the artist whose flash matches your idea. @blackandbluetattoo

UNDRGRND SF — 3191 Mission St, San Francisco. Woman and queer owned, and the shop a local panel of pros names for fine line and the spiky cybersigilism style all over your feed. Mostly by appointment, though they answer fast about walk in openings. Bring screenshots, they speak that language. @undrgrnd.sf

Black Heart Tattoo — 177 Valencia St, San Francisco. On the Valencia strip since 2004, the one to beat for bold American traditional, blackwork, and black and grey. Open noon to 8 daily, walk ins always welcome. There is even a vintage photo booth inside, so document the before and after. @blackhearttattoo

Idle Hand Tattoo — 575 Haight St, San Francisco. A Lower Haight anchor since 2004, known for clean bold traditional that still reads sharp decades later. Open daily until 8, walk ins welcome, and the flash on the walls is worth a look even if you are only browsing. @idlehandsf

Cold Steel America — 1783 Haight St, San Francisco. An Upper Haight fixture since 1999, equal parts tattoo studio and serious piercing counter that regulars trust for both. Open noon to 8 every day. Go here when you want the piercing done right the first time, not redone later. @coldsteelpiercing

Mom's Body Shop — 1408 Haight St, San Francisco. Open since Mother's Day 1998 and still one of the busiest counters on Haight, tattoos and piercings both, with a deep case of titanium and gold jewelry. The kicker is the hours, 12 to midnight daily, so it catches the late night ideas. Walk ins all day. @moms_body_shop

Seventh Son Tattoo — 765 Clementina St, San Francisco. Tucked on a SoMa side street, this is the name other SF tattooers give you for Japanese work and big back pieces. Custom and appointment based, though walk ins get squeezed in. Come with a clear idea and let the artist run. @seventhsontattoo

Let It Bleed Tattoo — 1124 Polk St, San Francisco. A Polk Street mainstay since 2009 with a tight crew that handles traditional, Japanese, black and grey, and lettering without blinking. Open daily, 1 to 8, walk ins and appointments both. Easy energy for a first tattoo. @letitbleedsf

Eight for getting inked in San Francisco: Black and Blue, UNDRGRND, Black Heart, Idle Hand, Cold Steel America, Mom's Body Shop, Seventh Son, and Let It Bleed. Which chair are you booking first?
