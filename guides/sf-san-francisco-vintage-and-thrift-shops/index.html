San Francisco Vintage and Thrift Shops

Wasteland on Haight sells vintage out of a 1911 theater, an ornate Art Nouveau facade now painted fire engine red you can spot a block away. San Francisco keeps its best secondhand on a handful of streets, not scattered across town. Eight vintage and thrift stops worth a Saturday.

Wasteland

1660 Haight St, San Francisco. Behind that red facade is a huge floor of vintage denim, leather, and designer resale, and they buy off the street if you bring a bag to sell. Go early for the good size runs. @shopwasteland

Buffalo Exchange

1555 Haight St, San Francisco. The dependable buy sell trade up the same block, well sorted and cheaper than the boutiques, paying cash or store credit on the spot. Bring a bag to sell and walk out with something new. @buffaloexchange

Relic Vintage

1475 Haight St, San Francisco. This one leans older and dressier, real 1940s to 1960s pieces, hats, and costume jewelry, not 90s castoffs. The place to find something for a themed party that actually looks the part. @relicvintage

Crossroads Trading

1901 Fillmore St, San Francisco. Over on Fillmore, this leans current and on trend resale, so it is the spot for near new brands at a fraction of retail. Check the clearance rack in the back first. @crossroadstrading

Relove

1815 Polk St, San Francisco. A Black owned resale boutique on Polk with a tight, well priced mix of vintage and designer, more boutique than warehouse dig. Easy in and out when you want quality over quantity. @shoprelove

No Shop

389 Valencia St, San Francisco. Kicks off the Valencia vintage stretch with cheap band tees, jewelry, and eclectic racks that reward a slow flip. Come for a $25 tee, stay for the odd jacket you did not plan on. @no_shop

Community Thrift Store

623 Valencia St, San Francisco. A big nonprofit thrift where donations get credited to a local charity the giver names, so your bargain hunt quietly funds the neighborhood. Hit the books and housewares, not just the clothes. @communitythriftsf

Valencia Street Vintage

714 Valencia St, San Francisco. A small, well edited shop with a killer $15 rack and rotating local art on the walls. A good last stop on a Valencia crawl when your arms are already full. @valenciastreetvintage

Eight for a vintage crawl: Wasteland, Buffalo Exchange, Relic Vintage, Crossroads Trading, Relove, No Shop, Community Thrift, and Valencia Street Vintage. Haight, Fillmore, or Valencia, which block do you start on?
