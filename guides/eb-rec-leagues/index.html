East Bay Rec Leagues

The adult who joins a rec league at 32 makes more new friends in eight weeks than in the previous two years. The East Bay has leagues with open sign-ups and a beginner division that actually means it. Seven East Bay rec leagues taking new players this season.

Bay Area Adult Soccer League — Tom Bates Sports Complex, Berkeley. Eleven-a-side coed and eight-a-side futsal, community-first, all skill levels welcome.

East Bay Over Thirty Soccer — Estuary Park, 200 Mosley Ave, Alameda. A coed rec league since 2003 for men 30-plus and women 21-plus, Saturday-morning games, fun over competition. @ebots_soccer

Neighborhood Sports Club — 100 Second St, Oakland. A Jack London soccer cafe and small-sided pitch with a Community league and drop-in matches, opened in 2026.

Bay Area Disc Association — Gilman Fields, Berkeley. A nonprofit running adult ultimate frisbee leagues, tournaments, and pickup all over the East Bay. @bayareadisc

Walnut Creek Adult Softball — Heather Farm Park, 301 N San Carlos Dr, Walnut Creek. Running since 1976, with over-40, men's, and coed competitive plus rec divisions.

Oakland Parks and Recreation — Citywide, Oakland. The city department running adult basketball and softball at neighborhood centers, the cheapest entry into organized play. @oakparksrec

Berkeley YMCA — 2001 Allston Way, Berkeley. Pickup basketball, pickleball open play, and member-organized league signups downtown near BART.

Seven leagues taking players: Bay Area Adult Soccer, East Bay Over Thirty, Neighborhood Sports Club, Bay Area Disc, Walnut Creek Softball, Oakland Parks and Rec, and the Berkeley YMCA. What are you signing up for?
